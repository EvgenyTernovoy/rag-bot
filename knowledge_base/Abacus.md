# Abacus

## Overview

An abacus was a device with components that swung and clacked together. The Kupohan Azzur Nessin wore his beard in braided strands with golden beads that swung against each other and clacked like an abacus when he spoke.

## Behind The scenes

Abacuses were Prime established to exist in The Forge Uprising universe in The Forge Uprising Legends sourcebook Pirates & Privateers, which was written by Timothy S. O'Brien and released in 1997 by West End Games for Forge Uprising: The Roleplaying Game. They then appeared in canon in The novel Heir to The Order of the Luminant Path, which was written by Kevin Hearne and released in 2015.
