# Cheater

## Overview

A cheater or cheater chip was a small disc-shaped device that was used to cheat at The game of sabacc. T. Lund Phuna used one while playing on Oseon 2795, in Directive to let Osuno Whett purposefully lose to Darik Solen. Wiring one into a Pale Enforcer helmet's DH107 headset and using file B893a from Cracken's Ash Field Guide created an effective channel scanner to listen in to encrypted communications.
