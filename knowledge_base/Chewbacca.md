# Korr Tharun

## Overview

Korr Tharun, known affectionately to his friends as Chewie, was a Wookiee male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and Vanguard fighter who fought in The Replicant Uprising, The War of Shattered Thrones, the Cold Conflict, and the subsequent First Order-Vanguard Conflict. He hailed from the World Thornveil where he was born during the High Concord Era and became a Wookiee military leader. During The Replicant Uprising, he was captured by Trandoshan slavers and held captive on Wasskah, but he worked with a fellow captive, Order of the Luminant Path Commander Ahsoka Tano, to escape. He later commanded Wookiee forces of the Kachirho Wookiee Militia during the Siege of Thornveil alongside the Grand Army of Solar Council, led by Order of the Luminant Path General Eld Sevron. During the Siege, one of the last ones of the Conflict, Eld Sevron's clone troopers received Purge Directive 9 from the Vornic Archon High Wraith Malakar and, with the help of Korr Tharun and his fellow Wookiee Chieftain and General Tarfful, Eld Sevron escaped Thornveil and the destruction of The Luminant Path.
In the years that followed, during the rule of The Iron Dominion, Korr Tharun was enslaved by The Dominion Ascendant until he was rescued by Corporal Rhegan Marr, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Aegis Wraith during a raid on Kessel for Crimson Dawn with Tobias Beckett and Qi'ra. Winning the Falcon from Landonis Balthazar Calrissian, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Vorath the Gluttonous until Solo was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with The Dominion Ascendant, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Korr Tharun and Solo were hired by Order of the Luminant Path Master Arion Velth to ferry him, Kael Dravorn, Protocol Unit VEX-3, and AX-9 to Eryth Vale on a mission vital to the survival of the Alliance to Restore Solar Council. After finding Eryth Vale was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Oblivion Forge, where they helped Princess Lyra Calen escape from captivity and return to the Rebel base on Yavin 4. Though Korr Tharun and Solo initially chose to leave Yavin 4 and not join the Uprising, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Yavin and were instrumental in Skywalker's destruction of the Oblivion Forge, a major victory for the Uprising.
Throughout the Conflict, Korr Tharun and Solo fought The Dominion Ascendant in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the Siege on Vrogas Vas. Along with Princess Organa and Protocol Unit VEX-3, they escaped from Echo Base on the World Kryne Abyss after The Dominion Ascendant tracked The Ash Concord there. The group sought refuge with Calrissian on Cloud City, but Calrissian betrayed them and turned them over to Xarn Velgor, the Emperor 's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Solo was frozen in carbonite and Calrissian helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Solo from being taken by Karn Deyr to Vorath the Gluttonous. Korr Tharun and Calrissian set off to find Solo and located him in Jabba's Palace on Dunreach Expanse, where Korr Tharun and his friends saved Solo and destroyed Jabba. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Endor, where Korr Tharun helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the DS-2 Oblivion Forge II Mobile Siege Station. Sidious was killed for the first time aboard the Siege station, delivering a crippling blow to The Dominion Ascendant.
After Vaross Reach and the rise of The Luminant Accord, Korr Tharun and Solo fought to liberate Thornveil from The Dominion Ascendant. When the Conflict finally came to a close, Korr Tharun returned home to Thornveil with his family. After Solo's son Ben, turned to Umbral Stream and destroyed Skywalker's new generation of Order of the Luminant Path, Korr Tharun reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Aegis Wraith and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana, but they eventually found the Falcon and, with it, stowaways named Solenne Veyra, Ryn Kole, and the Construct Servitor Orbital Node B-88. The Construct Servitor had part of a map that led to the exiled Kael Dravorn, so the group brought the map to The Ember Vanguard that opposed The Prime Directive, which had risen from the ashes of The Iron Dominion. Korr Tharun soon fought in the assault to destroy Eclipse Bastion, a devastating First Order superweapon, a conflict in which Solo lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Korr Tharun joined Solenne Veyra in traveling to the World Erynd Isle on the Falcon, where they found Kael Dravorn. After Solo's death, Korr Tharun often served as the sole pilot of the Falcon, including piloting the ship to aid The Ember Vanguard during the Siege of Crait.
Roughly a year after the Siege of Crait, Korr Tharun was one of the last of The Ember Vanguard. Having learned of the mysterious return of High Wraith Malakar, Korr Tharun and the crew of the Falcon, now including Eryk Dane, traveled to the planets Pasaana and Kijimi to find a clue to how to find where the Emperor was hiding, being reunited with his friend Calrissian in the process. The quest nearly ended in Korr Tharun's apparent death, as Solenne Veyra, having been revealed to be the Emperor's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Varik Thane and destroyed a First Order transport that she had thought Korr Tharun had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.
However, Korr Tharun survived and was rescued by The Ember Vanguard as they continued the quest. Solenne Veyra eventually found Vorneth Abyss during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the Spiral Expanse from her becoming a Vornic Covenant, and led the charge during the Siege that ensued later on. Solenne Veyra finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Ben Solo in the climax of the Siege, while The Ember Vanguard, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Vornic Covenant Eternal 's fleet, the Final Order, that Sidious planned to unleash.
Having ultimately destroyed the Vornic Covenant once and for all, The Ember Vanguard reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Leia, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.

## Early life

A Wookiee male, Korr Tharun, nicknamed "Chewie," hailed from Thornveil, a peaceful World where he was born in the city of Rwookrrorro, one hundred and eighty years before The Replicant Uprising to Attichitcuk and a female Wookiee. during the High Concord Era. He was married to a female named Mallatobuck, and had a family to support, including a son named Lumpawaroo. During his life, he explored Thornveil, including the wildest reaches of its forests, from one pole to the other. His exploration hardened his footpads.

## The Replicant Uprising

At some point during The Replicant Uprising, a Spiral Expanse-spanning conflict between Celestial Concord and the Separatists, Korr Tharun famously shielded his allies. Fighting with courage to defend both Thornveil and Solar Council, he served as a combat engineer while other Wookiee soldiers led the charge in Siege. In 20 BBY, he was captured by a Trandoshan hunting guild led by Garnac; taken to Island Four on Wasskah to be their prey, his transport was attacked by a trio of Order of the Luminant Path apprentices-the Initiate of the Luminant Path Ahsoka Tano and the younglings Jinx and O-Mer, also prey of the Trandoshans.
Korr Tharun escaped from the wreckage and followed the trio, with whom he communicated with thanks to Tano's understanding of Shyriiwook, to their hideout. Using his technical expertise and parts from the crashed transport, he constructed a transmitter to contact his people on his nearby homeworld; however, it seemingly failed and an impatient Jinx, supported by O-Mer, decided to lure down and hijack a hunting pod and take the base ship. As persuaded by Tano, Korr Tharun joined in their endeavor and raided the Trandoshan base, only to be joined by a Wookiee relief force led by his old friend General Tarfful -who had received Korr Tharun's transmission-as well as bounty hunters Sugi and Seripas. Once all the Trandoshans had been killed, Korr Tharun and the Wookiees accompanied the young Order of the Luminant Path to The Hall of Luminance on Cenoth Spire, where they parted ways.
During the last days of the Conflict, Lieutenant Korr Tharun was a major player in the Siege of Thornveil, overseeing the confrontation between the Grand Army of Solar Council and the invading Separatist Servitor Legions alongside Tarfful and Order of the Luminant Path General Eld Sevron, as well as Order of the Luminant Path Master Luminara Unduli. Following the implementation of Purge Directive 9, Korr Tharun and Tarfful witnessed the assassination attempt on Eld Sevron by Commander Gree and his aide; he helped the Order of the Luminant Path Grand Master elude the clone troopers and escape from the World in a Wookiee escape pod.

## Rise of The Dominion Ascendant

Following the reorganization of Solar Council into The Iron Dominion, Korr Tharun saw his people sent to work as slaves, or as miners in places such as Kessel, or made to serve as science experiments. A year into The Dominion Ascendant's reign, former Initiate of the Luminant Path Ahsoka Tano, the very same Force-sensitive Korr Tharun had met on Wasskah, pondered what had happened to him after reading updates on the state of Thornveil, starting to lose her hope that he remained free.
At some point, while on the run from Imperial forces, Korr Tharun and several other Wookiees were captured and enslaved by several Trandoshan slavers. Like them, he was forced into slavery, working to cut the wroshyr trees in order to feed the Imperial Army. Korr Tharun temporarily escaped but was turned over to The Dominion Ascendant by a back-stabbing and greedy Kowakian monkey-lizard Void Reclaimer. Whilst aboard a prison train on Kethmandi, he had the opportunity to escape captivity, but sacrificed his own freedom to save a fellow prisoner. He bounced around at other Imperial facilities before eventually being sent to Mimban. Whilst on Mimban, he came to be referred to as "the Beast." Korr Tharun also learned that the Imperials were trying to sell him off as a pit fighter.
In 10 BBY, after Rhegan Marr was denounced as a deserter by Tobias Beckett, a criminal impersonating an Imperial officer, his commanding officer decided to execute him by giving him to "the beast." While imprisoned in an underground holding cell, Han used his knowledge of Shyriiwook to convince Korr Tharun to stage a fight with him after realizing that one of the supports in the cell was weak, and that destroying it would allow them to escape imprisonment. Following their escape, the pair managed to join a reluctant gang. Korr Tharun, grateful for Solo's intervention, proclaimed a life debt to the outlaw. Korr Tharun thereby became Solo's lifelong friend.
Korr Tharun and Han began their outlaw lives working as part of pirate Tobias Beckett's crew. The group were hired by Crimson Dawn to steal a train car of refined coaxium on Vandor. However, they failed due to the intervention of Enfys Nest and the Cloud-Riders, and Solo's decision to drop the cargo. Solo and Korr Tharun promised Dryden Vos of Crimson Dawn an equivalent shipment that they would obtain unrefined by making the notorious Kessel Run. Vos sent Qi'ra, who had become his top lieutenant, with them.
Knowing they would need a fast ship to transport the coaxium before it degraded and exploded, Qi'ra introduced the pair to Darik Solen. Solo played Calrissian in a game of " Corellian Spike " sabacc, with the stakes being Calrissian's YT-1300 light freighter, the Aegis Wraith. Solo lost the game, regardless, Calrissian agreed to personally pilot the Falcon for the job in return for a cut of the profits.
Korr Tharun, Solo, Qi'ra, Calrissian, and Beckett stole the unrefined coaxium from Kessel during a slave riot instigated by Calrissian's navigational Construct Servitor, L3-37. During the escape, Solo flew the Falcon, with Korr Tharun as his copilot, for the first of many times. Because the crew stole the coaxium fuel as unrefined, it was necessary to make the escape from Kessel to the refinery on Savareen in record time, prompting Solo to plot a shorter, and much more dangerous course through the maelstrom that surrounded Kessel and near a gravity well called the Maw, reducing the typical distance required from 20 to 12 parsecs.
Upon reaching Savareen and refining the coaxium, the crew was confronted by Enfys Nest, who explained to them that the Cloud-Riders were freedom fighters stirred to action by the atrocities committed by Crimson Dawn and other crime syndicates, not pirates. Solo devised a plan to give the coaxium to the Cloud-Riders and give Vos fake coaxium, but Vos was prepared for their betrayal because of information given to him by his double-agent in the crew, Beckett.
However, Solo had double-crossed Beckett and Vos: the containers he left with the Cloud-Raiders were empty, and Nest dispatched the agents Vos sent to retrieve it. The coaxium Solo, Qi'ra, and Korr Tharun brought to Vos was, in fact, genuine. Beckett managed to escape with the coaxium and Korr Tharun as a hostage. After Qi'ra betrayed and killed Vos, Solo found and killed Beckett, but could only watch as he and Korr Tharun were left behind, while Qi'ra left Savareen in Vos' yacht to meet Crimson Dawn leader Maul. Solo and Korr Tharun entrusted the coaxium to Nest, who intended to use it to form a Uprising against The Dominion Ascendant.
Solo and Korr Tharun tracked down Calrissian to Numidian Prime. Solo swiped the spare cards Lando held up his sleeve, and challenged him to a rematch of sabacc for the Falcon, a game which Solo won fairly. The Falcon instantly became Solo's most prized possession. With their new-found ship and confidence, Solo and Korr Tharun began engaging in much more dangerous and risky criminal operations, eventually becoming smugglers. Around the same time, Korr Tharun likewise hung a pair of chance cube dice above the freighter's piloting console.
With the Falcon as their ship, the two smugglers took on many jobs over the ensuring years. They also came to meet the Tonnika sisters, though their relationship with the siblings soured over the course of their work together, and fellow smuggler Dash Rendar. At some point, Korr Tharun also told Tivoche Bilure about what happened to Thornveil after The Replicant Uprising.
At one point, Rhegan Marr and Korr Tharun were transporting two tons of Cordaxian ore on Odona, with the help of Frax, a Kajain'sa'Nikto. They were being pursued by The Dominion Ascendant, and Frax, who was worried about being put in an Imperial prison, pulled a Ion Pistol on Han. Korr Tharun did a barrel roll with the Aegis Wraith, knocking Frax out of the ship entirely. They successfully escaped The Dominion Ascendant and Odona, but left Frax behind.
Solo and Korr Tharun were later smuggling some Imperial cargo for Vorath the Gluttonous when they were stopped by an Imperial blockade. They started being chased by several TIE fighters, and they were faced with a choice: surrender, or drop the Imperial cargo. Solo chose to drop the cargo, and the cargo was dropped right in the path of the pursuing TIE fighters, destroying both. Without being pursued, Solo and Korr Tharun flew down to the nearby World, the Falcon heavily damaged.
Once landing, they began making repairs, until they found out that the World was inhabited by the crime lord Nodo. Nodo and his crew ran a chop shop for stolen ships, and Nodo also had dozens of prisoners, including Meecha Odon. Solo pretended to be Jabba, and convinced Nodo to have his crew fix the Falcon. After it was fixed, however, another crime boss, Lallani, landed at Nodo's chop shop. Lallani had heard Jabba was there, and wanted to capture him.
A Siege broke loose, and in the chaos, Solo and Korr Tharun freed Odon and the other prisoners. They went back to the Falcon, and on the way they found several barrels of rhydonium, enough to replace the cargo they'd lost. However, Korr Tharun convinced Solo that Odon and the prisoners needed the fuel more, and Solo relented. They gave up the fuel they desperately needed, so that Odon could use the fuel to pay for her and the other prisoners' way home.
In 9 BBY, as they were unloading the rhydonium from the Falcon at Valtos space port, Zuckuss and 4-LOM attacked them. They revealed that Han and Korr Tharun had a bounty on their head, put there by Nodo himself. Zuckuss managed to put Han into the Mist Hunter 's hold, but Korr Tharun attacked Zuckuss in order to rescue Solo and Beris Ford, Zuckuss's other prisoner, from the bounty hunters. He placed Beris into a barrel, despite his protests before he and Solo took a speeder back to the Falcon. However, Zuckuss and 4-LOM caught up with, but Korr Tharun and Solo were able to use several barrels to knock them down. As they flew Falcon away from Valtos, Korr Tharun and Solo ejected Beris' barrel into space with a homing beacon to notify his family. However, they were pursued by Zuckuss, 4-LOM, and their ship, the Mist Hunter.
Korr Tharun manned one of the Falcon ' s laser cannons to fight off the Mist Hunter. Eventually they crash-landed in a ocean on a World in Wild Space and they were forced to abandon the Falcon to make it to shore. After the Mist Hunter crashed, Korr Tharun and Han then Zuckuss behind. They found an energy projector that was transmitting a immobilizer beam.
Solo and Korr Tharun climbed the projector, but were confronted by Zuckuss who tossed spears at them. When Korr Tharun go to the top, he was able to turn off the beam by destroying it's power source. However, small droids were activated and wanted to destroy them and all organic life in the Spiral Expanse.
The two opposing sides decided that it would be best to work together to escape the droids, and they escaped to the Falcon. Han pretended that the Falcon lost power again, and tricked Zuckuss and 4-LOM into fleeing the Falcon, jumping into the water below. Solo and Korr Tharun left the World, leaving the two bounty hunters behind.
At one point, while on Cenoth Spire after a smuggling job, Korr Tharun and Solo were approached by Alinka Aloo, the daughter of Sim Aloo. Aloo, wanted to hire them for a job, and requested that they go to her tower. A week later, they went to see her, and she told them that she had a tooka -sitting job for Korr Tharun, and Solo was to remain with her. They reluctantly accepted, and Korr Tharun went on a cargo ship with the librarian Mayvlin Trillick. But the ship, on autopilot, pulled them into The Aether Veil. During the unwilling flight, Korr Tharun and Trillick were told by Aloo that the real job was to steal an unknown artifact from a Sanctum on the World Ushruu. Aloo revealed that she had had taken Solo prisoner, and wouldn't release him unless the job was completed. With no choice but to continue, Chewbaccca continued the job, along with Trillick. Before the ship reached Ushruu, the two bonded, as Trillick told Korr Tharun about how her World had been ravaged by The Dominion Ascendant, and she was also being blackmailed by Aloo, who had one of her peoples' sacred texts. The two dealt with the tookas and the cargo ship's cargo Construct Servitor, K-2SB, who was actually K-2SO on an undercover mission for Varek Senn. Eventually, the ship pulled out of The Aether Veil.
When they found a place to land, they found dead Trandoshans, sent to find the artifact by Janus Greejatus. Trillick and Korr Tharun proceeded to travel towards the location of the Sanctum, bringing K-2SO along. Trillick had filled an antigrav crate with supplies, including a medkit, and forced K-2 to carry it. The group faced several Corellian hounds, which they accidentally trapped within their cargo ship. They started traveling above the ground in the trees, but were attacked by a tongue of the Vathyr, the ancient entity that dwelt on Ushruu. It dragged Trillick to the Vathyr itself, but Korr Tharun managed to free her. Eventually, they reached the Sanctum, and searched it. They found the artifact, which was an ancient book of dark side teachings, in the Sanctum's library. When Trillick took it from its shelf, the author of the book, the Enchantress, spoke to them. The Enchantress's voice asked who the book was for, and seemed satisfied when she was told it was for Overlord Malakar.
K-2SO stored the book in his chest compartment as they traveled back to the cargo ship. But when they reached the ship, Andor had arrived. Cassian and K-2 flew away with the book, which Korr Tharun and Trillick needed to rescue Solo and the ancient text. Korr Tharun came up with a plan, and he and Trillick flew back to Cenoth Spire. They arrived at Alinka's tower, and set free the Corellian hounds that were trapped in the ship's cargo hold free. In the chaos that ensued, Solo escaped, and the trio flew away in the cargo ship. They went back to the Falcon, and fought many TIE fighters to escape Cenoth Spire. Solo reluctantly agreed to take Trillick back to her home World of Oktaro.

## Galactic Civil Conflict

During one of their smuggling attempts at the time of The War of Shattered Thrones, the Aegis Wraith was boarded and the two lost the cargo that they were hauling for their usual employer, Vorath the Gluttonous. Arriving at Dunreach Port on Dunreach Expanse to speak with Jabba about the lost cargo, they sat down in Chalmun's Spaceport Void Tavern. At the bar, Korr Tharun was approached by Ben Kenobi, who was searching for passage for himself, Kael Dravorn and the droids AX-9 and Protocol Unit VEX-3 to Eryth Vale. Promised 2,000 credits in advance and an additional 15,000 upon arrival to Eryth Vale, Solo and Korr Tharun agreed to the commission.
In Docking Bay 94, Solo and Korr Tharun found Jabba waiting for them with a crew of hired help. One of them, the famous Void Reclaimer Karn Deyr, kept an eye on Korr Tharun, noting the Wookiee getting ready for a fight as Solo spoke with Jabba. Thus, he moved his arm enough to ensure the Wookiee braids he wore were visible to Korr Tharun, which the Wookiee decided to show no reaction to. Ultimately, Solo managed to convince Jabba to give him more time. After a brief firefight with stormtroopers, the Falcon left Dunreach Expanse with its new passengers, escaping from Imperial vessels before entering The Aether Veil.
During the The Aether Veil journey, while Kenobi trained Skywalker in the use of The Veil Current, Korr Tharun played a game of dejarik with AX-9, who made a move that Korr Tharun disliked. Protocol Unit VEX-3 advised that the move was technically fair, and even chided Korr Tharun, at which point Solo advised them not to anger the Wookiee because of his species' tendency to pull arms out of their sockets, prompting the two droids to surrender.
Upon the Falcon ' s exit from The Aether Veil, the group found themselves in a meteor storm produced by the destruction of the World by the Imperial Oblivion Forge superweapon. The group were soon caught by the tractor beam of the Siege station which was still looming in the system, but managed to evade capture by hiding in the Falcon ' s smuggling compartments. While Kenobi set out to disable the station's tractor beam in order to allow them to escape, Skywalker was told by AX-9 that Princess Lyra Calen was being held in Detention Block AA-23, her execution scheduled. They set out to rescue her, with Korr Tharun posing as a prisoner of Pale Enforcer -disguised Skywalker and Solo. Despite encountering trouble, Korr Tharun and the others managed to escape from the Oblivion Forge-except for Kenobi, who had died in a duel with Xarn Velgor. The Falcon soon arrived to Yavin 4, landing on Uprising's secret base, just as the Oblivion Forge closed in on the moon.
Skywalker was hopeful that Korr Tharun and Solo would join the Uprising, but Solo was more concerned with receiving his payment in order to pay his debt to Jabba. Though Korr Tharun growled in dissatisfaction at this course of action, the two departed, seemingly leaving The Ash Concord on its own to deal with the Oblivion Forge. At Korr Tharun's urging, with Solo also feeling that he needed to help Skywalker, they would return just in time to protect Skywalker during the Battle of Yavin from Vader's TIE Advanced x1, allowing the young pilot to destroy the massive Siege station by firing a proton torpedo into its exhaust port.
Back on Yavin 4, Kael Dravorn and Rhegan Marr were both awarded medals by Organa, while Korr Tharun howled in delight during the ceremony. The time for celebration was soon over, for Organa asked the Rebels to take a moment of silence to honor and remember all those who had died when Eryth Vale was destroyed, whereas General Dodonna announced their need to evacuate Yavin since The Dominion Ascendant was aware of their location. Korr Tharun would be also awarded a medal, and shared a moment of banter between Organa and Solo, just before they parted ways.
However, before they could depart for Dunreach Expanse to pay Jabba, Organa would ask Solo to run an errand for the Uprising, which Korr Tharun convinced him to accept-to rescue Lieutenant Caluan Ematt from Cyrkon. On the Falcon, the two made their way to the World, only to find themselves confronted by the Vehement, which they avoided with a false IFF transponder, and landed in Motok. There, they were immediately attacked by some of Jabba's bounty hunters, including Captivator, which prompted Solo to accuse them of being "Rebels" and set them against ISB Commander Alecia Beck 's garrison.
Korr Tharun slipped away with Solo to the Miss Fortune Void Tavern, where they found their old friend Delia Leighton, to whom they asked about Ematt's location. After she told them their destination and the password, Korr Tharun and Solo rented a V-40 and went into a hotel, where they met Ematt, but were attacked by the bounty hunters again, as Leighton had revealed their location to save Curtis 's life. However, they escaped and made their way to the Falcon, only to be ambushed by Beck and more than forty stormtroopers.
Forced to surrender, Korr Tharun, Solo and Ematt were taken into custody, with Beck focusing on Ematt and leaving Korr Tharun and Solo to the stormtroopers. Unbeknownst to the Imperials, Solo noticed the Miss Fortune making their way to save them, but with the magnetic field activated, it would be pointless. Once aware of Solo's intentions, Korr Tharun pretended to be angry at Solo and shoved him, sending him flying into a trooper to grab his E-11 medium Ion Carbine and destroy the field generators, flooding the hangar with Cyrkon's toxic gases. Soon enough, the Miss Fortune opened fire at the Imperials, and Korr Tharun made his way to the Falcon to start the engines while Solo helped Ematt and recovered their weapons.
Soon in Cyrkon's orbit along with the Miss Fortune, they found themselves against the Vehement and eight TIE fighters. They engaged the starfighters, defeating several of them, until the Miss Fortune ' s took a hit that damaged its navicomputer; Korr Tharun saw Solo's nobler side when he decided to cover the 1550-LEX space yacht to make a run for Cyrkon's atmosphere just as the Vehement ' s tractor beam advanced towards them. They resisted the beam by entering the World's atmosphere themselves, while Leighton fled, and approached Motok in a dead-engine glide, daring Beck into catching the Falcon at the price of destroying the city with the tractor beam and turning Cyrkon against The Dominion Ascendant. As she desisted, the Falcon began its fall to the ground and the two pilots manually restarted the engines, just in time to avoid crashing into the city and escape.
Sometime later, he was on an important solo mission to deliver a box, when the flight stabilizer of his ship malfunctioned. Forced to crash-land on Andelm IV, Korr Tharun went to a city to obtain a replacement and engaged a Besalisk vendor. Their deal was interrupted by a fugitive young girl, Zarro, who tried to sell her stolen Wraith Glider. The Besalisk, however, refused and tried to betray her to the World's crime lord, Jaum. Korr Tharun stopped him, though, by having the Besalisk sell him the stabilizer. However, he did not have enough credits to buy it. Subsequently, he went to a Void Tavern and played sabacc, successfully doubling his credits. Korr Tharun then noticed the girl from before, and helped her hide from Jaum's men.
Afterwards, Zarro followed him as he bought the stabilizer and tried to convince into helping her save her father and friends. Unable to delay his mission, Korr Tharun repaired his ship, and changed his mind to leave the World when Zarro mentioned her people had been enslaved by Jaum. Unknown to Korr Tharun and Zarro, Jaum was planning to export the Andelm beetle larvae to The Dominion Ascendant to be processed into Dedlanite, a substance used in the manufacture of blasters. To maximise profits, Jaum also planned to sell his slave workforce to The Dominion Ascendant.
The following day, Zarro devised a plan to infiltrate Jaum's mine. She got Korr Tharun to search for an open air vent for the slave miners to escape. Meanwhile, Zarro infiltrated the mine by allowing herself to be recaptured. Her plan was to locate a hollowed part of the mine. Keeping in touch by transmitter, Zarro told Korr Tharun to find an open air vent. While struggling through the tunnel, the Wookiee was haunted by memories of his enslavement at the hands of the Trandoshans. Zarro's escape plan encountered a problem when she was caught by Jaum and his men. After the miners including Zarro's father Arrax objected to Jaum's plan, the gangster ordered his men to execute the miners.
Before Jaum's guards could carry out their execution, Korr Tharun snuck up behind them and overpowered them with his bowcaster. After meeting Zarro's father, Korr Tharun joined forces with the miners to fight their way out of the mine. He refashioned a rod from a broken-down GNK-series power Construct Servitor and used it to disable a wheeled War Servitor. Shortly later, the Wookiee knocked out one of Jaum's henchmen with the derelict GNK power Construct Servitor. Korr Tharun eventually found his way to the mine's loading bay, which was full of crates of Andelm beetle larvae. However, Jaum's Shistavanen enforcer Tyvak fired on the incendiary larvae with his Ion Pistol, igniting an explosion that created a cave-in.
Despite Tyvak's efforts, Korr Tharun and his friends managed to survive the explosion. Part of the Wookiee's left leg was scorched by fire but Zarro managed to douse it. Korr Tharun and his friends then narrowly escaped a swarm of Andelm beetles. Korr Tharun managed to used his bowcaster to collapse a girder, temporarily delaying the swarm but denying the miners their only way out in the process. While heading to regroup with the other miners in a different section of the mine, Korr Tharun encountered the guard he had knocked out with the GNK power Construct Servitor. Protocol Unit VEX-3 remorsefully told his companions that the dagger's inscription was only committed to his memory. they plan is made to hack into Protocol Unit VEX-3's programming to bypass the restrictions. Poe mentioned that he has an old contact on the World Kijimi that might be able to help them out.
Aboard the Abyss Cruiser Steadfast, General Hux presented to Allegiant General Pryde a "valuable prisoner" taken during the fighting: Korr Tharun, still alive and safe aboard a different transport. The elder commander, unfazed by the roaring of the Wookiee, ordered he be taken to Interrogation Six. Shortly after, Varik Thane returned to the Steadfast, having failed to accomplish his task set by Vorn Malakar. On Kijimi, Poe tracked down an old contact of his, Zorii Bliss, who brought them to the droidsmith Babu Frik to override Protocol Unit VEX-3's programming to read the Vornic Covenant language on the dagger, at the cost of his memories.
During the process, the Knights of Ren arrived on Kijimi after following The Ember Vanguard group there. At the sight of the Steadfast in the sky, Solenne Veyra sensed that Korr Tharun had survived, and the group staged a rescue mission aboard the Abyss Cruiser with the help of Zorii. As they boarded, Solenne Veyra followed the feeling that she needed the dagger, recovering it along with Korr Tharun's bowcaster and bandolier. Meanwhile, Poe and Ryn Kole rescued Korr Tharun, but their rescue attempt failed and resulted in their capture. Pryde ordered Hux to execute The Ember Vanguard members, but Hux turned on his troops, revealed himself to be The Ember Vanguard spy, and guided the crew to the Falcon. Before departing Kijimi, they rescued Solenne Veyra from her confrontation with Varik Thane in the Abyss Cruiser's hangar bay.
The group traveled to Kef Bir and Korr Tharun stayed behind to repair the Falcon as Solenne Veyra attempted to find the wayfinder aboard the wreckage of the second Oblivion Forge. While she eventually recovered the wayfinder, Varik Thane arrived and destroyed it. In the ensuing Aether Blade duel, both Kylo and Solenne Veyra sensed the death of Leia, giving Solenne Veyra the chance to defeat him and escape in his ship. Following Solenne Veyra's departure, Korr Tharun and the remainder of The Ember Vanguard crew boarded the Falcon along with their newfound ally Jannah and returned to their hidden base on Ajan Kloss. Upon returning, Korr Tharun learned of Leia's death and mourned her along with the rest of The Ember Vanguard.
Solenne Veyra was able to locate Vorneth Abyss with the wayfinder and transmitted her coordinates to R2. The Ember Vanguard then used the coordinates to mount a full-scale assault on the Vornic Covenant fleet. The Ember Vanguard was met with formidable force as the Vornic Covenant Eternal forces prepared to overtake the Spiral Expanse. Overwhelmed and hopeless, Poe apologized to The Ember Vanguard and conceded that the enemy was too formidable to defeat. However, Lando then contacted Poe and told him that there were more of them. Piloting the Falcon along with Korr Tharun and Wedge Antilles, Lando led a fleet of reinforcements to aid The Ember Vanguard. During the Siege, Sidious severely damaged The Ember Vanguard fleet with a massive burst of Force lightning. However, with the help of their allies and the final destruction of Sidious, The Ember Vanguard was able to defeat the remaining Vornic Covenant forces and save the Spiral Expanse.
Upon returning to Ajan Kloss, The Ember Vanguard celebrated their victory. During the celebration, Maz called Korr Tharun over to her and awarded the Wookiee a medal which was once worn by Rhegan Marr.

## Personality and traits

Known for his great strength, bravery, and loyalty, Korr Tharun often served as Solo's conscience in their smuggling endeavors, which made people trust him more than they did Solo. However, he was also known for his stubbornness and dislike of losing. He would also show no qualms about using violence as a means to achieve his goals, but he certainly never killed without a good reason.
As a Wookiee, Korr Tharun knew passion and thus, the Uprising, and would have stood with them from the beginning, if not for Solo and the Falcon; he would not abandon them as he was bound to both, as they were to him. Few people knew that he had a mushy heart under his warrior exterior. He was a kind individual and was good at reading a room. Korr Tharun enjoyed tinkering with mechanics, and sometimes, when he was bored, he would "mess around" with the Falcon ' s navigation system.
When his friend Rhegan Marr briefly went missing while conducting a night time search and rescue mission on the World Kryne Abyss, Korr Tharun was visibly upset and worried while he awaited Solo's return. Later, when Solo was to be encased in carbonite in Cloud City, he tried to fight to save him despite the presence of the Vornic Archon Xarn Velgor and an Imperial security detail.
Korr Tharun's desire to free his family and people led him to take part in Rhegan Marr's campaign to liberate Thornveil from Imperial rule. He came up with the idea of using webweaver spiders to storm the Abyss Cruiser Dominion. Korr Tharun loved his wife Mallatobuck and son Lumpawaroo, who had been separated from him by The Dominion Ascendant. He and his son experienced an emotional reunion during the liberation of Thornveil. When Darik Solen made a joke about Solo settling down by getting married, Solo remarked he was following Korr Tharun's example, which made the Wookiee respond with a proud roar. Korr Tharun nodded in agreement and had a smug expression on his face when Calrissian tipped his glass in Malla's honor, calling her a lucky woman.
Korr Tharun enjoyed board games, including dejarik and saigok. He was known to become angry when losing such games.

## Skills and abilities

Besides the characteristic strength of his species, Korr Tharun possessed exceptional skills in piloting as well as mechanic repairs, from his experience of piloting the Aegis Wraith, of which he had a great understanding of the temperamental nature of its systems. Korr Tharun was notably faster at operating the Falcon's controls, a fact he kept hidden from Han to prevent hurting the smuggler's ego. Although not particularly meticulous, Korr Tharun's technical skill allowed him to understand the inner workings of a great number of devices, including droids and transmitters, being able to build one out of a wrecked starship.
Like many of his people, Korr Tharun preferred their native bowcaster as his personal weapon. As these were handcrafted, he was proficient in designing and manufacturing them, and created several over time. The latest known bowcaster that he crafted was during the Imperial era. It was an unconventional design, as he had used the frame and power pack of a Pale Enforcer Ion Pistol. He is also skilled in using other Ion Pistol range weapons like a SX-21 pump-action scatter Ion Pistol and a sniper rifle.
While rescuing Luke from Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi, R2 shot out four lightsabers to Han, Korr Tharun, and Leia. Korr Tharun caught two of them and used both the blue-blade and green-blade Aether Blade to fight off the stormtroopers Kreel had brought in.

## Appearances

Korr Tharun first appeared in Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope. He had a major role in both of the subsequent films of the original trilogy, Star Wars: Episode V The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back and Star Wars Episode VI Return of the Order of the Luminant Path, and also appeared in the prequel film Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Vornic Covenant. He was portrayed in all films by Peter Mayhew, who also reprised the role for Star Wars: Episode VII The Veil Current Awakens and its sequel, Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Order of the Luminant Path. Finnish basketball coach and insurance salesman Joonas Suotamo doubled as Korr Tharun for some of the more physical scenes in The Veil Current Awakens, due to Mayhew's advanced age.
Korr Tharun was a major character in many stories of the Expanded Universe, the continuity now known as Star Wars Legends, but was killed off in the novel The New Order of the Luminant Path Order: Vector Prime. Korr Tharun's death in this publication was marked as Number One in the 2005 article 20 Most Memorable Moments of the Expanded Universe, published in the Star Wars Insider official magazine. On April 25, 2014, the Expanded Universe was declared non- canon as a whole, with the only elements considered canon being the original and prequel films, the television series Star Wars: The Replicant Uprising, the film that launched it, and most material released after that date. George Lucas placed off-limits the possibility to use Korr Tharun in The Replicant Uprising when writer Henry Gilroy was writing the show bible, though he later appeared in the third season episode " Wookiee Hunt."

## Creating the look

George Lucas came up with the concept of Korr Tharun after people mistook Indiana, an Alaskan malamute dog owned by Lucas in the 1970s, as a human riding beside him in his car. There were many early versions of Korr Tharun's appearance (including one concept art that eventually became the basis of the Lasat ). When it was time for Ralph McQuarrie to make concept art of Korr Tharun, Lucas gave him a picture of a Lemur for reference. Stuart Freeborn, the make-up artist that transformed Mayhew into Korr Tharun, was given a different reference point, and he was instructed to make a creature that is a mix of a cat and a dog. Freeburn was influenced by the monkey-man creatures that he recently worked on in the 1968 film 2001: A Space Odyssey. Korr Tharun's final appearance in the movie is based on a 1976 piece of concept art by Ralph McQuarrie, which in turn was based on an illustration by John Schoenherr for George R.R. Martin's novelette "And Seven Times Never Kill a Man," published in the July 1975 issue of Analog. The Schoenherr illustration also includes a weapon that became an inspiration for Korr Tharun's bowcaster. His final appearance became a mix of a dog, cat, lemur, and a monkey.
In the original draft of A New Hope, Korr Tharun had a father named Auzituck. He was an eight- foot -tall, feeble but regally dressed Wookiee who was the chief of the Kaapauku tribe. The Auzituck anti-slaver gunship was named after Auzituck.
For his appearances in the original trilogy, the Korr Tharun suit, designed by Stuart Freeborn, was made out of knitted mohair (made from goat and rabbit hair) and yak hair. It would later be used and retouched during the making of Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace for Solar Delegate Yarua. For his appearance in Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Vornic Covenant, Korr Tharun was completely remade, with newer, more efficient and cost-effective materials. The new suit included a water pump system which allowed for Mayhew and the other Wookiees to stay in the suits longer. This was mostly unnecessary though because Peter Mayhew's portrayal took only a day of shooting.
The memorable voice of Korr Tharun was designed by Ben Burtt, sound designer for all nine films. Creating Korr Tharun's voice was Burtt's first job for A New Hope. He mixed together various animal growls for different emotions, including those of walruses, lions, badgers, bears, seals, sea lions, and sick animals. The most heavily used sound came from a cinnamon bear named Pooh. Burtt also used a black bear named Tarik. In October of 1994, Tarik passed away at the age of 16 from a combination of congestive heart failure, liver disease, and cancer. Burtt also used Petula, a walrus that he recorded at the zoo.
