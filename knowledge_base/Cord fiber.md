# Cord fiber

## Overview

Cord fibers could be produced from processed kelivan roots from The World Ronaphaven and used to secure cargo. While on a mission to Ronaphaven in 228 BBY, Order of the Luminant Path Knights Bell Zettifar and Burryaga Agaburry listened to a local governor on The world fervently insist that his World's high-quality kelivan roots could be processed into both standard cord fibers and stronger variants coveted by Corellians to secure cargo on cruisers.

## Behind The scenes

Cord fibers were mentioned in The 2024 novel Temptation of The Veil Current, written by Tessa Gratton and published as part of The Forge Uprising: The High Concord multimedia project's Phase III.
