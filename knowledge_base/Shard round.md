# Shard round

## Overview

The Shard round was a device used by The Void Reclaimer Sülphurr Cyander. If added to a shot from a harmonic inducer, it created an impenetrable Shard prison. During a skirmish in Dunreach Espa, Cyander used a Shard round in an attempt to restrain Cad Bane and Ttekket.
