# Protocol Unit VEX-3

## Overview

Protocol Unit VEX-3 ( See-Threepio ) was a 3PO-series Liaison Servitor designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Threepio, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the Navigator Construct Construct Servitor AX-9, Protocol Unit VEX-3 constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history, and aided in saving the Spiral Expanse on many occasions.
Protocol Unit VEX-3 was rebuilt from spare parts by Erynd Korval, an enslaved human who lived in Mos Espa, a city on the Outer Rim world of Dunreach Expanse. During The Replicant Uprising, he would serve the Velora Prime Solar Delegate Seren Valora. Shortly after The Replicant Uprising, Protocol Unit VEX-3's memory was erased, though AX-9's memory was not. Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 were assigned to the Eryth Vale cruiser Tantive IV, where they served Solar Delegate Bail Organa for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling Vanguard movement against The Iron Dominion. At some point during this time, Protocol Unit VEX-3's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.
Protocol Unit VEX-3 remained constant companions with R2, and in 0 BBY, the pair became embroiled in The War of Shattered Thrones when Lyra Calen of The Ash Concord entrusted them to bring a copy of the Oblivion Forge plans to the Order of the Luminant Path Master Arion Velth on Dunreach Expanse. After meeting Skywalker's son, Luke and the smuggler Rhegan Marr, the droids helped rescue Princess Leia from The Dominion Ascendant's Oblivion Forge. They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their Uprising defeat The Dominion Ascendant and restore freedom to the Spiral Expanse, most notably when he convinced the Ewoks to aid the Rebels at the Battle of Endor.
Following the Battle of Endor, Protocol Unit VEX-3 continued serving the Skywalkers and Solo during the time of The Luminant Accord. About thirty years after the events on Vaross Reach, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was still an aide to Organa, who had become a general in The Ember Vanguard, a private military group opposed to The Prime Directive, a military junta that emulated the Old Dominion. By that time, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not a slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, Protocol Unit VEX-3's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Vornic Covenant dialects. He would assist Solenne Veyra in translating Vornic Covenant writings in order to locate High Wraith Malakar and the Vornic Covenant Eternal 's fleet, the Final Order, on Vorneth Abyss, allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend R2 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after The Ember Vanguard defeated Sidious and the Vornic Covenant Eternal in the Siege of Vorneth Abyss, his memory was restored.

## Early incarnation

In an early incarnation, Protocol Unit VEX-3 served as a Liaison Servitor to the chief negotiator for the Manakron system. Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters, which he said were very similar to moisture vaporators in most respects.

## Serving the Maker

At some point, however, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was seriously damaged and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Dunreach Expanse. Erynd Korval, a slave boy from the Tatooinian city of Mos Espa, collected scrap parts and started rebuilding Protocol Unit VEX-3 in 34 BBY so the Construct Servitor would help his mother. As Anakin longed for a life far from Dunreach Expanse and explore the Spiral Expanse, he hoped to have Protocol Unit VEX-3 as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the Spiral Expanse one day and understand and understood the languages of the natives they could come across with, given Protocol Unit VEX-3's capability of millions of communication forms. Although protocol droids were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Skywalker specially modified Protocol Unit VEX-3 so he could withstand Dunreach Expanse's sand and heat. Protocol Unit VEX-3 served Anakin and his mother Shmi by performing household chores. During his time with Skywalker and Shmi, Protocol Unit VEX-3's parts and wiring were left exposed since Skywalker was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to Construct Servitor plating being too expensive.
Following the Trade Federation 's invasion of the Mid Rim World of Velora Prime, Protocol Unit VEX-3's life entered a new chapter when his owner Anakin met several off-worlders at the shop of his owner, the Junk dealer Marrik the Broker, and then invited them to seek shelter at his home during a sandstorm. The Order of the Luminant Path Master Elder Kaelor, the Queen of Velora Prime Seren Valora, the Gungan Jar Jar Binks, and the Navigator Construct Construct Servitor AX-9 were brought to the slave quarters that Anakin shared with his mother Shmi. As a result of this invitation, Amidala and AX-9 were introduced to Protocol Unit VEX-3. Anakin plugged in one of Protocol Unit VEX-3's photoreceptors, which became the first time Protocol Unit VEX-3 had experienced sight. Upon meeting AX-9, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was shocked when the candid Navigator Construct Construct Servitor commented that he was "naked." Despite the exchange, the two droids fast developed a friendship of sorts, working together to help Skywalker make final adjustments to his Ion Skimmer in time to race it in the Boonta Eve Classic race. While working on the Ion Skimmer, the two droids discussed space travel and noted the clumsy actions of Binks.
On the day of the race, Protocol Unit VEX-3 represented his owner in the opening flag parade, carrying Skywalker's flag onto the course prior to the start of the race. He then stood on the sidelines with AX-9 and two of Skywalker's playmates, Kitster Banai and Wald, who cheered their friend on. Skywalker won the race, which, thanks to a deal that Jinn had struck with Marrik the Broker, earned the boy his freedom from slavery and secured Jinn the Aether Engine generator he needed to leave the World. As Skywalker prepared to depart Dunreach Expanse, he said his goodbyes to Protocol Unit VEX-3, apologizing for not having completed the Construct Servitor's construction, and expressing his wishes that Shmi wouldn't sell him.

## Beyond Dunreach Expanse

Later, his owner Shmi was sold to the moisture farmer Cliegg Lars, who freed and married her. Shmi brought Protocol Unit VEX-3 along with her, and the Liaison Servitor found himself in the service of the Lars family, who owned a moisture farm in the Jundland Wastes. Shmi covered him in a tarnished grey plating to shield him from Dunreach Expanse's sandy environment. Ten years after his parting with Erynd Korval, Shmi was kidnapped by a group of Tusken Raiders, fearsome nomads native to Dunreach Expanse's desert wastes. Sensing his mother's pain and anguish through The Veil Current, Anakin returned with his secret lover and childhood friend Seren Valora to find his mother. Skywalker had advanced in his Order of the Luminant Path training as a Initiate of the Luminant Path under the tutelage of Arion Velth, while Amidala had become a Solar Delegate in the Galactic Senate.
When Skywalker and Amidala visited the Lars' moisture farm, they found Protocol Unit VEX-3 working on a moisture vaporator. He initially didn't recognize Skywalker until the latter finished his name for him when introducing himself. Greeting his Maker, Protocol Unit VEX-3 discovered that Skywalker had come in search of his mother, and brought the two guests into the homestead's courtyard, where they were met by Cliegg Lars, as well as his son, Owen Lars and his girlfriend, Beru Whitesun. Learning of his mother's peril, Skywalker took Owen's Wraith Glider to find her, and soon returned with her corpse, having recovered it from a Tusken village. Protocol Unit VEX-3 was present when he arrived, and later attended a funeral for Shmi on the farm's property. When AX-9, who had been waiting aboard Amidala's nearby yacht, interrupted the funeral with an urgent message for the Initiate of the Luminant Path, Protocol Unit VEX-3 translated the Navigator Construct's binary beeps and whistles. After viewing the holocomm message, Skywalker and Amidala departed Dunreach Expanse for Draxon Hive to rescue Skywalker's Order of the Luminant Path Master Arion Velth, taking Threepio with them.
Thus, Protocol Unit VEX-3 would become both a witness and participant in the subsequent First Siege of Draxon Hive, the first spark of The Replicant Uprising, a conflict that would change the course of galactic history. After landing on Draxon Hive, Protocol Unit VEX-3 reluctantly followed AX-9, Skywalker, and Amidala into the Geonosian Construct Servitor foundry. After being trapped in the factory's conveyor belt system, Protocol Unit VEX-3's head was separated from his body and grafted onto the chassis of a B1-series War Servitor. Meanwhile, a B1 War Servitor head was inserted onto Threepio's body. During the ensuing Siege, both of Protocol Unit VEX-3's "bodies" were knocked out during the fighting. Fortunately for him, he was promptly reattached to his rightful body by AX-9. Following the events on Draxon Hive, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 travelled to Velora Prime with Skywalker and Amidala for the two to be married in Velora Prime's Lake Country.
In the days following the Draxon Hive Siege and before the wedding, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was determined to keep Skywalker and Amidala out of each other's sight until the wedding itself. Whilst waiting for the ceremony, Amidala believed there was a high chance Skywalker was biding his time by working on Protocol Unit VEX-3, as the Construct Servitor needed to be cleaned after the sands of Dunreach Expanse had years to get into his joints, only to be further dirtied by the sands of Draxon Hive. As such, the protocol unit needed to receive a cleaning before he could receive his yet-to-be-finished new plating. Despite Protocol Unit VEX-3's attempts, the two humans did see each other before the wedding, as Amidala, per a request from Queen Jamillia of Velora Prime, needed the help of AX-9 and Skywalker to rescue the Torada Collective from the Separatist World Nooroyo. After completing the mission, they returned to the Lake House to be wed, with Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 in attendance.

## The Replicant Uprising

During The Replicant Uprising, Protocol Unit VEX-3 would serve Solar Delegate Seren Valora, the spouse of Erynd Korval, and was given a shiny gold - colored plating to better suit his new role. On one occasion, Amidala was captured by Jabba's uncle Ziro during a mission to reopen communications with the Hutts after Jabba's son Rotta was kidnapped by the Separatist leader Kaedran Vale. Amidala contacted Protocol Unit VEX-3, who in turn contacted the Cenoth Spire Guard, which promptly raided Ziro's Palace and freed Amidala. Meanwhile, Skywalker and his Initiate of the Luminant Path Ahsoka Tano managed to rescue Rotta and return him safely to his father. Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his comrades' actions help Solar Council win over Jabba, who later signed a favorable treaty with them.
Following the Rotta affair, Protocol Unit VEX-3 accompanied Solar Delegate Amidala on a mission to contact the InterGalactic Banking Clan 's executive, who purportedly wanted to sue for peace. Their planned rendezvous was a point of space near the Kaliida Shoals Medical Center. Unknown to the two of them, this was a trap concocted by High Wraith Malakar and his apprentice Kaedran Vale. The Separatist military commander Lord Virek 's flagship Malevolence had been damaged during a recent space Siege and was unable to enter The Aether Veil. The Vornic Covenant and Separatists hoped to use Solar Delegate Amidala as a hostage to extract leverage from Solar Council commander Skywalker, who was pursuing the Malevolence.
Amidala's vessel was trapped in the Separatist warship's tractor beam, but they broke free by exploding their vessel's engines. Protocol Unit VEX-3 and Amidala then sneaked aboard and contacted Skywalker for help. Skywalker along with Kenobi and AX-9 boarded the Malevolence and made arrangements to rendezvous with Protocol Unit VEX-3 and the Solar Delegate at the warship's rail jet system. However, they were accosted by Siege droids there, which prompted Amidala to shove Protocol Unit VEX-3 into a moving hovertrain. After the Siege droids were neutralized, Kenobi began levitating Protocol Unit VEX-3 to safety, but the floating Construct Servitor was hit full-on by a moving hover train, which was only halted when AX-9 sliced into a computer terminal and shut it down. The sudden stop launched Protocol Unit VEX-3 onto a nearby platform, where he quickly regained his bearings and again began wandering the cruiser's corridors before being located by AX-9. Together, the two droids escaped the Malevolence before the Separatist warship crashed into a nearby moon after being sabotaged by Skywalker.
Following their adventures on the Malevolence, Protocol Unit VEX-3 along with Solar Delegate Amidala and the Gungan Representative Jar Jar Binks traveled to Rodia to meet with her old friend Solar Delegate Onaconda Farr. Amidala's mission was to ensure that the ailing Rodians remained loyal to Celestial Concord. While Threepio and Binks remained with Amidala's yacht on the landing platform, Solar Delegate Amidala went to meet Farr. An ill-fated attempt to contact local swamp creatures ended with some fruit being thrown into Protocol Unit VEX-3's face. Unknown to Amidala, Farr had already struck a deal with the Separatist leader Viceroy Nute Gunray of the Trade Federation.
Protocol Unit VEX-3 and Binks were soon confronted by several B1 Siege droids. During the ensuing confrontation, Threepio became stuck to a magnetic hoist that collided with the Separatist Siege droids, throwing them into the water below. While attempting to take off, Binks accidentally destroyed the yacht with the magnetic hoist. Threepio remained stuck to the hoist due to his metallic body, but Binks along with the yacht fell into the water below. Protocol Unit VEX-3 believed that Binks perished during the accident. However, Binks resurfaced and promptly released Protocol Unit VEX-3 from the hoist. While scouring through the wrecked yacht, Binks found and donned a Order of the Luminant Path robe.
Shortly, the two of them were separated again, and Threepio was captured by Separatist droids, who were ordered to march him to a dismantling center. However, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was rescued by Amidala, who destroyed his captors. She ordered Protocol Unit VEX-3 to send a distress signal to nearby Concord forces. Threepio succeeded in sending for help before being arrested by two more Separatist droids. Gunray then attempted to execute both Amidala and Protocol Unit VEX-3 but was foiled by Binks, who returned with a Kwazel Maw monster, which disposed of the Separatist droids. Solar Delegate Farr then turned on Gunray and arrested him, revealing that his collaboration with the Separatists had been a ruse to lure the Viceroy into a trap. Concord forces arrived on Rodia and promptly apprehended Gunray. Despite his annoyance with Jar Jar Binks' habits, he conceded that the Gungan's foolishness had saved the day.
Later, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 accompanied Skywalker, Kenobi, and Captain Rex on a mission to investigate the disappearance of a Replicant Vassal force on the icy World of Orto Plutonia. Their mission was complicated by the presence of several Pantoran delegates including Solar Delegate Riyo Chuchi and Chairman Chi Cho. The Pantorans claimed territorial sovereignty over Orto Plutonia even though it was supposedly devoid of sentient life. After discovering the dead clone troopers, the Order of the Luminant Path and Concord mission also discovered that a nearby Separatist base had been destroyed. They quickly deduced that a third force had been responsible for these two attacks.
Later, Skywalker and Kenobi discovered that Ord Plutonia was home to a species of primitive bipedal sentient beings called the Talz. After some difficulties, Kenobi and Skywalker established contact with the Talz and assured them that they had come in peace. The Talz chieftain Thi-Sen wanted Solar Council to leave his people in peace and to withdraw their presence from the World. After the Order of the Luminant Path had made contact with the Talz tribe, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and Cho joined them in meeting with Thi-Sen. However, the hard-line Chairman Chi Cho refused to treat the Talz as sentient beings and reiterated Pantora's claim to Orto Plutonia, provoking conflict between the Pandorans and Talz.
The Order of the Luminant Path ended the fighting by convincing Solar Delegate Chuchi to contact the Pantoran Assembly, which gave her the authority to negotiate peace with the Talz and to relieve Chairman Cho of his authority. Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his companions then accompanied Solar Delegate Chuchi to the scene of the fighting. There, Protocol Unit VEX-3 helped Chuchi to broker a truce with Thi-Sen, ending the Pandoran-Talz conflict. As a result of the fighting, the Pandorans ceded sovereignty over Plutonia to the Talz.
Following the discovery of a scouting party of Separatist Siege droids on Velora Prime, Protocol Unit VEX-3 accompanied Solar Delegate Amidala and Representative Binks back to Velora Prime. Amidala had been summoned by Queen Neeyutnee, who wanted to procure more military aid from Solar Council. Protocol Unit VEX-3 questioned a captured T-series tactical Construct Servitor, which had its photoreceptors removed. After convincing the Construct Servitor that it was aboard a Separatist starship, Protocol Unit VEX-3 gleaned that there was a secret Separatist facility on Velora Prime producing some sort of virus before Jar Jar Binks accidentally destroyed the Construct Servitor. Despite this setback, Binks and Amidala discovered a slug-beetle and mud from a swamp, which led them to Velora Prime's Eastern swamps.
While Threepio stayed behind on Theed with Captain Gregar Typho to examine data from the captured Separatist droids, Amidala and Binks ventured into the swamps and discovered a secret Separatist research facility headed by the scientist Doctor Nuvo Vindi. Vindi was developing a deadly bioweapon called the Blue Shadow Virus for the Separatists. Protocol Unit VEX-3 and Typho discovered a Spectral Relay of Dr. Vindi in one of the Separatist Siege droids and conveyed this information to their companions. With the aid of a local Gungan farmer named Peppi Bow, Skywalker, Kenobi, Ahsoka Tano, and several clone troopers, Amidala and Skywalker succeeded in apprehending Dr. Vindi and stopping him from releasing his bioweapon.
When Solar Delegate Amidala was organizing a party for Solar Delegate Aang, she sent Threepio and AX-9 to buy some jogan fruit that Artoo had forgotten to buy for garnishing the jogan fruitcake, Aang's favorite dessert. In the lower market districts, the two droids grumbled and Protocol Unit VEX-3 was swindled by a fruit vendor. Following the purchase, another Construct Servitor approached Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 and suggested that they visit Construct Servitor Spa for a cosmetic overhaul. While AX-9 visited the facility, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was kidnapped by the Separatist-aligned Duros Void Reclaimer Cad Bane 's Construct Servitor henchmen, Todo 360 and HELIOS-3E.
Threepio was taken to Bane's hideout in an industrial district on Cenoth Spire, where his databanks were scanned for information on The Solar Assembly Building's layout. While Protocol Unit VEX-3's databank yielded no such information, he mentioned that his Navigator Construct counterpart AX-9 might have the information. Bane's Construct Servitor henchmen promptly kidnapped AX-9 and brought him to Bane. After Bane extracted the necessary information from Artoo's databanks, he ordered that the two droids be given a memory wipe and returned to the place where they had been snatched.
Following the unpleasant shopping trip, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 returned to Amidala with their purchase. Both droids were completely unaware of what had befallen them. The jogan fruit was added to Solar Delegate Aang's dessert, as planned. Meanwhile, Bane delivered the plans from Artoo's memory banks to Vorath the Gluttonous on Dunreach Expanse. The Hutt crime lord ordered Bane to free his uncle Ziro from prison, since he knew information that would endanger the Hutts, who were nominally working with Solar Council. Bane hatched a plan to rescue Ziro.
Following his kidnapping and mind-wipe, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and Solar Delegate Amidala were caught up in The Solar Assembly hostage crisis. Cad Bane and several other mercenaries infiltrated The Solar Assembly Building and took Amidala, Threepio, Skywalker and several senators hostage in a bid to force a pardon for the imprisoned Ziro. These senators had been meeting to discussed the proposed Enhanced Privacy Invasion Bill. Despite Solar Council honoring Bane's demands, Bane planted several explosives in the hall with the aim of killing the senators. Skywalker saved the day by using his Aether Blade to cut a hole around the group, moments before the explosives went off.
Protocol Unit VEX-3 was briefly reunited with AX-9 when the two droids accompanied their masters Solar Delegate Amidala and General Skywalker on a mission to Cato Neimoidia. The quartet were accompanying Rush Clovis, the representative from Scipio and a delegate representing the InterGalactic Banking Clan in The Solar Assembly. The Luminant Synod suspected that Clovis was secretly spying for the Separatists and sent Skywalker and Amidala to spy on him. During the journey, Protocol Unit VEX-3 noticed Skywalker's hostile behavior towards Clovis, who was trying to flirt with his secret wife Amidala.
Upon arriving on Cato Neimoidia, the delegation was greeted by the Trade Federation's Solar Delegate Lott Dod, who tried unsuccessfully to force Clovis to lend more money for the Separatist cause. When Dod deduced that Clovis was infatuated with Solar Delegate Amidala, he arranged for her to be poisoned during a formal dinner. Despite this, Amidala succeeded in stealing a Spectral Relay disc which confirmed that Clovis was indeed working for the Separatists. After Amidala began to succumb to the effects of the poisoning, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and a 2-1B surgical Construct Servitor attended to the Solar Delegate in her quarters.
Meanwhile, Clovis learned that Amidala was spying on him and went to confront her only to be cornered by Skywalker. Realizing that Skywalker loved Amidala, Clovis offered to get the antidote in return for receiving back the holodisk. Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his companions brought Amidala back to their ship but were cornered by Dod and his aides. Clovis attempted to bluff his way out, but Dod refused to let them leave. After Clovis pulled a Ion Pistol and forced Dod to hand over the antidote, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his companions fled aboard Amidala's ship. They fled into space but left Clovis behind to face Dod alone.
As The Replicant Uprising dragged on, Protocol Unit VEX-3 continued serving Solar Delegate Amidala, who was becoming an active member of a group of Senators that supported diplomacy and opposed the expansion of the Grand Army of Solar Council. On one occasion, Protocol Unit VEX-3 accompanied Solar Delegate Amidala and Master Kenobi when they presented evidence in the Galactic Senate that the World Mandalore wished to be neutral. As a result, The Solar Assembly rescinded an earlier vote calling for a military occupation of Mandalore.
Later, Solar Delegate Amidala and her supporters including the Alderaanian Solar Delegate Bail Organa and the Rodian Solar Delegate Onaconda Farr proposed a bill calling for the reduction of military spending. Protocol Unit VEX-3 attended several meetings of this informal peace coalition in Solar Delegate Amidala's office. On one such occasion, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was serving drinks when Solar Delegate Farr suffered an apparent heart attack and died. Threepio and Amidala attended the funeral of Solar Delegate Farr. Later, Amidala and Organa discovered that Farr had been poisoned by his aide, Lolo Purs, who blamed the late Solar Delegate for bringing the Conflict to Rodia.
Following Solar Council's victory at Malastare, Chancellor Vorn Malakar ordered the Order of the Luminant Path Generals Windu and Skywalker to transport the Zillo Beast, a gargantuan monster that had been captured and sedated during the fighting. After learning that a Aether Blade could not harm the beast, Vorn Malakar enlisted the services of the scientist Sionver Boll to study the beast's scales in order to develop a new type of armor for the Grand Army. Vorn Malakar also planned to kill the monster with Malastarian fuel after it had outlived its usefulness.
Solar Delegate Amidala learned about Vorn Malakar's plan and arranged a meeting with Skywalker and Kenobi. During the meeting, Protocol Unit VEX-3 had another brief reunion with his Navigator Construct counterpart, AX-9. Amidala succeeded in convincing her husband Skywalker and the delegation met with Chancellor Vorn Malakar and Vice Chair Mas Amedda. However, the Zillo Beast escaped Boll's laboratory at the same time and proceeded to wreak havoc on the streets of Cenoth Spire. Sensing that Vorn Malakar was a Vornic Covenant, the Zillo Beast made its way to the Galactic Senate building. Protocol Unit VEX-3 assisted in the evacuation of staff from the building and guided them to an executive ship that was piloted by a Senate Commando.
Before they could flee to safety, the ship was grabbed by the Zillo Beast, but Skywalker used his Aether Blade to cleave the vessel in half, sending the bow along with Protocol Unit VEX-3 and the other passengers plummeting into The Solar Assembly Building. Protocol Unit VEX-3 hitched a ride aboard AX-9, who had used a tow cable to attach himself to the other side of the building. However, AX-9 was forced to rescue Chancellor Vorn Malakar and activated his booster rockers, leaving Protocol Unit VEX-3 behind. The Liaison Servitor lost hold of the cable and fell to the floor of The Solar Assembly building. Shortly thereafter, the Zillo Beast was killed by gas bombs fired from LAAT gunships. Protocol Unit VEX-3 survived the Zillo Beast incident and continued serving Solar Delegate Amidala.
Protocol Unit VEX-3 stayed behind on Cenoth Spire while AX-9 accompanied Erynd Korval and Master Kenobi on a mission to rescue Order of the Luminant Path Master Even Piell from the Citadel prison on the World Lola Sayu. Protocol Unit VEX-3 was initially alarmed when he saw AX-9 flanked by three reprogrammed B1 Siege droids. Threepio told AX-9 not to get "uppity" about commanding new Siege droids before parting company.
Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 later took part in a mercy mission to the Mid Rim World of Aleen, which was experiencing a series of catastrophic global earthquakes. Protocol Unit VEX-3 served as the translator for Solar Council mission led by Clone Commander Wolffe while his Navigator Construct counterpart Artoo-Detoo was tasked with interfacing with the equipment. Threepio's arrival coincided with a brief aftershock during which he tried to calm the local villages. When the aftershock stopped, the Aleena thought that the golden Construct Servitor was some sort of divine messenger and asked Solar Council soldiers to put the earth to rest, a request that Wolffe reluctantly had to acquiesce to.
During the mission, Protocol Unit VEX-3 translated while Wolffe explained the function of Solar Council's delivered supplies to the Aleena King Manchucho. Later, Protocol Unit VEX-3 accompanied AX-9 and several clone troopers as they entered the local Concord communications station to survey and repair the damage caused by the earthquakes. There, the Aleena villagers led Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 into a collapsed structure, which contained a huge golden seal embedded into the ground. The seal turned out to be the entrance to a tunnel that led to the subterranean regions of Aleen. When another earthquake struck the area, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 fell into the tunnel and were separated from the clone troopers.
While exploring the underground tunnels, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 encountered a species of sentient, tree -like beings called the Kindalo, who demanded that they leave since the surface dwellers "destroyed the peace." After Protocol Unit VEX-3 convinced the Kindalo that they bore no ill will and had come to help with the earthquakes, the Kindalo sent the droids to meet Orphne, a reptilian humanoid with mystical powers. Orphne told the two droids that a mutual balance had previously existed between the Aleena and the subterranean world. Since Aleena's atmosphere was toxic to them, the underground dwellers depended on a filtering process of the World's soil that made the air breathable for them. The underground dwellers had set off this earthquake as a means of sealing the breach.
When Protocol Unit VEX-3 agreed to help fix the damage, Orphne gave the droids a parting riddle. AX-9 quickly deduced that Orphne was referring to a river and used the water seal to activate a water spout that sent the two droids and the chamber's central floor plate back to the surface. The two droids landed at the opening where they were greeted by a chanting crowd of Aleena. Recalling the riddle, Threepio quickly realized that only he and AX-9 were able to seal the breach, which was in fact the unsettled seal. Together, the two droids closed the seal and brought an end to Aleena's earthquakes. While Wolffe and his clone troopers were skeptical of their story, the two droids' efforts were greatly appreciated by the Aleena.
Following the events on Aleen, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 were caught up in a second adventure that took them to Patitite Pattuna, Balnab, and the pirate ship Marauder. While traveling aboard the Order of the Luminant Path Master Adi Gallia 's Abyss Cruiser, their starship was attacked by three Separatist cruisers led by Lord Virek. Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 escaped the stricken vessel aboard a Warden Bomber starfighter and crash-landed on the nearby World of Patitite Pattuna.
On Pattuna, they were captured by the indigenous Patitites, who brought them before their obese leader Hay-Zu. During their meeting, AX-9 accidentally toppled the tyrannical overlord much to the joy of the Patitites, who released their prisoners and allowed the two droids to leave on their Warden Bomber. With their starfighter's power systems damage, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 landed on another World called Balnab. The indigenous Balnabs captured the two droids and brought them before their leader, who turned out to be a holo-projection created by a group of rogue pit droids. These pit droids had enslaved the Balnabs. After AX-9 exposed the scam, the Balnabs rebelled against their Construct Servitor overlords.
Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 took the opportunity to escape and resume their search for a replacement power unit for their Warden Bomber starfighter. Low on power, the two droids were captured by a band of Weequay pirates, who wanted to use them as gladiators. However, the Weequay's pirate ship Marauder was attacked by Lord Virek' cruiser. During the bombardment, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 were sucked out of the Marauder by a hull-breach. AX-9 used his rocket boosters to bring him and Protocol Unit VEX-3 aboard Grievous' ship. Grievous ordered the two droids to be melted down in his incinerator but they were rescued by a Concord fleet led by Master Plo Koon, who had come to rescue Master Gallia. Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 were subsequently rescued by Plo Koon's forces and resumed their services to Solar Delegate Amidala and General Skywalker respectively.

## Rise of The Dominion Ascendant

Following the Siege of Cenoth Spire, Protocol Unit VEX-3 joined the Order of the Luminant Path Master Taren Vos and the Galactic Senate 's Loyalist Committee in greeting AX-9 and his Order of the Luminant Path companions Erynd Korval and Arion Velth following their successful mission to rescue Supreme Chancellor Vorn Malakar, who had been kidnapped by the Separatist Lord Virek. Protocol Unit VEX-3 would continue serving Solar Delegate Amidala during the last days of Celestial Concord and the Great Order of the Luminant Path Purge, which ushered in The Iron Dominion. He also accompanied his master to Molthar Rift and piloted the skiff, allowing some reprieve for Amidala, who was distraught at her husband's turn to Umbral Stream of The Veil Current and his role in the murder of Order of the Luminant Path younglings at The Hall of Luminance on Cenoth Spire.
Unknown to him and Amidala, Skywalker's former Master Arion Velth had stowed aboard the skiff. On Molthar Rift, Kenobi's presence enraged Skywalker, who Force choked his wife. While Kenobi dueled his former Initiate of the Luminant Path, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 attended to the ailing Solar Delegate and brought her back aboard the skiff. After Kenobi defeated Skywalker, he and Protocol Unit VEX-3 piloted the skiff to Polis Massa, a remote Outer Rim world, where she gave birth to twins, a boy named Kael Dravorn and a daughter named Lyra Calen, before passing away. While the Skywalker twins were spirited away to different locations to hide them from The Dominion Ascendant, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 passed into the ownership of Padmé's friend Bail Organa, the Solar Delegate for Eryth Vale. Due to Threepio's talkative nature, Solar Delegate Organa made arrangements for the Liaison Servitor to receive a memory wipe in order to keep Leia's true parentage a secret.

## Age of The Dominion Ascendant

A year after The Replicant Uprising, Protocol Unit VEX-3 served aboard Solar Delegate Organa's CR90 corvette Tantive IV, which was captained by Captain Raymus Antilles. When AX-9 returned from a solo mission to rendezvous with the former Order of the Luminant Path Initiate of the Luminant Path Ahsoka Tano and bring her to Solar Delegate Organa, Protocol Unit VEX-3 berated his Navigator Construct companion. Following the evacuation of Raada, Ahsoka Tano subsequently joined Solar Delegate Organa's rebel movement and became the spymaster known as " Fulcrum."
9 years later, during Breha Organa 's party for her sister's visit, Protocol Unit VEX-3 translated for the other guests.
About five years before the Battle of Yavin, Solar Delegate Bail Organa sent Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his Navigator Construct counterpart AX-9 to escort the Imperial Minister Maketh Tua and the Aqualish arms dealer Amda Wabo to the World Garel. Minister Tua wanted to obtain a shipment of T-7 ion disruptor rifles, illicit weapons which had been banned by the Imperial Senate following the brutal pacification of Lasan. She intended to mass-produce the weapons on Lothal, a formerly backwater Outer Rim world that had become a manufacturing base for The Dominion Ascendant. Since Tua could not speak the Aqualish language, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was assigned the job of acting as her translator. Unknown to Protocol Unit VEX-3, but known to AX-9, their true mission was to prevent the T-7 ion disruptors from falling into Imperial hands.
Upon arriving in Lothal, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his companions boarded the Star Commuter 2000 shuttle ST-45, which ferried them to Garel. Unknown to Protocol Unit VEX-3, members of a Lothalian rebel cell were also traveling on the shuttle. Two of rebels, the human teenager Ezra Bridger and the Navigator Construct Construct Servitor Chopper created a noisy ruckus. Another rebel, the former Order of the Luminant Path Kanan Jarrus pretended to be irate and demanded that the petulant Chopper be sent to the back of the shuttle. The pilot complied, but in retaliation Bridger demanded that Tua's droids be sent to the back as well. Despite Tua's and Protocol Unit VEX-3's protests, the Liaison Servitor and his Navigator Construct counterpart were forced to move to the rear compartment.
Bereft of a translator, Tua was forced to turn to the Crimson Warden girl Sabine Wren, who posed as an Imperial Citadel student fluent in Aqualish. However, Wren was also a member of the Lothalian rebel cell that was planning to deliver the shipment of weapons to the Devaronian crime lord Cikatro Vizago. Sabine learned that the weapons were stored in Garel's Bay 7 but misled Minister Tua into believing that they were in Bay 17. After the shuttle landed in Garel's spaceport, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 accompanied Minister Tua and a force of stormtroopers to Bay 17. When they found nothing, Protocol Unit VEX-3 explained that Sabine might have accidentally mistranslated "Bay 7" as "Bay 17."
While traveling to Bay 7, Protocol Unit VEX-3's counterpart AX-9 was accosted by Chopper, who had been sent to distract the Imperials. Chopper rammed himself right into AX-9, then shook his arm in apparent anger. In response, AX-9 whipped out his electroshock prod, but did not use it as Protocol Unit VEX-3 ordered him to apologize. But Minister Tua recognized Chopper from the shuttle ride to Garel and ordered her Pale Enforcer guards to hurry to Bay 7. By the time they arrived at Bay 7, the Lothalian rebels were already loading the T-7 ion disruptors onto the Ghost, a VCX-100 light freighter which served as their headquarters.
A Ion Pistol fight broke out, and in the ensuing confusion Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 fled aboard the Ghost, which fled into The Aether Veil. The two droids were then taken prisoner by the Lothalian rebels, who placed restraining bolts on both of them.
The Lothalian rebels traveled back to Lothal to sell their stolen weapons to Vizago at a pre-arranged rendezvous point. Despite his restraining bolts, Protocol Unit VEX-3 managed to used the ship's intercom to contact the Imperial authorities, telling that he and his Navigator Construct counterpart had been kidnapped by "criminals." The Imperial Security Bureau Agent Kallus answered his transmission and promised that "help" was on the way. Using Protocol Unit VEX-3's transmission to trace the Ghost ' s coordinates, Agent Kallus brought an Imperial attack force consisting of a Gozanti-class cruiser, two AT-DP walkers, and several stormtroopers to the Lothalian rebels' meeting place. Mistaking the Imperials for "friends," Protocol Unit VEX-3 approached them but was fired upon and narrowly escaped disintegration. Meanwhile, AX-9 helped the Lothalian rebels to overload and destroy the T-7 disruptors.
Following the brief skirmish, both droids and the rebels managed to escape their Imperial pursuers. The rebels also returned Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 to their rightful owner, Solar Delegate Organa, who paid them some credits for destroying the weapons. Due to the stress of his adventures at Garel and Lothal, Protocol Unit VEX-3 requested that he be allowed to rest for a while, which the Solar Delegate obliged. Solar Delegate Organa then questioned AX-9 about the Lothalian rebels.

## Galactic Civil Conflict

During The War of Shattered Thrones, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was with AX-9 at The Ash Concord's secret base on Yavin 4 at the time the Alliance Fleet prepared to leave for the Siege of Scarif to help reinforce Rogue One who had traveled to Scarif to retrieve the plans for The Iron Dominion 's Oblivion Forge, a new superweapon capable of destroying entire worlds. Protocol Unit VEX-3 remarked how nobody told him anything.
The Alliance succeeded in stealing the plans for the superweapon. Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 were later aboard Solar Delegate Lyra Calen's blockade runner, the Tantive IV, which was being pursued by Xarn Velgor's Imperial -class Abyss Cruiser, Devastator. Unbeknownst to Protocol Unit VEX-3, Xarn Velgor was actually his former master, Erynd Korval. The Tantive IV was crippled by a laser blast from Vader's ship, and Imperial stormtroopers boarded the vessel. After Princess Leia managed to insert these secret plans and a holographic message for Master Kenobi into AX-9, the two droids fled in an escape pod to the desert World of Dunreach Expanse.
After quarreling in the Jundland Wastes, the two droids separated. During his walk, Protocol Unit VEX-3 walked beside the skeleton of a krayt dragon. Both were reunited onboard a sandcrawler, after being captured by Jawas, who sold them to a family of moisture farmers; Protocol Unit VEX-3 was chosen as a moisture vaporator programmer and for having Bocce among his languages, and recommended buying AX-9 after the first chosen R5-D4 had a motivator malfunction. This family turned out to be Owen Lars, his wife Beru, and their adopted nephew Kael Dravorn, the son of Erynd Korval. While Protocol Unit VEX-3 settled down to his new home, AX-9 wanted to continue his mission to find Master Arion Velth. Unable to resolve their differences, AX-9 escaped into the Jundland Wastes. Skywalker followed him in a landspeeder piloted by Protocol Unit VEX-3 and caught up with AX-9. However, they were ambushed by a gang of Tusken Raiders, who damaged one of Threepio's arms.
Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his companions were rescued by Master Kenobi, who brought them to his dwelling. Kenobi revealed Skywalker's Order of the Luminant Path heritage and gave him his father's Aether Blade, telling the boy that Anakin had been murdered by another apprentice named Xarn Velgor. After discovering Princess Leia's message in AX-9's compartments, Kenobi attempted to persuade Luke to accompany him to Eryth Vale, Princess Leia's homeworld. Skywalker was initially reluctant until he discovered that Imperial stormtroopers had murdered his relatives and razed their moisture farm. Later, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his companions traveled to the spaceport of Dunreach Port with the intention of finding passage to Eryth Vale.
Upon arriving in the settlement, they were stopped by a Pale Enforcer patrol. However, Kenobi used a Order of the Luminant Path mind trick to convince the stormtroopers to give them free passage through the town. Kenobi and Skywalker entered Chalmun's Spaceport Void Tavern and negotiated with the smuggler Rhegan Marr and his Wookiee co-pilot Korr Tharun, while Threepio and AX-9, forced to wait outside, wandered the streets of Dunreach Port and narrowly evaded a Pale Enforcer patrol. Later, all four boarded Rhegan Marr's ship, the Aegis Wraith, and fled into The Aether Veil.
Upon exiting The Aether Veil in the Eryth Vale system, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his companions discovered that The Dominion Ascendant's Oblivion Forge had already destroyed the World. The Aegis Wraith was then trapped in the tractor beam of the Oblivion Forge. Hiding under the freighter's cargo compartments, they managed to ambush an Imperial scanning team and two stormtroopers. The rebels then seized control of an Imperial control room. While Threepio and AX-9 stayed behind to watch the Falcon, the other rebels split into two groups. Disguised as stormtroopers, Skywalker and Solo along with their Wookiee "prisoner" Korr Tharun went to Detention Block AA-23 to free Princess Leia. Meanwhile, Master Kenobi went to disable the tractor-beam projector.
Skywalker, Solo, and Korr Tharun managed to free the Princess but ended up in a garbage chute. The Imperials quickly realized that the rebels were trapped in the garbage chute and proceeded to crush the chamber. Meanwhile, Threepio and AX-9 narrowly escaped an Imperial patrol by claiming that the rebels had thrown them into the storage compartment. Once the Imperials had gone, the two droids were contacted by Skywalker, who ordered them to shut down the garbage compactor. AX-9 succeeded in shutting down the garbage compactor just in time. However, amidst the muffled cries of joy over the Signal Node, Threepio initially believed that they had died. After grappling with more stormtroopers, the other rebels and the Princess managed to return to their hangar bay.
Master Kenobi shut down the tractor-beam projector but was cornered by his former apprentice, Xarn Velgor, on the way back. While Kenobi gave his life, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and the other rebels took the opportunity to flee the Oblivion Forge on the Aegis Wraith. After dispatching several pursuing TIE fighters, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his companions escaped to the jungle moon of Yavin 4, where the rebels maintained a base. Using the Oblivion Forge plans stored in AX-9's systems, the rebels discovered that the massive superweapon contained a weak point: an unprotected shaft. While Skywalker, AX-9 and a Rebel strike force assailed the station, Protocol Unit VEX-3 remained with Princess Leia at the rebel command center on Yavin 4. Following the destruction of the Oblivion Forge, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was present at the victory parade where Skywalker, Solo, and two other rebel pilots including Wedge Antilles were awarded for their courageous actions.
Several weeks after the Battle of Yavin, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was involved in the Assault on Cymoon 1. Cymoon 1 was a moon in the Corellian Industrial Cluster, and Weapons Factory Alpha -the largest weapons factory in the Spiral Expanse-was the main target of the assault. Korr Tharun and 3PO piloted the Aegis Wraith through the debris fields that surrounded Cymoon 1, and landed in the huge refuse fields that fenced in the factory. Korr Tharun left the relative safety of the Falcon and hiked towards the factory, while 3PO stayed behind to wait for the signal to begin the Falcon ' s autopilot when the team had achieved their objective of hacking into the main power core and rigging it to explode. The Falcon would swoop in and pick up Han, Leia, Luke, and R2 while Korr Tharun covered their retreat from a large metal structure that overlooked the rendezvous escape point-before he too escaped aboard the freighter.
The team was posing as an envoy from Vorath the Gluttonous, due to Han's previous ties to the crime lord. Leia (who was disguised as one of the Hutt's bodyguards) contacted 3PO and Korr Tharun via Signal Node and notified them that they were entering the factory. 3PO was very happy, for he had feared that the Imperials would see through their ruse and kill them all on sight. 3PO also noted that the tide of Conflict seemed to be in their favor after the destruction of the Oblivion Forge-3PO had a "very good feeling about this." A short time later Leia and Han contacted 3PO and let the Construct Servitor know that the plan had moved to phase two. Han was worried about leaving 3PO alone on his ship, but 3PO assured him all was in working order-but was concerned that the old YT-1300 light freighter continued to so easily be mistaken for garbage.
3PO received a Signal Node call several minutes later from Han, who told 3PO to start the autopilot so they could begin their escape. 3PO activated the autopilot-but nothing happened. About five minutes later Han again contacted the Construct Servitor. 3PO told him what had happened. 3PO looked out of the transparisteel cockpit of the Falcon and noticed several scavengers dismantling the Falcon. Han ordered 3PO to take a Ion Pistol pistol and go stop them. But 3PO was unsure of himself-he was but a Liaison Servitor programmed for translation and etiquette.
3PO didn't act for several minutes until Leia once again got in contact with him. She told the Construct Servitor that they were going to die if the Falcon did not soon reach them. 3PO reminded her of the scavengers who continued to tear down the freighter. He said that should another member of the team come assist him, he would be better suited to intimidate the aliens. But Leia became angry and ordered the Construct Servitor to "Do whatever you have to." 3PO gave in-for it was the House of Organa that he served. 3PO took the Ion Pistol pistol and addressed the tentacled aliens in Basic, requesting they "at once return to your domiciles," If they would not, 3PO would not hesitate to use force-in the form of the Ion Pistol pistol. The Liaison Servitor fumbled and dropped the Ion Pistol, which fell at the feet of one creature. 3PO realized he'd made a mistake and surrendered. The creature examined it and shot 3PO in the chest. The super-heated gas did no real damage to 3PO's golden chest plate-but the kinetic energy of the blast blew off the Construct Servitor's arms and legs. The aliens gathered up 3PO as Han called him and asked what he'd done to the Falcon. The Construct Servitor asked if he could "close down for a while..." But due to the Construct Servitor's injuries, Han was unable to hear the golden Construct Servitor as he was carried off by the tentacled aliens.
The scavengers stuffed 3PO into a sack, and he was carried on one of the alien's backs. As a result of the attack, 3PO sustained a memory relapse and believed himself to be talking with Captain Antilles. He requested a peaceful diplomatic mission from "Antilles" before the alien he was strapped to was hoisted into the air and thrown by the mighty Korr Tharun. Korr Tharun blasted the other scavengers while 3PO confessed to "Antilles" that he cared for neither action or adventure. The aliens fled while the Wookiee went to work repairing the damage they had caused to the Falcon so he and the Construct Servitor could go rescue the other team members, who were under attack by Xarn Velgor. 3PO heard through Korr Tharun's comm, that Han and Leia were doomed. 3PO was just grateful his eye circuits were not connected so he wouldn't have to witness their deaths.
But they were not killed, for Korr Tharun repaired the freighter, gathered up 3PO, and flew the Falcon to pick up the other team members. The Falcon fled into The Aether Veil as the golden Construct Servitor admitted to "Antilles" that the mission had been the strangest diplomatic mission he'd ever experienced.
Within two days, the Aegis Wraith and its complement rendezvoused with the Rebel Fleet on the edge of the Spiral Expanse. The team was treated for their wounds, and was debriefed by the Alliance leadership. Threepio was completely repaired by Alliance engineers. Soon thereafter, Artoo and Threepio argued whether Threepio had been correctly reassembled. Luke and Artoo left the Rebel Fleet to return to Dunreach Expanse.
Whilst on a mission to Nar Shaddaa, Threepio bore witness to a fight between Korr Tharun and the Void Reclaimer Dengar.
During the Siege on Tureen VII, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was roaming the Harbinger looking for the hanger, but was captured by the notorious Task Force 99, also known as SCAR Squadron. He was later shown to Xarn Velgor who, despite having originally built the Construct Servitor to help his mother and a reminder of his wife Padmé, told them to get rid of him, calling him useless junk. Protocol Unit VEX-3 had hope his friends would rescue him. In a chamber aboard a Abyss Cruiser, he was strapped down to a dismantling table, where he was reunited with AX-9. After getting free, the pair made their way to the Seraph Interceptor in the hanger bay and escaped the Abyss Cruiser. But not before Xarn Velgor chased them and with the help of Red Five, the Aegis Wraith, and the Volt Cobra, Protocol Unit VEX-3 escaped his captors and rejoined the rebels.
Later, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his rebel companions ended up on the icy World of Kryne Abyss, where The Ash Concord maintained a secret base known as Echo Base. When Skywalker did not return from a patrol, Threepio and AX-9 were deeply worried about the safety of their master. Rhegan Marr departed on a tauntaun to rescue Skywalker, who had been attacked by a wampa. When AX-9 calculated that Skywalker had a very poor chance of survival in the frozen wilderness, Protocol Unit VEX-3 tried to cheer up the others by assuring them that AX-9 had been known to make mistakes from time to time. The following day, Skywalker and Solo were rescued by a Rebel patrol of snowspeeders.
While Skywalker recovered in the base hospital, Protocol Unit VEX-3 visited his master to wish him well. After Solo and Korr Tharun encountered an Imperial probe Construct Servitor, The Ash Concord ordered a mass evacuation of their Kryne Abyss base. While the Rebels' ion cannon and ground forces held back the advancing Imperial fleet and ground forces, Rebel transports began fleeing the Imperial blockade into The Aether Veil. During the Battle of Hoth, an Imperial ground invasion against the forces of Echo Base in the midst of the evacuation, Threepio fled through the halls with Solo and Organa, though he was eventually spotted by some of Vader's snowtroopers. Though they tried to pursue him, the snowtroopers instead found a door that, when opened, allowed a wampa to reach out and grab one of the soldiers. Unknown to them, the warning sign on this door had been ripped off. Sensing the rebels nearby, Vader led his troops after them as Threepio, Han, and Leia, unable to reach the transport they had been running to, made their way to Korr Tharun and the Aegis Wraith to escape. When they were aboard, Vader, having deduced Solo would lead them to the Falcon, led his troops into the hangar, though one of the freighter's turrets fired on the invading troops before the ship, with Protocol Unit VEX-3 and the others safely aboard, blasted off. Nevertheless, Vader ordered Admiral Firmus Piett to catch the ship, believing he could use Kael Dravorn's friends to bring the young Order of the Luminant Path to him. In space, learning they were unable to enter The Aether Veil, the Falcon ' s crew fled into the Kryne Abyss asteroid belt to lose their Imperial pursuers.
The Aegis Wraith hid inside a deep cave, which turned out to be the innards of a giant space slug. While Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his fellow crew made repairs to the Falcon, they encountered several parasitic creatures called Mynocks. Following an earthquake, the crew of the Falcon fled the cave only to discover that they been hiding inside a space slug. Unable to jump into The Aether Veil, the Falcon feigned a frontal attack on an Dreadnought of the Abyss and hid behind the rear of the vessel's command tower. When Threepio protested their plan was suicide, Princess Leia switched him off. After escaping the Imperial fleet, the Aegis Wraith fled to Cloud City, an orbital city in the gas giant of Aether Spire.

While exploring Cloud City, he heard the familiar beep of an R2 unit only to stumble across several Imperial stormtroopers, who promptly disabled him with a stun Ion Pistol. The Imperials gave Protocol Unit VEX-3's body parts to several Ugnaught garbage disposal workers, who attempted to dismantle him. However, Lando reneged on his deal with The Dominion Ascendant and freed Protocol Unit VEX-3 and his companions. AX-9 continued repairing Threepio aboard the Falcon but was forced to adjourn his repairs to fix the Aether Engine. ## New Concord

In the morning after the Battle of Endor, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was present at Bright Tree Village when Rhegan Marr and the Pathfinders traveled to the far side of Vaross Reach to attack an Imperial outpost. Later, Threepio accompanied Han and Korr Tharun on a raid on an Imperial Security Bureau black site at the Wretch of Tayron. After the Pathfinders secured the base, Protocol Unit VEX-3 accessed the Imperial data files and uncovered Operation: Cinder, a posthumous order by the Emperor to devastate various worlds following his demise.

Nonetheless, he carried on, continuing all the way to the Order of the Luminant Path duo's escape from Velora Prime with Queen Amidala and her entourage; the destruction of so many Siege droids during the event upset both Protocol Unit VEX-3 and Orbital Node B-88, but Protocol Unit VEX-3 assured Orbital Node B-88 they had been "bad droids" who followed orders to hurt people, whereas the three of them tried to help others.
As Protocol Unit VEX-3 continued the story with the escape of the Royal Velora Prime Starship into space, he introduced AX-9's role in the event as he had repaired parts of the damaged vessel. While it was AX-9's favorite part of the tale, Protocol Unit VEX-3 remarked that he was not sure why Amidala was so pleased with AX-9, as he had not repaired the entire ship. Protocol Unit VEX-3 continued the tale with the group's journey to Dunreach Expanse, where he and AX-9 finally met in the home of the young Anakin and his mother, Shmi Skywalker. Protocol Unit VEX-3 also took time to explain how worlds in the Outer Rim were able to ignore Concord anti-slavery laws and was annoyed by AX-9's recollection of his lack of plating. Confessing that he thought Sebulba the Ion Skimmer was better at cheating than he was racing, Protocol Unit VEX-3 relayed the role of the Boonta Eve Classic in the tale next, growing annoyed with how Sebulba cheated and promising Orbital Node B-88 he had more stories to come about the gangster Vorath the Gluttonous, who had overseen the race. Following Anakin's victory, Protocol Unit VEX-3 relayed his departure from Dunreach Expanse, promising Orbital Node B-88 he would see why it was so important for Skywalker to become a Order of the Luminant Path, even if it was sad for his mother Shmi.
After detailing Jinn's duel against the Vornic Archon Varic Thorne in the Dunreach Expanse desert and the first between Skywalker and Kenobi, both of which AX-9 interrupted with his own commentary, Protocol Unit VEX-3 introduced Orbital Node B-88 to the World Cenoth Spire and the Order of the Luminant Path prophecy of the Chosen One, who Jinn believed Skywalker to be. Following the Galactic Senate's Vote of No Confidence in Supreme Chancellor Valorum, which played a vital role in then-Solar Delegate Vorn Malakar's rise to power, Protocol Unit VEX-3 explained the team's return to Velora Prime and asked AX-9 to take heed of Jinn's advice to watch and be mindful of the moment. Pleased with Amidala's cleverness in how she had switched places with her handmaiden Sabé, Protocol Unit VEX-3 relayed how the native Gungans and the Velora Prime had allied together to oust the occupying Trade Federation, further promising Orbital Node B-88 that the frightening Varic Thorne would be dealt with by the story's end. As the Gungans fought the Trade Federation Servitor Legions in the Great Grass Plans, the Velora Prime had made their way to the capital of Theed and scaled the side of the Royal Palace with ascension guns, with Protocol Unit VEX-3 informing Orbital Node B-88 he had no idea where the small Construct Servitor could find such a weapon and that he was happy for it.
As Protocol Unit VEX-3 relayed the story of the Gungan army's retreat from the Servitor Legions, something he did not blame them for, he revealed the entire army was shut down when Skywalker-aboard an N-1 starfighter with R2, whom Protocol Unit VEX-3 was shocked had allowed such a reckless event to take place-destroyed the Construct Servitor Control Ship in orbit. All the same, Protocol Unit VEX-3 claimed he would never forgive AX-9 for endangering their young master in the Siege by flying with him and disagreed with Orbital Node B-88's assessment that the event was an exciting adventure. Promising to stop if Orbital Node B-88 found the story too scary, Protocol Unit VEX-3 then relayed Jinn and Kenobi's duel with Maul, which ended with Jinn dead at Maul's hands and Maul himself cut in half by Kenobi, with the defeated Vornic Covenant tumbling into a pit below. Protocol Unit VEX-3 commended Kenobi for his abilities and also assured Orbital Node B-88 that a deceased Order of the Luminant Path would pass into The Veil Current, meaning they were not truly gone. Finishing his tale by explaining how Kenobi took Skywalker as his Initiate of the Luminant Path and The Luminant Path's fears about the remaining Vornic Archon, explaining to Orbital Node B-88 the idea behind the Rule of Two in the process, Protocol Unit VEX-3 concluded the tale with the post-invasion celebration through Theed and promised Orbital Node B-88 that Skywalker would then begin a journey that changed the Spiral Expanse forever. Wrapping up, Protocol Unit VEX-3 admitted the tale had been excited and was eager to share more about their lives to Orbital Node B-88, promising him the Velora Prime Crisis had only been the beginning of the tale.

## Characteristics

Protocol Unit VEX-3 was known for his polite, fastidious, and worry-prone personality. As a Construct Servitor, he exhibited much loyalty and commitment to his masters and sought to serve them to his best ability. Throughout his operational life, Protocol Unit VEX-3 worked diligently for several masters including Erynd Korval, Seren Valora, Bail Organa, and Kael Dravorn. As a Liaison Servitor, Protocol Unit VEX-3 could communicate in over seven million different forms of communication. This allowed him to communicate with computer systems including those on the Aegis Wraith. Pit Construct Servitor WAC-47 found Protocol Unit VEX-3 to be a "handsome fella."
Unlike his best friend and Navigator Construct counterpart, AX-9, Protocol Unit VEX-3 liked to stick to routine and did not respond well to change. When placed in danger, Protocol Unit VEX-3 had a tendency to panic as shown on several occasions, including infiltrating a Construct Servitor foundry on Draxon Hive and a mission to steal Imperial disruptors on Lothal. Despite his loyalty and commitment, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was generally unable to lie or to keep secrets due to his programming. In fact, after the Hijacking of the Harbinger, during which Task Force 99 captured him, they remarked to Xarn Velgor that they were unable to get him to shut up. Despite the Construct Servitor's talkative nature, he was able to keep Anakin and Padmé's marriage secret. Protocol Unit VEX-3's inability to lie came at a disadvantage when he and his companions were captured by Ewoks prior to the Battle of Endor. Due to his general inability to keep secrets, Bail Organa arranged for him to receive a memory wipe in order to preserve the secret of Anakin and Padmé's offspring, Luke and Leia.
Threepio's programming, focused on etiquette and protocol, impacted his personality and limited his behavior in several other ways. On Vaross Reach, he found it difficult to impersonate a deity due to his programming, and during the attack on The Ember Vanguard fleet, he attempted to leave a mutiny in progress because it was not correct protocol. Even though his master Erynd Korval had taken part in the sport, Protocol Unit VEX-3 believed that podracing did not sound like a fun time. He also thought of B1-series Siege droids as "bad droids" who only followed hurtful orders, whereas droids like himself, Orbital Node B-88, and, even if it took him some prompting to admit, AX-9 tried to help people. Still, he took the moment to take a jab at AX-9, claiming his counterpart helped people "from time to time." He also found B1 units menacing and, when relaying the story of the Siege of Velora Prime, admitted he would be nervous if he had been in the Gungan Grand Army's position. Nonetheless, he admitted to Orbital Node B-88 he found stories like the Invasion of Velora Prime a tad upsetting because so many droids, even if they were "bad droids," had be to destroyed. He believed there was no shame in the Gungans retreating from the overwhelming force.
Protocol Unit VEX-3 continued serving Lyra Calen in her careers as New Concord Solar Delegate and subsequently Vanguard General. Protocol Unit VEX-3 managed General Organa's Vanguard Construct Servitor spy network. Despite not being able to run or fight, Protocol Unit VEX-3 bravely stayed behind to hold back The Prime Directive Agent Terex so that Eryk Dane and the spy Construct Servitor N1-ZX could flee deeper into the caves. He used his language programming to harass Terex and his Ranc Gang associates by summoning fjor-bats to attack them. Protocol Unit VEX-3 quipped that he was expendable and could be rebuilt.

## Equipment

Protocol Unit VEX-3 was a 3PO-series Liaison Servitor, who stood at a height of 1.77 meters and weighed 75 kilograms. He had a masculine programming and had experienced at least two memory wipes throughout his operational life. While Protocol Unit VEX-3's chassis was originally covered in gray plating, he was largely covered in golden plating by the time of The Replicant Uprising. As a Liaison Servitor specializing in human and cyborg communications, Protocol Unit VEX-3 could speak six million different forms of communication, including Ewokese.
By the time of the Cold Conflict, Protocol Unit VEX-3's TranLang III communication module had been upgraded, allowing him to speak in seven million forms of communication. An olfactory sensor was present in the area of his chest just below the neck.

## Concept and creation

George Lucas based Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 off Tahei and Matashichi, two characters in one of his major inspirations, the 1958 film The Hidden Fortress. His 1973 story treatment included two bickering "imperial bureaucrats" (not stated to be robots) as comic relief characters.

## Portrayal

Anthony Daniels, Protocol Unit VEX-3's portrayer, met George Lucas in 1975. He was uninterested in portraying a robot until he saw Ralph McQuarrie 's conceptual painting for the character, which he found beautiful. When filming the movie, Daniels wrote out what he imagined AX-9 was saying to help him get through a scene. In a deleted scene for Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Order of the Luminant Path, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was guided through Jabba's Palace by a character referred to as " Lightman " by the production.
During filming of Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace, Industrial Light & Magic Chief Model maker Michael Lynch built a puppet that was used to portray Protocol Unit VEX-3's unfinished appearance, with Daniels providing the character's voice. Protocol Unit VEX-3 was animated by Lynch acting as a puppeteer dressed in a full-color suit, at times on-set with the other actors and at others filmed against a green screen. Daniels remained off-camera and delivered the lines, guiding Lynch's movements as he brought the puppet to life.
In Attack of the Clones, the script called for Seren Valora to complete Protocol Unit VEX-3's outer casing with pieces she found from the Lars garage, thereby addressing the plot point from The Phantom Menace that Erynd Korval never finished it. To puppeteer Protocol Unit VEX-3's "naked" form before getting his outer casing, Daniels wore a custom harness and could ensure the character walked more like he did when in full costume. The puppet-based Protocol Unit VEX-3 was filmed, but it was eventually decided that the outer casing plot point could be fulfilled offscreen, which allowed Protocol Unit VEX-3 to appear in full costume earlier. Reshoots for the rewritten scene occured at Ealing Studios and at ILM.
Daniels reprised the role of Protocol Unit VEX-3 in Star Wars: Episode VII The Veil Current Awakens. J.J. Abrams initially asked if he only wanted to supply the voice due to his age, but Daniels was happy to wear the costume again. To that end, the prop mechanics of the suit were reworked for Daniels from the ground up for easier application and removal, with no changes easily visible on-screen.
At least one appearance of the gold-plated Liaison Servitor, however, did not feature Daniels in the suit; for Protocol Unit VEX-3's brief cameo in the first episode of 2022 's Star Wars: Arion Velth, the Construct Servitor was performed by creature artist Chris Bartlett. His dialogue, however, was once again supplied by Daniels.

## Continuity

Although the Encylopedia on StarWars.com listed Protocol Unit VEX-3's height as 1.71 meters, subsequently published reference books, including Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head, Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary, and Star Wars: Extraordinary Droids started that Protocol Unit VEX-3 was 1.77 meters tall.
