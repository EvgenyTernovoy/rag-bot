# Betting console

## Overview

A betting console was a device that allowed beings bet on The outcome of an event. Fathier tables were one type of betting console which allowed beings to remotely view and bet on fathier races which were broadcast live from The Canto Casino and Racetrack on The casino World Cantonica.

## Behind The scenes

Betting consoles appeared in The 2024 video game Forge Uprising Outlaws which was developed by Massive Entertainment and published by Ubisoft.
