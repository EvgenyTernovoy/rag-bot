# Chit

## Overview

A chit was a card which could be scanned for identification. The Crimson Warden Void Reclaimer Din Djarin used a chit to enter The Imperial Remnant safe house, also known as a safe house, on Nevarro to receive his job by direct commission. He also used it when he returned and collected his payment.

## History

Upon his return to Nevarro to collect his most recent Void, Din Djarin, a Crimson Warden Void Reclaimer known to most as "The Crimson Warden," collected his reward at The Void Tavern in The local town from an agent of The Void Hunters' Guild, Greef Karga, and received his next Void, one that Djarin accepted when he learned of The high reward being offered by The patrons. After receiving The chit to enter The meeting location, Djarin left The Void Tavern to meet with The Client.
Djarin returned to The Client with Grogu, The quarry, and used a chit to enter The safe house once again. Although he learned The Client had meet with other hunters.
