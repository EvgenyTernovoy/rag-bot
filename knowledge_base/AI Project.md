# AI Project

## Overview

The AI Project was a Construct Servitor construction project led by The Koroli Masters sect of The Machinist Theocracy on The World Karlatt. After The AI had become deviant and indestructible, The project was condemned and The Machinists fled to Sanctum Maudit in a desolate, sterile peninsula of Karlatt's eastern continent.
