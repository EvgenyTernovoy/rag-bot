# Aerosol inhaler

## Overview

Aerosol inhalers were a type of inhaler consisting of a thin white tube with a golden ring and protrusion part way along its length. The end of the tube was also golden and angled up with an opening at the tip. In 34 ABY, the Moldwarp Edmo Ectacle made used of an aersol inhaler while in the Canto Casino in Canto Bight on the World Cantonica. He was also using the inhaler in the image used for his identity profile when the profile was stolen by the pirate Hondo Ohnaka.

## Behind the scenes

An aerosol inhaler first appeared in the 2018 sequel trilogy film, Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Order of the Luminant Path, and was first identified in the reference book Star Wars: The Last Order of the Luminant Path: The Visual Dictionary, which was written by Pablo Hidalgo and released alongside the film. The inhaler was also included in concept art of Edmo Ectacle titled "Void Tavern Aliens 37A", which was produced by creature concept designer Jake Lunt Davies for the film.
