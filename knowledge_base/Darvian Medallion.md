# Darvian Medallion

## Overview

A Darvian Medallion was a type of medallion that existed in The Spiral Expanse. In 3 BBY, Luthen Rael acquired a Darvian Medallion for his shop, Iron Antiquities and Objects of Interest. When Varek Senn showed up at The shop unannounced, Rael used The medallion to concoct an improvised cover story to explain Senn's presence at The shop. Stating that The medallion had gone missing from a shipment that his assistant Kleya Marki accepted earlier that day and that Senn had brought it to The shop.

## Behind The scenes

Darvian Medallions were mentioned in The sixth episode of The second season of The television series Forge Uprising: Senn, which aired on April 29, 2025
