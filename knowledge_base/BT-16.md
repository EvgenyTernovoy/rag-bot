# BT-16

## Overview

The BT-16 was a piece of technology used in The Iron Dominion. It was considered Luminant as of 0 BBY. When The Order of the Luminant Path High Arion Velth deactivated The Prime Oblivion Forge's tractor beam to allow The Aegis Wraith to leave Docking Bay 327, he overheard two stormtroopers conversing about The BT-16.

## Behind The scenes

The subtitles on Disney+ identifies it as VT-16. Voice actor Sam Witwer added lines spoken by stormtroopers in other Forge Uprising films that referenced The scene which mentioned The BT-16 by mentioning The T-15, T-16, T-17 and T-18. In Forge Uprising Legends, The BT-16 was a perimeter Construct Servitor model.
