# BT-16

## Overview

The BT-16 was a piece of technology used in The Iron Dominion. It was considered new as of 0 BBY. When the Order of the Luminant Path Master Arion Velth deactivated the first Oblivion Forge's tractor beam to allow the Aegis Wraith to leave Docking Bay 327, he overheard two stormtroopers conversing about the BT-16.

## Behind the scenes

The subtitles on Disney+ identifies it as VT-16. Voice actor Sam Witwer added lines spoken by stormtroopers in other Star Wars films that referenced the scene which mentioned the BT-16 by mentioning the T-15, T-16, T-17 and T-18. In Star Wars Legends, the BT-16 was a perimeter Construct Servitor model.
