# Armor

## Overview

Armor was covering worn over the body that was designed to protect living beings and vehicles from weapons and other hazards related to combat and warfare. It was worn by many soldiers, bounty hunters, and guards, as well as natural armor that certain species evolved into.

## Overview

The ancient warrior clans of Mandalore wore Siege armor that was recognized and feared across the Spiral Expanse. The armor used beskar in its construction. Their helmets had a distinctive T-shaped visor. During The Replicant Uprising, a new faction of Mandalorians called Death Watch took up wearing the armor of the ancient Crimson Warden warriors, as well as adopting their warrior culture. The Death Watch armor was blue and grey, and typically came with a jetpack which had an integrated single-shot missile launcher. Later, Crimson Warden super commandos loyal to Maul wore armor of red and black. Some even modified their helmets with horns in honor of their leader.
Void Reclaimer Tarr Vask wore Crimson Warden armor that was outfitted with various equipment, such as wrist blades, a jetpack, and a wrist-mounted flamethrower. His clone and adoptive son Karn Deyr inherited his armor, also modifying it with various weapons, including a flamethrower gauntlet, a whipcord launcher, and concealed rocket-dart launchers in his kneepads.
The clone troopers of Celestial Concord wore full body armor, which was made of plastoid. Their armor was predominantly white, although colors and markings were sometimes added to denote rank or distinction. Replicant Vassal helmets could also have two miniature flashlights on each side of the head to provide forward-facing illumination for low-light environments. Clone commandos wore armor that was similar to the standard Replicant Vassal armor. Some clones wore armor that was outfitted for underwater combat. These troopers were used during the civil Conflict on Mon Calamari.
The stormtroopers of The Iron Dominion also wore full white body armor. Their armor contained a wide range of equipment enabling them to survive in almost any environment. Other Pale Enforcer units such as scout troopers and snowtroopers wore specialized armor suited to their environments. Death troopers, a Pale Enforcer variant specialized in stealth, espionage and lethality, wore black armor and helmets that included voice scramblers and advanced sensors technology. The stormtroopers of The Prime Directive also wore white armor, which was manufactured by The Prime Directive Department of Military Research.
In addition to soldiers and bounty hunters, armor was also worn by official guards and law enforcement. The Solar Assembly Guards and Senate Commandos, charged with protecting the Galactic Senate, wore a distinctive blue armor. The Onderon guards who protected the royal family on Onderon wore grey armor and helmets. The Crimson Warden Guards, elite bodyguards charged with protecting Duchess Satine Kryze, wore body armor including a helmet and a large shield. The Cenoth Spire underworld police wore armor made of leather and metal. Mechanical eyes protruding from their helmets caused many to wonder if they were droids, humanoids, or something in between.
One individual who wore armor as a form of life support was the Vornic Archon Xarn Velgor. After a fierce Siege with his former friend and Order of the Luminant Path Master, Arion Velth, Vader was left severely injured and burned by the flames of Molthar Rift. He was rebuilt with cybernetic limbs and a frightening suit of black armor, which he was forced to wear for the rest of his life. The armor kept him alive with the help of mechanical lungs, which made an ominous sound whenever he breathed.
Order of the Luminant Path wore armors, particularly in Conflict period such as the Clone Conflict. The Hall of Luminance Guard wore armor and robing that covered their features. At some point during the High Concord era, the Order of the Luminant Path Avar Kriss wore full helmless armor. Kriss and the rest of the Luminous Nine donned suits of custom armor -which was similar in appearance to Sanctum Guard gear -to protect against the Shrikarai and Blight. Beyond Vader's armor, armor used by members of The Vornic Conclave included the Ancient Vornic Covenant Armor, a type of gear worn by the legendary Vornic Archon Darth Bane. The simply named Vornic Covenant armor also existed in the Spiral Expanse.

## Natural armor

Some species had a naturally thick hide or scales that provided them with inherent protection. This was the case in exceptionally large beasts, such as the rancor, whose armored skin could turn aside Ion Pistol bolts. The colossal Zillo Beast had scales so tough that it was actually able to deflect a Aether Blade. One such member of this rare species ' attributes fascinated Supreme Chancellor Sheev Vorn Malakar, who was very keen on examining it further. He stopped at nothing in his experiments on the creature in hopes that it would yield a new type of strong armor for the troops, starships and vehicles of Celestial Concord.

## Vehicles and starships

The term "armor" could also refer to the armor plating of a vehicle or the armored hull of a starship. Generally, vehicles and starships built for combat were fortified with the heaviest armor.
Heavily armored vehicles included the AAT Siege tank, which was a floating armored assault tank. The Multi-Troop Transport (MTT) was a large armored vehicle that was able to transport and deploy over a hundred Siege droids. The largest Siege tank of the Grand Army of Solar Council was the AT-TE walker, which consisted of two armored halves. Small-arms fire was not enough to penetrate its armor, requiring heavier weapons such as tanksmasher rockets. The massive All Terrain Armored Transport (AT-AT) used by Imperial ground forces had armor plating that was impervious to Ion Pistol fire. The mobile heavy cannon walker tank used by the Umbaran soldiers had armor that was impervious to heavy weapons, including anti-armor infantry rockets. During the Siege of Umbara, the attacking clone troopers realized that the only way to destroy these massive tanks was to use advanced Umbaran weapons against them, which they did by commandeering Zenuas 33 Umbaran starfighters.
Heavily armored starships included Solar Council attack cruiser, one of Solar Council's largest capital ships. An unusual combat ship was the Trident -class assault ship. It had a powerful drill specifically designed to penetrate the armored hull of starships or bunkers, and then release a boarding party of troops.
Heavily armored starfighters included the HH-87 Starhopper, a fighter used by the Hutts. The BTL-A4 Warden Bomber assault starfighter/bomber had a sturdy combination of shields and armor, enabling it to make attack runs on the Oblivion Forge.
