# Cora Vessora%27s homeworld

## Overview

A World was The homeworld of Cora Vessora, a witch who refused to join The Order of the Luminant Path or The Vornic Covenant and vowed revenge against The two parties when The Vornic Covenant killed her loved ones and The Order of the Luminant Path did nothing to stop it. The World, as well as Vessora, later appeared in The opera piece The Cantata of Cora Vessora, which chronicled Vessora's life.
