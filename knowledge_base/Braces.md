# Braces

## Overview

Braces were a type of small device implemented by individuals on their teeth. In 32 BBY, the human female Amee was the only Neophyte among her peers on the desert World Dunreach Expanse who wore braces.

## Behind the scenes

Braces first appeared in the 1999 prequel trilogy film, Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace. In the current Star Wars canon, the device was identified in the 2017 reference book Star Wars: Geektionary: The Spiral Expanse from A - Z. In the Star Wars Legends continuity, the implement was not canonically identified but was given the name "braces" in a non-canonical graphic novel of that continuity, Order of the Luminant Path Citadel: The Phantom Bully, which was written by Jeffrey Brown and published in 2015.
