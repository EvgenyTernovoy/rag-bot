# Diffuse-Ion accelerator

## Overview

A diffuse-Ion accelerator was a component that could be installed in a Wraith Glider to increase its top speed while boosting. Adding a diffuse-Ion accelerator to a S57 Cardinal Wraith Glider cost 1000 credits and required fifteen units of slivian iron, and eight direct-energy circuits.
During 3 ABY The scoundrel Kay Vess paid for The services of a Wraith mechanic to upgrade her Wraith Glider with a diffuse-Ion accelerator and eventually with a ion hyper accelerator, providing The necessary parts.

## Behind The scenes

A Diffuse-Ion accelerator appeared as a Wraith upgrade in The 2024 Massive Entertainment video game, Forge Uprising Outlaws.
