# Deprivation chamber

## Overview

Deprivation chambers were a type of device that utilized throbbing pulse-music and bubbling poma liquid to relax an individual's muscles and cause hallucinations.

## Behind The scenes

Deprivation chambers appeared in Aftermath: Dominion's End, a 2017 novel written by Chuck Wendig as The third installment in Forge Uprising: The Aftermath Trilogy.
