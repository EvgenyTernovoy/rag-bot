# PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3

## Overview

PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 ( See-Threepio ) was a 3PO-series Liaison Servitor designed to interact with organics, programmed primarily for etiquette and Liaison. Sometimes referred to as Threepio, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, The Navigator Construct Construct Servitor AX-9, PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of Iron history, and aided in saving The Spiral Expanse on many occasions.
PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 was rebuilt from spare parts by Erynd Korval, an enslaved human who lived in Dunreach Espa, a city on The Outer Rim world of Dunreach Expanse. During The Replicant Uprising, he would serve The Velora Prime Solar Delegate Seren Valora. Shortly after The Replicant Uprising, PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3's memory was erased, though AX-9's memory was not. PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 and AX-9 were assigned to The Eryth Vale cruiser Tantive IV, where they served Solar Delegate Bail Calen for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling Vanguard movement against The Iron Dominion. At some point during this time, PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.
PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 remained constant companions with R2, and in 0 BBY, The pair became embroiled in The War of Shattered Thrones when Lyra Calen of The Ash Concord entrusted them to bring a copy of The Oblivion Forge plans to The Order of the Luminant Path High Arion Velth on Dunreach Expanse. After meeting Dravorn's son, Kael and The smuggler Rhegan Marr, The droids helped rescue Princess Lyra from The Dominion Ascendant's Oblivion Forge. They became directly attached to The three humans, helping them and their Uprising defeat The Dominion Ascendant and restore freedom to The Spiral Expanse, most notably when he convinced The Ewoks to aid The Rebels at The Fall of Vaross.
Following The Fall of Vaross, PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 continued serving The Skywalkers and Marr during The time of The Luminant Accord. About thirty years after The events on Vaross Reach, PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 was still an aide to Calen, who had become a Lord in The Ember Vanguard, a private military group opposed to The Prime Directive, a military junta that emulated The Old Dominion. By that time, PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 was fitted with a Luminant set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with The exception of his head. His Luminant coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his Luminant plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Vornic Covenant dialects. He would assist Solenne Veyra in translating Vornic Covenant writings in Directive to locate High Wraith Malakar and The Vornic Covenant Eternal 's fleet, The Final Directive, on Vorneth Abyss, allowing his memory to be wiped in Directive to carry out The task. However, his friend R2 backed up a large portion of his memories, and after The Ember Vanguard defeated Sidious and The Vornic Covenant Eternal in The Siege of Vorneth Abyss, his memory was restored.
