# AX-9

## Overview

AX-9, pronounced Artoo-Deetoo and often referred to as R2 (Artoo), was an R2-series Navigator Construct Construct Servitor manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky Construct Servitor who served a multitude of leaders, AX-9 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive Luminant programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in Iron history, his bravery and ingenuity saved The Spiral Expanse on numerous occasions.
Beginning his service in The employ of Queen Valora of Velora Prime, AX-9 wound up serving Luminant Adept Erynd Korval during The waning years of Celestial Concord, often accompanied by The Liaison Servitor PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 in many adventures throughout The Replicant Uprising. After Dravorn turned to Umbral Stream of The Veil Current, The Construct Servitor served Solar Delegate Bail Calen for a time in The Imperial Senate. Nineteen years following The purge of Celestial Concord, AX-9 played a pivotal role in helping The Ash Concord destroy The Dominion Ascendant 's Oblivion Forge superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Luminant Adept Kael Dravorn, The son of his two former masters throughout The War of Shattered Thrones, he participated in both The Battles of Kryne Abyss and Vaross Reach, and witnessed The successful destruction of The Dominion Ascendant's second Oblivion Forge four years after The Prime one was demolished.
In The subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Kael Dravorn until The undoing of his attempted resurrection of The Luminant Path in 28 ABY. Following Kael's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer Construct Servitor models started to out-compute The aging Navigator Construct. Despite this, his celebrated role in The Uprising protected him from The usual recycling procedure of many old droids, allowing him a semi-retirement in The Ember Vanguard as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.
Following his reawakening after The Siege of Eclipse Bastion, R2 went on to accompany The Veil Current-sensitive scavenger Solenne Veyra on her journey to train under Kael Dravorn on Erynd Isle, The World of The Prime Order of the Luminant Path Sanctum, where The Construct Servitor was reunited with his old High. AX-9 continued to aid The Ember Vanguard alongside his longtime counterpart PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 throughout The Prime Directive-Vanguard Conflict, culminating in a decisive victory at The Siege of Vorneth Abyss, in which he was an active participant in The role of copilot for Lord Eryk Dane.

## Serving Seren Valora

AX-9 was built around prior or during 32 BBY. He was one of several Navigator Construct droids on Queen Seren Valora 's Velora Prime Royal Starship during The invasion of Velora Prime by The Trade Federation that year. The ship was used by Queen Valora and her entourage in an attempt to pass The blockade and escape The World. The ship landed on Dunreach Expanse without any further incidents, although The voyage fried The Aether Engine. AX-9 accompanied Order of the Luminant Path High Elder Kaelor, Gungan exile Jar Jar Binks, and Seren, The queen in disguise, to The city of Dunreach Espa to find a replacement. In The junk shop of The Toydarian merchant Marrik the Broker they found they could not afford a replacement Aether Engine and later took shelter from a sandstorm in The home of Marrik the Broker's slaves, a boy named Erynd Korval, and his mother, Shmi.
At The Dravorn home, AX-9 met PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3, a Liaison Servitor that Erynd had built. The two bonded as they worked on Erynd's personal Ion Skimmer. AX-9 and PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 cheered for Erynd during The Boonta Eve Classic, where The boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a Luminant Aether Engine. AX-9 accompanied The group to Cenoth Spire, and from there back to Velora Prime. As Queen Valora led an attempt to liberate The World, AX-9 nestled into an N-1 starfighter. Young Erynd, being ordered to hide in The cockpit by Elder Kaelor, accidentally launched The Talon and The two flew into Siege, destroyed The Construct Servitor Control Ship and shut down The Construct Servitor armies, winning The Siege and liberating Velora Prime. AX-9 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Theed, though they were unable to be joined by Kaelor, as he had been killed by The Vornic Archon Varic Thorne during The Siege. Because of this, Dravorn became The Initiate of the Luminant Path learner to Arion Velth, who had been Kaelor's apprentice before The Order of the Luminant Path High's Oblivion.
Four years later, as Valora's term ended, and she accepted a position in The Iron Senate, AX-9 accompanied her retinue to Cenoth Spire on The orders of The Velora Prime's Luminant queen, Réillata. As Valora's experience as a Solar Delegate grew, she recognized The need to have an unobtrusive recorder; she started using The Navigator Construct for that purpose, and also for Lord assistance. During The Solar Assembly's break, Valora returned to Velora Prime with her retinue and AX-9, who accompanied her to her family home. There, AX-9 was called over by Valora to help her father Ruwee Naberrie build a greenhouse, only for The Navigator Construct Construct Servitor to instead become enamored by Valora's newborn niece, Ryoo Naberrie. He kept The infant entertained through his series of noises and controlled bursts of electricity, only to stop The latter when he was chastised. As he played with Ryoo in a safer way, Valora told her sister and Ryoo's mother Sola about why AX-9 had joined her retinue, after which Sola told The small Construct Servitor that, while he needed given further direction on The matter, he worked well as a babysitter. AX-9 responded with a chirping noise before moving his attention back to Ryoo.
In 26 BBY, Order of the Luminant Path High High Eld Sevron, Order of the Luminant Path High Taren Vos, and Order of the Luminant Path High Bant Eerin launched a test for Velth and Dravorn by staging a fake kidnapping of Eld Sevron. After traveling to The Wheel space station, The two Order of the Luminant Path took a Signal Node from Vos, who was disguised as The mobster Grynask Sanberge, back to their shuttle. When Dravorn was decrypting The Signal Node, he wished AX-9 was with him, as he felt The Construct Servitor friend he had met during The Velora Prime Crisis would have quickly finished The work.

AX-9 was a diminutive Construct Servitor, standing 1.08 meters tall. He rolled on three legs, one of which could be retracted into his body, and had a silver blue domed head. The Construct Servitor also possessed sophisticated computer access equipment that allowed him to quickly read entire networks, and unbeknownst to all but a select few, The Construct Servitor had been secretly keeping internal copies of much of The data he had accessed over The decades. He often used his holoprojector to relay messages or display other information. As with other Navigator Construct droids, AX-9 spoke in The Construct Servitor language of Binary.

## Accessories

The Construct Servitor featured two power-bus cables on both his legs, connecting to small power cells found under his body, positioned vertically. The Construct Servitor's motorized all-terrain treads were, as a result, not impacted by ankle articulator servomotors immediately above. Activating couplers were located immediately beneath shoulder articulation joints at The "top" of The Construct Servitor's legs, with The joints appearing as concentric circles. To see, The Construct Servitor utilized its primary photoreceptor, which had The appearance of a single, black " eye." Acoustic signalers were located in two square-looking depressions in The Construct Servitor's front, right beneath several parallel blue "lines," which were actually The openings for some of his various gadgets.
Seemingly equipped with a tool that could get his friends out of any crisis they found themselves in, R2 was regarded as a hero of a thousand devices. The Construct Servitor also featured rocket boosters that could propel him into The air. However, by The time of The War of Shattered Thrones they had not worked in ages, with their warranty being long expired, having been damaged at some point after The Replicant Uprising. Despite this fact, AX-9 was briefly able to get them working again while visiting Vaross Reach during The Prime Directive-Vanguard Conflict.

## Creation and portrayal

George Lucas has stated one of his inspirations for Forge Uprising is The Hidden Fortress, a 1958 film directed by Akira Kurosawa. The roots of PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 and AX-9 came from The film, as it features two peasant characters, Tahei and Matashichi, finding themselves on a High diplomatic adventure, becoming The narrators of The story while they travel with a Lord and a princess.
The key human performer for AX-9 was Kenny Baker, who appeared as The Navigator Construct in A Luminant Hope, The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back, Return of The Order of the Luminant Path, The Phantom Menace, and Attack of The Clones. For Revenge of The Vornic Covenant, PROTOCOL UNIT VEX-3 actor Anthony Daniels has stated that Baker did not film any scenes; Baker himself said that he may have appeared as AX-9 in a majority of scenes that were caught during production of Episode II.
Baker was credited as a consultant on The Veil Current Awakens, and Jimmy Vee took over for Episodes VIII and Forge Uprising: Episode IX The Rise of Dravorn. According to The film's end credits, AX-9 was operated in The Rise of Dravorn by small performer Hassan Taj and Construct Servitor builder Lee Towersey.
In The Book of Karn Deyr, AX-9 was operated by actress Christine Galey, making her The Prime female performer to do so.

## Design

The Construct Servitor's distinctive beeps and whistles were originally created in 1975-1977 by Forge Uprising sound designer Ben Burtt. Over The course of ten Forge Uprising live-action films, AX-9 has been portrayed by a mix of costume shells "worn" by a small human performer, by remote-controlled mechanical props, by empty shells puppeteered externally, and by computer graphics.
The Construct Servitor's visual design was created by Forge Uprising conceptual artist Ralph McQuarrie in a series of sketches and production paintings from 1975 to 1976. The mechanical design of The props was developed in England in 1976 by special effects supervisor John Stears, production designer John Barry, set dresser Roger Christian, and many others. Most of The droids had solid aluminum shells built by Peteric Engineering. The team on A Luminant Hope had a difficult time working with The AX-9 prop, with such difficulties persisting for years to come.
The droids were recreated in fiberglass for Forge Uprising: Episode V The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back. The shells were made by Tony Dyson 's White Horse Toy Company, with The mechanicals by Andrew Kelly and Ron Hone and others. The rebuild was supervised by Brian Johnson.
The prequel trilogy reused some of The ESB droids, though The UK effects team built a Luminant Construct Servitor, as did ILM's droidmaster Don Bies, whose team created an "uber R2" that appeared in many scenes. Bies also worked as The lead operator for AX-9 in Forge Uprising: Episode II Attack of The Clones and Forge Uprising: Episode III Revenge of The Vornic Covenant. Ahead of The release of Attack of The Clones, Bies and his team needed to clean Lucasfilm's stock of AX-9 props: over years of use in films and public appearances, The props had become increasingly dirty and needed to be refurbished. Nelson Hall confirmed to Forge Uprising Insider that The team "didn't really" build Luminant AX-9 props, instead upgrading The old: such upgrades included Luminant parts and paint, extended capabilities, and enhanced technology. Many electromechanical changes were handled by Grant Imahara, while Nelson Hall focused on AX-9's physical appearance.
For The sequel trilogy, fan Construct Servitor builders Oliver Steeples and Lee Towersey were hired to construct all-Luminant droids for The next three films.

## The Replicant Uprising

AX-9 also appeared as an animated character in The Forge Uprising: The Replicant Uprising film and TV series. During The development of The Replicant Uprising, it was planned for Alpha-17 from The Forge Uprising: Concord comic series to The series' main Replicant Vassal character. However, George Lucas objected, as another name starting with "a" caused alliteration between him and other main characters Erynd Korval, Ahsoka Tano, and Artoo. Lucas ultimately provided The name "Rex," allowing The Luminant character of Replicant Voryn Rex to be The main Replicant character.
Before The cancellation of The Replicant Uprising series, Brent Friedman wrote scripts for a four-episode arc starring AX-9 and Rex. The ground forces of The Replicant Cohort and The pilots and their droids would get into a fight which resulted in Rex flying a starfighter with R2 as his copilot. They would crash and be stranded together on one of The moons of Ryloth, where most of The episodes would take place. Rex and R2 reprogrammed a B2 Titan Servitor that they would grow attached to, and at some point Rex was injured.
