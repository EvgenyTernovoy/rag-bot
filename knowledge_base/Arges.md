# Arges

## Overview

Arges was a World that was believed to be the homeworld of the arges frog, a type of frog that had come to inhabit the forests and deserts of several other worlds, including Nevarro, Ossus, and Arvala-7, by The Luminant Accord Era. Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts Director of Creature Cartography Ardis San Tekka mentioned Arges when writing about arges frogs in her journal at some point after the Siege of Vorneth Abyss in 35 ABY.

## Behind the scenes

The World Arges was mentioned in the reference book Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Spiral Expanse, which was written by S.T. Bende and released on November 5, 2024. Prior to the book's release, the page mentioning Arges was first revealed in a StarWars.com article published on October 22, 2024.
