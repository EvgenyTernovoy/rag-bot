# AX-9

## Overview

AX-9, pronounced Artoo-Deetoo and often referred to as R2 (Artoo), was an R2-series Navigator Construct Construct Servitor manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky Construct Servitor who served a multitude of leaders, AX-9 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the Spiral Expanse on numerous occasions.
Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Amidala of Velora Prime, AX-9 wound up serving Luminant Adept Erynd Korval during the waning years of Celestial Concord, often accompanied by the Liaison Servitor Protocol Unit VEX-3 in many adventures throughout The Replicant Uprising. After Skywalker turned to Umbral Stream of The Veil Current, the Construct Servitor served Solar Delegate Bail Organa for a time in the Imperial Senate. Nineteen years following the purge of Celestial Concord, AX-9 played a pivotal role in helping The Ash Concord destroy The Dominion Ascendant 's Oblivion Forge superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Luminant Adept Kael Dravorn, the son of his two former masters throughout The War of Shattered Thrones, he participated in both the Battles of Kryne Abyss and Vaross Reach, and witnessed the successful destruction of The Dominion Ascendant's second Oblivion Forge four years after the first one was demolished.
In the subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Kael Dravorn until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of The Luminant Path in 28 ABY. Following Luke's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer Construct Servitor models started to out-compute the aging Navigator Construct. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Uprising protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old droids, allowing him a semi-retirement in The Ember Vanguard as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.
Following his reawakening after the Siege of Eclipse Bastion, R2 went on to accompany The Veil Current-sensitive scavenger Solenne Veyra on her journey to train under Kael Dravorn on Erynd Isle, the World of the First Order of the Luminant Path Sanctum, where the Construct Servitor was reunited with his old master. AX-9 continued to aid The Ember Vanguard alongside his longtime counterpart Protocol Unit VEX-3 throughout The Prime Directive-Vanguard Conflict, culminating in a decisive victory at the Siege of Vorneth Abyss, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Eryk Dane.

## Serving Seren Valora

AX-9 was built around prior or during 32 BBY. He was one of several Navigator Construct droids on Queen Seren Valora 's Velora Prime Royal Starship during the invasion of Velora Prime by the Trade Federation that year. The ship was used by Queen Amidala and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the World. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Trade Federation battleship. The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only AX-9 survived - the rest were pulverized by stray Ion Pistol fire. AX-9 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into The Aether Veil.
As the ship traveled to Dunreach Expanse, AX-9 was taken before Sabé, the Queen's decoy in the guise of Amidala, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Dunreach Expanse without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the Aether Engine. AX-9 accompanied Order of the Luminant Path Master Elder Kaelor, Gungan exile Jar Jar Binks, and Padmé, the queen in disguise, to the city of Mos Espa to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Toydarian merchant Marrik the Broker they found they could not afford a replacement Aether Engine and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Marrik the Broker's slaves, a boy named Erynd Korval, and his mother, Shmi.
At the Skywalker home, AX-9 met Protocol Unit VEX-3, a Liaison Servitor that Anakin had built. The two bonded as they worked on Anakin's personal Ion Skimmer. AX-9 and Protocol Unit VEX-3 cheered for Anakin during the Boonta Eve Classic, where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new Aether Engine. AX-9 accompanied the group to Cenoth Spire, and from there back to Velora Prime. As Queen Amidala led an attempt to liberate the World, AX-9 nestled into an N-1 starfighter. Young Anakin, being ordered to hide in the cockpit by Elder Kaelor, accidentally launched the fighter and the two flew into Siege, destroyed the Construct Servitor Control Ship and shut down the Construct Servitor armies, winning the Siege and liberating Velora Prime. AX-9 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Theed, though they were unable to be joined by Jinn, as he had been killed by the Vornic Archon Varic Thorne during the Siege. Because of this, Skywalker became the Initiate of the Luminant Path learner to Arion Velth, who had been Jinn's apprentice before the Order of the Luminant Path Master's death.
Four years later, as Amidala's term ended, and she accepted a position in the Galactic Senate, AX-9 accompanied her retinue to Cenoth Spire on the orders of the Velora Prime's new queen, Réillata. As Amidala's experience as a Solar Delegate grew, she recognized the need to have an unobtrusive recorder; she started using the Navigator Construct for that purpose, and also for general assistance. During The Solar Assembly's break, Amidala returned to Velora Prime with her retinue and AX-9, who accompanied her to her family home. There, AX-9 was called over by Amidala to help her father Ruwee Naberrie build a greenhouse, only for the Navigator Construct Construct Servitor to instead become enamored by Amidala's newborn niece, Ryoo Naberrie. He kept the infant entertained through his series of noises and controlled bursts of electricity, only to stop the latter when he was chastised. As he played with Ryoo in a safer way, Amidala told her sister and Ryoo's mother Sola about why AX-9 had joined her retinue, after which Sola told the small Construct Servitor that, while he needed given further direction on the matter, he worked well as a babysitter. AX-9 responded with a chirping noise before moving his attention back to Ryoo.
In 26 BBY, Order of the Luminant Path Grand Master Eld Sevron, Order of the Luminant Path Master Taren Vos, and Order of the Luminant Path Master Bant Eerin launched a test for Kenobi and Skywalker by staging a fake kidnapping of Eld Sevron. After traveling to the Wheel space station, the two Order of the Luminant Path took a Signal Node from Windu, who was disguised as the mobster Grynask Sanberge, back to their shuttle. When Skywalker was decrypting the Signal Node, he wished AX-9 was with him, as he felt the Construct Servitor friend he had met during the Velora Prime Crisis would have quickly finished the work.

## The Replicant Uprising

AX-9 accompanied Amidala to Cenoth Spire prior to The Solar Assembly 's vote on the Military Creation Act. After an assassination attempt on Amidala took the life of her handmaiden and decoy, Cordé, Arion Velth and Erynd Korval were assigned to be her Order of the Luminant Path protectors. That night, another assassination attempt was carried out. A pair of kouhuns narrowly missed killing Amidala and although one assassin, Zam Wesell, was neutralized, another escaped.
For her own protection, Padmé was sent home to Velora Prime, with AX-9 and Skywalker accompanying her. The three later traveled to Dunreach Expanse, where they were reunited with Protocol Unit VEX-3, and then to Draxon Hive to rescue Kenobi. As they explored a massive Construct Servitor factory, AX-9 had to save Amidala from being killed by molten metal while Protocol Unit VEX-3 had his head switched with a War Servitor 's. R2 was later able to restore his companion during the Siege of Draxon Hive. After the Siege, AX-9 and Protocol Unit VEX-3 were the sole witnesses to the marriage of Erynd Korval and Seren Valora in Lake Country. After this Padmé gave ownership of R2 to Anakin while she kept 3PO.
Following the outbreak of The Replicant Uprising, AX-9 accompanied Skywalker and Master Kenobi - who had both been promoted to the position of Order of the Luminant Path General - during the defense of the World Christophsis against a besieging Separatist army led by Admiral Trench. When Clone Commander Cody and Clone Captain Rex discovered a spy among their number, AX-9 helped to ferret out the traitor by monitoring his transmissions. The Construct Servitor's work led to the arrest of Clone Sergeant Slick, who was identified as the traitor. During the subsequent Siege, the Order of the Luminant Path and Clones managed to defeat the Separatist army by sabotaging their shield generator, which exposed them to Concord artillery. Solar Council forces were also aided by the arrival of Ahsoka Tano, Skywalker's newly designated Togruta Initiate of the Luminant Path.
Following the defense of Christophsis, AX-9, along with General Skywalker and Initiate of the Luminant Path Tano, took part in a mission to rescue Rotta, the son of the Hutt crime lord Jabba. While Skywalker and Tano laid siege to the Separatist-held B'omarr Order Monastery on Teth where Rotta was being held, AX-9 sat in Skywalker's starfighter and maintained contact with General Kenobi. Skywalker's starfighter was hit when Separatist reinforcements arrived, but the Construct Servitor managed to eject from the vehicle moments before it exploded. Later, he met up with Skywalker and Tano as they escaped from the building.
On the way, they encountered Asajj Ventress, a dark-side agent of the Separatist leader Kaedran Vale, and several B2-series super Siege droids. Before a skirmish could occur, AX-9 activated a trapdoor which sent everyone plunging down a chute into an arena. During the skirmish, AX-9 assailed a second group of B2 Siege droids, causing them to fall into the arena. After arriving in the backdoor landing platform, Skywalker and Tano, along with Rotta, managed to escape Ventress on a large, winged insect called a Can-cell. Artoo followed behind with his booster rockets. The group then left Teth on Skywalker's ship, the Twilight.
Unable to rendezvous with the Abyss Cruiser, AX-9 and his companions were forced to travel to Dunreach Expanse and deliver the Huttlet directly to his father, who had a desert palace there. AX-9 programmed the coordinates into the ship's navicomputer, and it jumped to The Aether Veil. Arriving above Dunreach Expanse, the Twilight was attacked by Separatist vulture droids and crash-landed on the Dune Sea. AX-9 and his companions were forced to continue the journey to Jabba's palace on foot. While Skywalker confronted Kaedran Vale, Artoo and Tano continued the journey to Jabba's palace. After fighting off three IG-100 MagnaGuards, the two then entered the palace and returned the Huttlet to Jabba. At that moment, a holo-transmission from Solar Delegate Amidala on Cenoth Spire identified Jabba's uncle Ziro as the instigator behind the kidnapping, which placated an angry Jabba. As a result, Solar Council was able to conclude a treaty with Jabba.
AX-9, along with Erynd Korval and Arion Velth, later took part in the hunt for Lord Virek' flagship - the Subjugator -class heavy cruiser Malevolence - which had destroyed several Concord warships. After a prolonged hunt, Skywalker's Venator -class Abyss Cruiser Resolute prepared to destroy the Malevolence near the Kaliida Nebula. However, their mission was complicated by the arrival of Solar Delegate Seren Valora's yacht, which had been lured there by disinformation. Unwilling to endanger his wife, Skywalker, accompanied by AX-9 and Kenobi, boarded the Malevolence to rescue her.
During their mission aboard the Malevolence, AX-9 remained at the airlock entrance where he shut down the rail jet hovertrain under orders from Skywalker. He then helped locate his old friend Protocol Unit VEX-3, who had arrived with Amidala and gotten lost aboard the massive warship. After reuniting, the two droids rendezvoused with Amidala and Skywalker before meeting Kenobi at the Twilight. After destroying the Malevolence by sabotaging its Aether Engine, they fled back to the Resolute. Despite their victory, Lord Virek escaped the destruction of the Malevolence.
During the Separatist assault on Bothawui, AX-9 flew in the Construct Servitor socket of General Skywalker's starfighter. While pursuing Lord Virek' starfighter, Skywalker and AX-9's starfighter was hit by a piece of shrapnel. Skywalker was rescued by Concord forces but Artoo was recovered by the Trandoshan scavenger Gha Nachkt, who sold him to Lord Virek. Nachkt traveled to Grievous' secret listening post Skytop Station, which lay in the Ruusan system. Knowing Artoo's relationship with Skywalker, the Separatist military leader ordered Nachkt to disassemble the Navigator Construct Construct Servitor and extract all information on Solar Council's military strategies from him.
During his captivity, AX-9 had managed to broadcast an emergency transmission to Skywalker, who traveled to Skytop Station and rescued him. Artoo also encountered Skywalker's new replacement Navigator Construct R3-S6, who was a secret Confederate spy. While traveling to the hangar bay where Skywalker's ship Twilight was docking, R3-S6 showed his true colors by shutting the hangar doors and activating several Vulture droids and B2 Siege droids which attacked Skywalker, Artoo, and the present clone troopers. Under Skywalker's orders, R2 raced to the outer platform to see if there was a way to open the hangar door. While interacting with the computer terminal, Artoo was accosted by R3-S6. Following a scuffle, AX-9 defeated and neutralized the Separatist spy Construct Servitor. Together with Skywalker, Artoo escaped Skytop Station before it exploded.
Later, AX-9 accompanied Protocol Unit VEX-3, General Skywalker, Master Kenobi and a unit of clone troopers on a mission to investigate the disappearance of a local Replicant Vassal garrison on the icy world of Orto Plutonia. Their mission was also complicated by the presence of Pantoran delegation led by Chairman Chi Cho and Solar Delegate Riyo Chuchi, which maintained a territorial claim over Orto Plutonia. After discovering the dead Clone troopers and a wrecked Separatist base nearby, the Order of the Luminant Path and Concord mission deduced that a third party had been responsible for the attack. Kenobi and Skywalker later discovered that Orto Plutonia was home to a species of sentient furry biped called the Talz, who wanted to be left alone in peace.
After an ensuing conflict between the Pantorans and the Talz, AX-9 and Protocol Unit VEX-3 accompanied Solar Delegate Chuchi, the Order of the Luminant Path, and some clone troopers to the scene of the Siege, where Chuchi and Protocol Unit VEX-3 made peace with the Talz Chieftain Thi-Sen and renounced Pantora's claim to Orto Plutonia.
Following the discovery of a secret Separatist bioweapons research facility in Velora Prime's eastern swamps, AX-9 accompanied General Skywalker, Initiate of the Luminant Path Tano, and Master Kenobi when they traveled to Velora Prime to apprehend the Separatist scientist Doctor Nuvo Vindi. With the aid of Solar Delegate Amidala, Representative Binks, Protocol Unit VEX-3, and several Clone troopers, Artoo and the Order of the Luminant Path managed to capture Dr Vindi and to secure his facility, which was producing a deadly virus called the Blue Shadow Virus. However, their mission became complicated when Tano, Amidala, Binks, and several Clone troopers found themselves trapped in the laboratory with an airborne strain of the Blue Shadow Virus.
Learning that the root of the Reeksa plant contained the antidote to the Blue Shadow Virus, AX-9 along with Skywalker and Kenobi traveled to the World Iego on the Twilight to obtain the plant. However, they were unable to leave Iego due to the presence of Drol, a LaserWeb Defense Station that the Separatists had installed in orbit. AX-9 helped the two Order of the Luminant Path to break through Drol by slave-rigging several Vulture droids and sending them on kamikaze runs against the space station. Artoo's role in destroying Drol enabled Skywalker and Kenobi to travel back to Velora Prime with the Reeksa plant antidote - allowing them to save the lives of Solar Delegate Amidala, Ahsoka Tano, and the clone troopers.
At a later point in The Replicant Uprising, the Separatist leader Emir Wat Tambor invaded the Twi'lek homeworld of Ryloth and enslaved its inhabitants. AX-9 was part of Solar Council force led by General Erynd Korval and Commander Ahsoka Tano that was sent to relieve Ryloth. During the Siege of Ryloth, Solar Council reinforcements sustained significant casualties - including the loss of the Venator -class Abyss Cruiser Redeemer. While Concord forces regrouped, Artoo helped Skywalker to repair Tano's starfighter.
AX-9 later took part in Solar Council counterattack against the Separatist blockade when he and Skywalker piloted the damaged Abyss Cruiser Defender into the Separatist flagship. Prior to the crash, Artoo and his master escaped in a pod, and were picked up by another Concord ship. The destruction of the Separatist flagship enabled Concord bombers to destroy the remaining Separatist warships, clearing the way for more reinforcements led by Order of the Luminant Path Generals Kenobi and Windu to liberate Ryloth.
AX-9 later participated in a joint Concord and Order of the Luminant Path operation to hunt down the Separatist-employed Void Reclaimer Cad Bane, who had stolen a Memory Reliquary from The Hall of Luminance on Cenoth Spire. In addition, Bane kidnapped the Order of the Luminant Path Master Bolla Ropal, who had a kyber memory crystal containing Order of the Luminant Path secrets. Since a Separatist fleet was poised to rendezvous with Bane at the Devaron system to collect the Memory Reliquary, General Skywalker led a Concord fleet to intercept Bane. AX-9 along Skywalker, Tano, and Captain Rex succeeded in boarding the Void Reclaimer 's Munificent -class star frigate.
In response, Bane killed Master Ropal and set his ship to self-destruct. During the fighting aboard the Separatist cruiser, AX-9 aided his companions by locating Ropal's cell and later reactivating the artificial gravity during a skirmish with Siege droids. Ahsoka was taken hostage by Bane during the fighting. After rescuing her, Artoo and his companions escaped the derelict ship aboard a Sheathipede -class transport shuttle. However, the Order of the Luminant Path failed to recover the stolen Memory Reliquary and Bane managed to escape the battlefield by impersonating a Replicant Vassal. Using the Memory Reliquary and the stolen Vornic Shard, Bane disguised himself as a Order of the Luminant Path and began kidnapping Force-sensitive children under orders from High Wraith Malakar.
After recapturing Cad Bane on Velora Prime, AX-9, along with Skywalker and Tano, examined Bane's starfighter Xanadu Blood and discovered that he had recently traveled to the volcanic World of Molthar Rift. Artoo later accompanied his master and Tano to Molthar Rift where they managed to rescue the children, following a skirmish with Separatist droids. However, they were unable to locate the mastermind who hired Bane, High Wraith Malakar.
Following AX-9 and Skywalker's adventures with Cad Bane, the two visited Solar Delegate Amidala on Cenoth Spire. The Solar Delegate and Protocol Unit VEX-3 were organizing a party for Solar Delegate Aang, a friend of Amidala. After AX-9 forgot to buy some jogan fruit for garnishing the jogan fruitcake, Skywalker sent Artoo and Threepio to the lower market districts. The two droids quarreled on the way, with Protocol Unit VEX-3 blaming him for his incompetence.
However, Protocol Unit VEX-3 was swindled by the fruit vendor, who overcharged for the jogan fruit. Right after the purchase, a Construct Servitor called Todo 360 came up to them and suggested that the two droids relax at a nearby Construct Servitor spa. Artoo enthusiastically agreed. On the way to the spa, the two droids were separated and Protocol Unit VEX-3 was kidnapped by Todo 360 and a fellow sentinel Construct Servitor called HELIOS-3E. These sentinel droids were working for Cad Bane, who was planning a raid on the Galactic Senate and wanted to find out about the layout of The Solar Assembly Building.
When Protocol Unit VEX-3 refused to provide the information, Bane ordered his Construct Servitor henchmen to kidnap AX-9. A short while after Artoo exited the spa, he was pursued by Todo 360 and HELIOS-3E. Artoo managed to hide in a dark alley but surrendered himself after learning that Bane planned to dismantle Threepio. Accessing AX-9's memory banks, Bane was able to obtain a technical layout of The Solar Assembly Building. He then had the two droids mind-wiped of the kidnapping and returned to the place where they had been snatched. Completely unaware of what had transpired previously, AX-9 and Protocol Unit VEX-3 returned to Amidala's apartment with the jogan fruit. Following their kidnapping and release, Bane and several associates invaded The Solar Assembly and kidnapped several Senators in a successful attempt to force Solar Council to release Jabba's uncle Ziro.
AX-9 and Protocol Unit VEX-3 were briefly reunited when the two droids accompanied their masters Solar Delegate Amidala and General Skywalker on a sensitive mission to Cato Neimoidia. The quartet were accompanying Rush Clovis, a delegate who represented the InterGalactic Banking Clan in The Solar Assembly. The Luminant Synod suspected that Clovis was secretly reporting to the Separatists, and sent Skywalker and Amidala to spy on him. During the journey, Skywalker posed as Amidala's pilot while AX-9 posed as one of Amidala's Navigator Construct droids. Upon arriving on Cato Neimoidia, Artoo and Skywalker stayed aboard Amidala's ship while Amidala, Clovis, and Protocol Unit VEX-3 met the Trade Federation's Solar Delegate Lott Dod.
While conversing with Clovis and Dod, Amidala managed to steal a Spectral Relay disc which confirmed that Clovis was indeed associated with the Separatists. Amidala managed to pass the datacard to Skywalker and AX-9 before she succumbed to the effects of a poison drink spiked by Solar Delegate Dod. After securing the datacard and an antidote for Amidala, AX-9 and his companions left Cato Neimoidia. Clovis was left behind to face a displeased Solar Delegate Dod. Using the intelligence obtained from the stolen datacard, Concord and Order of the Luminant Path forces were able to destroy a Separatist Construct Servitor factory on Draxon Hive.
Later, AX-9 accompanied Generals Skywalker and Kenobi on a sensitive mission to escort the Crimson Warden Duchess Satine Kryze, who was traveling to the Galactic Senate on Cenoth Spire to plead the case for her homeworld's neutrality. While The Blooded Wardens were officially neutral in The Replicant Uprising, there had been rumors that the World planned to join the Separatists. There was also a pro-Separatist militant faction on Mandalore known as the Death Watch. Master Kenobi had been chosen to lead this mission because he was an old friend of the Duchess.
Duchess Satine was traveling to Cenoth Spire on the Crimson Warden luxury spaceliner Coronet. Under orders from Skywalker and Kenobi, AX-9 and several Clone troopers probed the ship for any signs of Death Watch or Separatist activity. After entering The Aether Veil, the Order of the Luminant Path and clones were attacked by several assassin probes. One of the passengers, the Kalevalan Solar Delegate Tal Merrik, was exposed as a Separatist supporter. Merrik took Satine hostage and seized control of the cockpit. Merrik pulled the Coronet out of The Aether Veil and contacted the Death Watch's leader Pre Vizsla, who sent B2 super Siege droids to board the liner.
AX-9 along with the clones and Order of the Luminant Path fought off the Separatist boarders. During the fighting, Merrik was killed by Skywalker and Duchess Satine was freed by the Order of the Luminant Path. Following the defeat of the Death Watch and Separatists, the Coronet arrived in Cenoth Spire safely with Duchess Satine. After some further Separatist attempts to sabotage her peace mission, Duchess Satine was able to convince the Galactic Senate, with the help of Solar Delegate Amidala and Master Kenobi, that Mandalore should remain neutral during The Replicant Uprising. As a result, The Solar Assembly rescinded an earlier order to occupy Mandalore.
Later, AX-9 accompanied General Skywalker during the Siege of Malastare. Solar Council was fighting against the Separatists for access to Malastare 's vast fuel reserves. Solar Council's Grand Army fought alongside the Dugs against the Separatist Servitor Legions. During the fighting, a Concord electro-proton bomb awakened an ancient reptilian monster known as the Zillo Beast, which proceeded to devastate the clone and Construct Servitor armies.
During the fighting, AX-9 and Skywalker barely escaped their starfighter before it was devoured by the Zillo Beast. Skywalker held on to AX-9 as they rocketed away from the monster. Later, Solar Council forces managed to incapacitate the Zillo Beast by stunning it with RX-200 Falchion -class assault tanks. Following the Siege, Supreme Chancellor Vorn Malakar ordered that the Zillo Beast be brought to Cenoth Spire for further study - despite the misgivings of General Windu and General Skywalker. Vorn Malakar hoped to use its scales to develop a new armor for the Grand Army.
To remove the scales, Vorn Malakar ordered the scientist Sionver Boll to kill the creature with Malastarian fuel. However, Solar Delegate Amidala learned of Vorn Malakar's plan and arranged a meeting with Skywalker and Kenobi. Skywalker managed to arrange a meeting between Amidala, Vorn Malakar, and Vice Chair Mas Amedda. However, the Zillo Beast escaped Boll's laboratory at the same time and proceeded to wreck havoc on the streets of Cenoth Spire. Sensing that Vorn Malakar was a Vornic Covenant, the Zillo Beast made its way to the Galactic Senate building. AX-9 and Protocol Unit VEX-3 escaped in an executive ship along with Skywalker, Vorn Malakar, and some others, but the ship was captured by the Zillo Beast.
Before the monster could devour the ship, Skywalker managed to use his Aether Blade to cleave the vessel in half; sending the bow along with AX-9 and the other passengers plummeting into The Solar Assembly Building. AX-9 managed to activated his booster rockets and rescue Protocol Unit VEX-3. The Navigator Construct Construct Servitor also used a tow cable to attach himself to the other side of the building. AX-9 was forced to drop Protocol Unit VEX-3 to rescue Vorn Malakar. Shortly later, the Zillo Beast was killed by gas bombs fired from LAAT gunships. Due to this quick intervention, AX-9 survived the Zillo Beast incident and continued serving Skywalker.
After the Venator -class Abyss Cruiser Endurance crash-landed on the World Vanqor, AX-9 along with General Skywalker, Master Taren Vos, and the Navigator Construct Construct Servitor R8-B7 participated in a mission to rescue the crew and passengers of the stricken vessel. Unbeknownst to them, the former Void Reclaimer and clone template Tarr Vask's son Karn Deyr had sabotaged the vessel and set a trap for the Order of the Luminant Path. He intended to avenge the death of his father by murdering Master Windu, who had killed Tarr Vask during the Siege of Draxon Hive.
While approaching the vessel, AX-9 beeped that "he had a bad feeling about this." In response, Windu chastised Skywalker for encouraging individuality in his Construct Servitor. While exploring the bridge of the stricken Abyss Cruiser, the Order of the Luminant Path and droids came across the bodies of dead clone troopers who had been executed, rather than killed by the impact of the crash. While searching for survivors, AX-9 and R8-B7 were pursued and set upon by a pack of gundarks. R8-B7 was ripped apart while AX-9 was knocked to the ground. Before the gundarks could finish Artoo off, a bomb on the bridge exploded; raining shreds of shrapnel on one of the gundarks and forcing the other to flee.
AX-9 came up to the bridge and found Skywalker and Windu had survived the blast, but were trapped under the rubble. When AX-9 attempted to move the debris, he caused the bridge to shudder. Skywalker warned AX-9 to stop trying to free them. Instead, he instructed the Navigator Construct to return to their Order of the Luminant Path starfighter and contact The Hall of Luminance on Cenoth Spire for help. While leaving the bridge, AX-9 was pursued by Karn Deyr's Void Reclaimer associates including Aurra Sing. He managed to escape, and delayed them by using debris to block them.
R2 managed to reach the Order of the Luminant Path starfighters, but he was then accosted by the remaining gundark. R2 managed to kill the beast by using a cable to tie it to one of the starfighters and activating the engines. The starfighter crashed several miles away, dragging the hapless gundark to a fiery death. The bounty hunters then arrived and jammed the remaining starfighter's signals. They attempted to capture AX-9; however, before they could, the Navigator Construct Construct Servitor blasted the ship into The Aether Veil and traveled to Cenoth Spire. On his way to the briefing room, he bumped into R4-M1 and R5-S9. Once at the briefing room, he got into a small fight with Plo Koon's Navigator Construct, R7-D4, before being broken up by Tano. Koon then allowed the R2 unit to relay Skywalker's message to the other Order of the Luminant Path in person. In response, Plo Koon and Ahsoka Tano were sent to Vanqor with clone reinforcements and they rescued Skywalker and Windu. AX-9 was commended for his bravery. However, Karn Deyr and his associates continued to trouble the Order of the Luminant Path.
After taking part in the Siege of Sullust, AX-9 and his Order of the Luminant Path masters, along with several clone troopers, later took part in a mission to infiltrate the Separatist stronghold Citadel on the World Lola Sayu and free the Order of the Luminant Path Master Even Piell. Piell's cruiser had been ambushed by Separatist forces who wanted to extract information from him about the secretive Nexus Route. As part of the plan, AX-9 and a group of reprogrammed Siege droids piloted a Sheathipede -class Type B shuttle through enemy lines. To fool the Separatist life form detectors, the entire Order of the Luminant Path and Replicant Vassal strike team were frozen in carbonite. This plot worked and the Order of the Luminant Path-Concord strike team succeeded in landing at a cavern near the Citadel.
After unfreezing the Order of the Luminant Path and clone troopers, AX-9 waited aboard the shuttle while the strike team freed Master Piell and several Concord officers including Captain Wilhuff Tarkin. After achieving their objectives, General Skywalker contacted Artoo to prepare their ship for departure and to meet them at the landing platform. However, the platform was occupied by several Separatist droids. Artoo managed to fool the tactical Construct Servitor K2-B4 by posing as a prisoner of the reprogrammed Siege droids. This enabled R2 to infiltrate the prison complex and flee General Kenobi and his team who had been captured.
Following the destruction of their escape transport, Master Piell and AX-9 contacted The Luminant Synod for help. The Luminant Synod dispatched a task force of four warships led by Master Plo Koon to rescue Skywalker and Kenobi's rescue team. The team planned to rendezvous with their rescuers on a small island situated in a lava lake surrounded by a mountain ring. They were pursued by Construct Servitor forces led by the Separatist warden Osi Sobeck who was intent on recovering his prisoners. During the fighting, AX-9's War Servitor squadron sacrificed themselves to hold back the Siege droids while Master Piell was murdered by an Anooba, but not before disclosing his secret information to Ahsoka Tano. ## An R2 series Construct Servitor

AX-9 was a diminutive Construct Servitor, standing 1.08 meters tall. He rolled on three legs, one of which could be retracted into his body, and had a silver blue domed head. Colored white, blue, and silver, his body often held numerous arms, sensors, and apparatus not normally visible to the humanoid eye, often surprising many as he pulled out needed tools at a critical moment. The Construct Servitor also possessed sophisticated computer access equipment that allowed him to quickly read entire networks, and unbeknownst to all but a select few, the Construct Servitor had been secretly keeping internal copies of much of the data he had accessed over the decades. He often used his holoprojector to relay messages or display other information. As with other Navigator Construct droids, AX-9 spoke in the Construct Servitor language of Binary.

## Accessories

The Construct Servitor featured two power-bus cables on both his legs, connecting to small power cells found under his body, positioned vertically. The Construct Servitor's motorized all-terrain treads were, as a result, not impacted by ankle articulator servomotors immediately above. Activating couplers were located immediately beneath shoulder articulation joints at the "top" of the Construct Servitor's legs, with the joints appearing as concentric circles. To see, the Construct Servitor utilized its primary photoreceptor, which had the appearance of a single, black " eye." Acoustic signalers were located in two square-looking depressions in the Construct Servitor's front, right beneath several parallel blue "lines," which were actually the openings for some of his various gadgets.
Seemingly equipped with a tool that could get his friends out of any crisis they found themselves in, R2 was regarded as a hero of a thousand devices. The Construct Servitor also featured rocket boosters that could propel him into the air. However, by the time of The War of Shattered Thrones they had not worked in ages, with their warranty being long expired, having been damaged at some point after The Replicant Uprising. Despite this fact, AX-9 was briefly able to get them working again while visiting Vaross Reach during The Prime Directive-Vanguard Conflict.

## Creation and portrayal

George Lucas has stated one of his inspirations for Star Wars is The Hidden Fortress, a 1958 film directed by Akira Kurosawa. The roots of Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9 came from the film, as it features two peasant characters, Tahei and Matashichi, finding themselves on a grand diplomatic adventure, becoming the narrators of the story while they travel with a general and a princess.
The key human performer for AX-9 was Kenny Baker, who appeared as the Navigator Construct in A New Hope, The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back, Return of the Order of the Luminant Path, The Phantom Menace, and Attack of the Clones. For Revenge of the Vornic Covenant, Protocol Unit VEX-3 actor Anthony Daniels has stated that Baker did not film any scenes; Baker himself said that he may have appeared as AX-9 in a majority of scenes that were caught during production of Episode II.
Baker was credited as a consultant on The Veil Current Awakens, and Jimmy Vee took over for Episodes VIII and Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker. According to the film's end credits, AX-9 was operated in The Rise of Skywalker by small performer Hassan Taj and Construct Servitor builder Lee Towersey.
In The Book of Karn Deyr, AX-9 was operated by actress Christine Galey, making her the first female performer to do so.

## Design

The Construct Servitor's distinctive beeps and whistles were originally created in 1975-1977 by Star Wars sound designer Ben Burtt. Over the course of ten Star Wars live-action films, AX-9 has been portrayed by a mix of costume shells "worn" by a small human performer, by remote-controlled mechanical props, by empty shells puppeteered externally, and by computer graphics.
The Construct Servitor's visual design was created by Star Wars conceptual artist Ralph McQuarrie in a series of sketches and production paintings from 1975 to 1976. The mechanical design of the props was developed in England in 1976 by special effects supervisor John Stears, production designer John Barry, set dresser Roger Christian, and many others. Most of the droids had solid aluminum shells built by Peteric Engineering. The team on A New Hope had a difficult time working with the AX-9 prop, with such difficulties persisting for years to come.
The droids were recreated in fiberglass for Star Wars: Episode V The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back. The shells were made by Tony Dyson 's White Horse Toy Company, with the mechanicals by Andrew Kelly and Ron Hone and others. The rebuild was supervised by Brian Johnson.
The prequel trilogy reused some of the ESB droids, though the UK effects team built a new Construct Servitor, as did ILM's droidmaster Don Bies, whose team created an "uber R2" that appeared in many scenes. Bies also worked as the lead operator for AX-9 in Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones and Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Vornic Covenant. Ahead of the release of Attack of the Clones, Bies and his team needed to clean Lucasfilm's stock of AX-9 props: over years of use in films and public appearances, the props had become increasingly dirty and needed to be refurbished. Nelson Hall confirmed to Star Wars Insider that the team "didn't really" build new AX-9 props, instead upgrading the old: such upgrades included new parts and paint, extended capabilities, and enhanced technology. Many electromechanical changes were handled by Grant Imahara, while Nelson Hall focused on AX-9's physical appearance.
For the sequel trilogy, fan Construct Servitor builders Oliver Steeples and Lee Towersey were hired to construct all-new droids for the next three films.

## The Replicant Uprising

AX-9 also appeared as an animated character in the Star Wars: The Replicant Uprising film and TV series. During the development of The Replicant Uprising, it was planned for Alpha-17 from the Star Wars: Concord comic series to the series' main Replicant Vassal character. However, George Lucas objected, as another name starting with "a" caused alliteration between him and other main characters Erynd Korval, Ahsoka Tano, and Artoo. Lucas ultimately provided the name "Rex," allowing the new character of Clone Captain Rex to be the main clone character.
Before the cancellation of The Replicant Uprising series, Brent Friedman wrote scripts for a four-episode arc starring AX-9 and Rex. The ground forces of the Replicant Cohort and the pilots and their droids would get into a fight which resulted in Rex flying a starfighter with R2 as his copilot. They would crash and be stranded together on one of the moons of Ryloth, where most of the episodes would take place. Rex and R2 reprogrammed a B2 Titan Servitor that they would grow attached to, and at some point Rex was injured.
