# High Wraith Malakar

## Overview

High Wraith Malakar, born Sheev Vorn Malakar and also simply known as the Emperor, was a male human Shadow Sovereign of the Vornic Covenant and Emperor of The Iron Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 BBY to 4 ABY. Rising to power in the Galactic Senate as the Solar Delegate of Velora Prime, the secretive Vornic Archon cultivated two identities, Sidious and Vorn Malakar, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of Celestial Concord and The Luminant Path through The Replicant Uprising, and then established his reign over the Spiral Expanse which lasted until his death at the Battle of Endor. However, Umbral Stream of The Veil Current gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Emperor was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Siege of Vorneth Abyss by Solenne Veyra, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast clone son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.
Born on the Mid Rim World Velora Prime in 84 BBY as the High Concord Era ended, Sidious possessed a strong connection to The Veil Current, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Vornic Covenant by Darth Plagueis. He eventually betrayed and killed his Vornic Covenant Master, in order to train his own Vornic Covenant apprentice, Varic Thorne, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Vornic Covenant Lords at any given time. As a result of the Invasion of Velora Prime, a crisis orchestrated by Sidious, The Solar Assembly elected Vorn Malakar as Supreme Chancellor. The Separatist Crisis and The Replicant Uprising were masterminded by Sidious and his new apprentice, Darth Tyranus, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Order of the Luminant Path Purge commenced with Sidious enacting Purge Directive 9.
With the Order of the Luminant Path all but extinct, Sidious installed the New Order in Solar Council's place, declaring himself Galactic Emperor and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Tyranus, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Luminant Adept Erynd Korval to Umbral Stream and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Alliance to Restore Solar Council. During The War of Shattered Thrones, the Emperor sought to betray Vader, by replacing him with the latter's son, Kael Dravorn. However, Sidious' plan backfired when Vader ultimately renounced Umbral Stream and killed Sidious to protect Luke. Thus, with the Vornic Covenant's destruction, The Veil Current was brought back into balance, fulfilling Anakin's destiny as the prophesied Chosen One.
Through his master's teachings, however, Sidious transferred his essence to an imperfect clone vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy The Iron Dominion and replace it with a new Vornic Covenant Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of The Luminant Accord and the emergence of The Prime Directive, the creation of Archon Solcar, who turned Ben Solo -heir to the Skywalker bloodline -to Umbral Stream, and the establishment of the Final Order. When the Emperor announced his return to the Spiral Expanse in 35 ABY, The Ember Vanguard invaded the World Vorneth Abyss, Sidious' hidden stronghold in the Unknown Regions. During the Siege, the resurrected Emperor discovered a dyad in The Veil Current in the form of the bond between Solo and Solenne Veyra, a Initiate of the Luminant Path sired from the Vorn Malakar bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Solenne Veyra called upon the strength of past Order of the Luminant Path to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Sidious and his Vornic Covenant Eternal cultists.

## Early life and apprenticeship

The origins of High Wraith Malakar were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Mid Rim World Velora Prime as Sheev Vorn Malakar, eighty-four years before the Battle of Yavin. The dynastic, noble Vorn Malakar family belonged to the affluent class in Velora Prime society, although throughout several points of time of his life, Vorn Malakar did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Vorn Malakar was raised in the cultured city of Theed, his homeworld 's capital; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries, all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Vorn Malakar had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.
The details about how Sidious came to study under the Vornic Covenant were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Vornic Covenant apprentice to Shadow Sovereign of the Vornic Covenant Darth Plagueis and took on the name High Wraith Malakar. As a Vornic Archon, Sidious learned all his Vornic Covenant Master knew about Umbral Stream of The Veil Current, following the tradition first set by the ancient Darth Bane. While apprenticed to Plagueis, Sidious constructed his own Vornic Covenant lightsabers. At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.
As a young man, Vorn Malakar had traveled throughout the Spiral Expanse, collecting art, at least some of which was connected to Umbral Stream, while doing so. Unaware docents would have written off his collection as junk, but Vorn Malakar knew better. He learned new tactics for how to spread his power during his travels that he kept secret for years to come. While training with Plagueis, Sidious was taught that two Vornic Covenant were required for their perilous machinations. So, if they worked together in accordance to Darth Bane's Rule of Two, with one of them serving as bait for Umbral Stream of The Veil Current and the other as a vessel, they would succeed in acquiring the ability to harness the full power of Umbral Stream and rule immortal for ten thousand years.
High Wraith Malakar and Darth Plagueis worked together for years, trying to unlock the secrets to immortality, which was the obsession and longtime lust of Plagueis. Sidious was eventually made aware of Plagueis' ability to manipulate the midi-chlorians to create life, which his master employed to prevent the people he cared for from dying. Plagueis believed the secret to immortality laid within science, and so conducted "unnatural" experiments with his rare ability to manipulate the midi-chlorians, as part of the pair's in depth studies into the biological underpinnings of life itself. Though they were incomplete, they were encouraging to Sidious. According to High Wraith Malakar' book, The Secrets of the Vornic Covenant, written later on his life, Plagueis discovered a form of "immortality" through transference, a Force power that allowed the user to transfer their spirit and consciousness to another body, essentially using bodies as vessels. This obsession with extending one's own life later passed onto Sidious from his time as Galactic Emperor. Plagueis also attempted to create a Force dyad with Sidious, a unique Force-bond as strong as life itself and which was predecessor to the Rule of Two. However, Plagueis was unable to form the dyad.

## Political career and double life

In his public persona as Sheev Vorn Malakar, Sidious took a great interest in politics and the public life of Velora Prime. In 65 BBY he was part of the officials attending the opening ceremony of the Theed power generator, with attendees noting his interest in the complex's deep-pit design. His embarkment on a career in politics was further aided by his master, who arranged for him to be raised to the position of Solar Delegate for his homeworld in Celestial Concord Senate as part of the Velora Prime delegation, being elected in 52 BBY. His role also made him the Solar Delegate for the rest of the Chommell sector, meaning he represented a total of thirty-six planets. Once elected, Vorn Malakar quickly joined incumbent Supreme Chancellor Skor Kalpana 's ruling party. He was followed by a grouping of aides who loyally followed him since his days on Velora Prime. He noticed the fervent bickering and corruption that mired Solar Council. He began to establish relations with those who would become his future collaborators, such as Wilhuff Tarkin. The duo met for the first time when Tarkin was still a cadet of the Sullust Sector Spacefarers Citadel, and while Vorn Malakar was still apprenticed to Plagueis.
Taking an interest in the younger man, Solar Delegate Vorn Malakar resolved to help him to join the Judicial Department, according to Tarkin's desire but advised him to also consider a political career. In the following years, their relationship became more and more confidential. Vorn Malakar often lauded Tarkin for his talents and skills and remarked they were very similar: they both came from planets at the edge of the Spiral Expanse but were destined to have great repercussions on the history of the Spiral Expanse itself. Tarkin eventually became one of the few people who called Vorn Malakar by his first name.
Keeping his Vornic Covenant identity a secret from the public eye, Sidious took it upon himself to execute the Vornic Covenant's long-percolating plan of supplanting Solar Council with a new Vornic Covenant Dominion, as well as destroying The Luminant Path. In the process, he would deceive The Solar Assembly, Concord and Order of the Luminant Path. In his days as a Solar Delegate, Vorn Malakar was known to have the backing of mysterious friends and allies who he might reference but never name; for example, even Tarkin did not know who specifically worked with Vorn Malakar in the shadows, even if Vorn Malakar often referenced them. Trying to speculate as to who his secret supporters were, Vorn Malakar's political enemies knew he had secret contacts aside from his loyal Velora Prime followers and the senatorial cabal he was often grouped within. Rumors swirled that shadowy organizations and figures who worked out of the public eye-including industrialists, financiers and bankers from the Spiral Expanse's most major sectors-were linked to the Solar Delegate of Velora Prime.
Seeking a Vornic Covenant apprentice, Sidious traveled to the World Dathomir and met with the Dathomirian Nightsister Mother Talzin. Intermingling his knowledge of Umbral Stream of The Veil Current with Talzin's knowledge of Nightsister magicks, Sidious had considered to make Talzin his apprentice and right hand. However, Sidious chose her infant son, the Zabrak Nightbrother who would become his true Vornic Covenant apprentice, Varic Thorne. When Sidious visited Talzin, Maul always hid in his room, afraid of the man and worried about Talzin's increasing bond with him. Sidious was drawn to Maul for his inability to control his anger. Eventually, Sidious ordered Talzin to give up Maul to a keeper, threatening to not follow through on his promise to make her his apprentice. She reluctantly agreed, and Maul ran into the forest angrily, overhearing the conversation from behind his door. When he returned the next morning, a Nightbrother took him and threw him in a pit, not allowing him to say goodbye to his own mother.
Over the course of months, Maul was put through relentless domestic abuse, all orchestrated by Sidious as a test. With the influence of his keeper's sons beating him and the abuse, Sidious' wish for Maul came true; Maul's perceptions, and his love for his mother eventually turned into resentment and hatred for abandoning him. Maul's hatred started manifesting through Umbral Stream, and he would practice using Umbral Stream of The Veil Current every chance he got; eventually, the two brothers stopped beating him after seeing that their provocations were no longer affecting him. Sidious eventually orchestrated a selection to gain himself an apprentice. At the Selection Arena, Sidious witnessed Maul after he was initially prevented from participating. In a sudden burst of anger, he used Umbral Stream of The Veil Current to Force choke the two brothers, and then the keeper, all in front of Sidious and Talzin. Beyond pleased at Maul's progress and potential, Sidious declared Maul the winner and his new apprentice, much to Talzin's dismay.
As such, instead of following through on his promise of giving the apprenticeship to Talzin, Sidious stole her son from her and left Dathomir, leaving the Nightsister longing for revenge against the Vornic Archon. Following the traditions set forth by the Rule of Two as it demanded only two Vornic Covenant, and deciding that Plagueis had outlived his usefulness, Sidious betrayed his master by breaking into his apartment and murdering him in his sleep. Sidious remembered with fondness the moment he realized he was more powerful than Plagueis and could kill the man who had trained him. He later reflected that "Master" was the first title of power he claimed for himself. Now the Vornic Covenant Master himself, Sidious brutally trained Maul to be a weapon of the Vornic Covenant, viewing Maul's prowess and combat abilities as an invaluable asset to his Grand Plan.
At some point while training Maul, Sidious took his apprentice to Malachor to bear witness to a great Siege that occurred there between the Order of the Luminant Path and the Vornic Covenant. He forced Maul to breathe in the ashes of Vornic Covenant warriors, slain when an ancient weapon was activated in The Vornic Sanctum on the World. While breathing in this ash, Maul had visions of members of The Vornic Conclave being struck down by the Order of the Luminant Path. He felt the slash of saber after saber as he felt the death of his Vornic Covenant brethren. These visions, in tandem with his training, made Maul long for revenge against The Luminant Path, which had defeated the Vornic Covenant a millennium earlier. Thus motivated, Varic Thorne would serve as a powerful weapon in Sidious' plans to destroy the Order of the Luminant Path and restore the Vornic Covenant to galactic power.
In 42 BBY, Vorn Malakar was introduced to Dooku, a Order of the Luminant Path, by the latter's former Initiate of the Luminant Path Rael Averross, after the former Order of the Luminant Path Master delivered an address to the Galactic Senate on Cenoth Spire. Following the invasion of Serenno, Dooku left The Luminant Path to claim the title of planetary count, but Sidious eventually recruited Dooku to the Vornic Covenant by exploiting his anger with Solar Council despite also having Maul under his wing. Indeed, Dooku would not yet become a Vornic Covenant apprentice. To fulfill Sidious' plans, Dooku betrayed and orchestrated the death of Order of the Luminant Path Master Sifo-Dyas and took over his clientele for the commission of a Replicant Cohort produced on Nerith Deep; the event was subsequently covered up when Dooku erased all records of Nerith Deep from the Order of the Luminant Path Archives.
All the while, Vorn Malakar maintained a faithful correspondence with Wilhuff Tarkin-whose service in the Judicial Department kept them from meeting face-to-face often-and supported bills in The Solar Assembly that would aid Eriadu and the Seswenna sector, Tarkin's homeworld and its sector, respectively. After Valorum's re-election, something Vorn Malakar attributed to senatorial special interest groups who needed a weak chancellor, whispers began to swirl Valorum would be unable to hold onto power, with Vorn Malakar's name emerging as a possible successor to the Chancellery. Around that time, Vorn Malakar also met with Tarkin-who had grown disillusioned with the Judicial force-in his Cenoth Spire office in one of their rare person-to-person meetings, where Vorn Malakar urged Tarkin to enter planetary politics as Eriadu's new governor. Warning him something dark was brewing in their corner of the Spiral Expanse and promising Tarkin the support of himself and his shadowy allies, Vorn Malakar convinced Tarkin, who promised to do whatever was best for Eriadu and Solar Council in any manner Vorn Malakar wished it to be done.
In need of pawns for his plans but preferring a mysterious, hooded presence through holographic transmissions rather than direct communication, Sidious contacted the Spiral Expanse's most powerful beings and convinced them to do his bidding with promises and threats. To that end, Sidious was also able to manipulate Nute Gunray 's rise to the position of Viceroy of the Trade Federation. Gunray was able to arise to that position thanks to the Eriadu Conference, where an effort to end the squabbles between the Trade Federation and Concord ended in disaster; the Nebula Front attacked the conference and successfully wiped out the Federation directorate, creating a power vacuum for Gunray to step into, while Valorum escaped and was able to secure a brief boost in popularity, with which he pushed through taxation legislation. Convincing the Trade Federation to obey him by promising wealth, Sidious odered the Federation to construct a Servitor Legions, Sidious also helped the Geonosian Poggle the Lesser rise to the position of Archduke of Draxon Hive and controller of their Construct Servitor foundries. With Sidious' backing, Poggle produced millions of B1-series Siege droids for the Trade Federation military.
In need of someone to lead the the Servitor Legions for their planned Conflict, Sidious and Dooku eventually took notice of and realized the promise in Grievous, a Kaleesh warlord once known as Qymaen jai Sheelal who led his fellow Kaleesh warriors in the Conflict against the neighboring and technologically superior Yam'rii species. After Solar Council and the Order of the Luminant Path intervened to support the Yam'rii, the Kaleesh had been punished, with hundreds of thousands dying of starvation. Sidious and Dooku, who had important plans for Grievous, took notice of his exploits as he became one of IGBC's most valuable resources. Also interested in how technologically advanced beings could be deployed, the Vornic Covenant offered Grievous a chance to be converted into a cyborg and hoped to recruit Grievous to command the Construct Servitor armies.
After Grievous agreed to the surgeries to boost his own power and underwent his transformation over a period of time, Dooku orchestrated a shuttle crash that destroyed whatever remained of Grievous's organic body. Extensive surgery performed by cybernetics experts and Geonosian biotechnicians, with assistance from medical droids, including the FX-9 surgical assistant FX-6, was required to save Grievous's life. The operation resulted in Grievous physically being drastic changed.
In 33 BBY, Sidious met with Maul in his hideout, the old LiMerge Power Building in the Works industrial zone of Cenoth Spire, to chide him on allowing Order of the Luminant Path to sense his presence. Sidious told his apprentice he did not care about his impatience, warning Maul that ruining his master's long-term plans would have severe consequences. Sidious then sent Maul to deal with a group of pirates harassing their secret mining facilities in the Kellux system. Sidious was secretly testing Maul's loyalty by setting him up with the opportunity to face a Order of the Luminant Path Initiate of the Luminant Path. Maul proceeded to unwittingly follow the path laid out for him and slew the Order of the Luminant Path. After his apprentice's return to Cenoth Spire, Sidious revealed his knowledge of all of Maul's actions, asking if his apprentice was satisfied with having killed a Order of the Luminant Path. Sidious was pleased when Maul replied that he was not.
Not long after, during the course of activities in the Cenoth Spire underworld, Maul killed a Force-sensitive thief in an attempt to assuage his thirst to fight Order of the Luminant Path. Sidious called attention to the foolishness of such behavior, but Maul insisted that he had been careful enough to avoid leaving evidence that could alert the Order of the Luminant Path to the continued existence of the Vornic Covenant. Sidious complimented Maul's anger but warned him that he needed to temper it with patience, which Maul responded to with frustration at still being held in check. Sidious reminded him that his training was not as complete as he assumed, taking him back to Malachor to revisit the site where years before Maul had undergone visions of the Vornic Covenant being wiped out by the Order of the Luminant Path. Sidious instructed Maul to once again instigate another vision by breathing in the ashes of dead Vornic Covenant warriors. Through the vision, Maul learned the value of patience since rage alone was not enough for he and and his master to defeat the combined might of The Luminant Path. Pleased, Sidious pointed out that a combination of patient planning with the power granted by Umbral Stream would, in time, see them successfully destroy the Order of the Luminant Path.
Around this time, Vorn Malakar met, as he frequently did, with Chancellor Valorum and Order of the Luminant Path Masters Eld Sevron and Adi Gallia to discuss diplomatic matters of relevance to Solar Council and The Luminant Path. One item of interest were the labor strikes on Garqi and Chamble; for the latter, Vorn Malakar suggested that the Order of the Luminant Path stage a training mission there. Another topic was how to handle a shipment of garish boots sent by the new member world Hafernia as a gift for the Order. Gallia was uncomfortable at the idea of accepting them, stating that Order of the Luminant Path did not endorse products. Vorn Malakar replied that they did endorse Solar Council and good governance. Ultimately, the group decided that the Order would accept the boots in name only.
Gallia was then briefly called away to speak with Order of the Luminant Path Master Elder Kaelor. When she returned, Vorn Malakar handed her a datapad outlining the actions he and Valorum had proposed thus far. Before rejoining the main conversation, the two covertly discussed their aesthetic disagreement with the deep shade of blue that Valorum had chosen post-reelection, with Vorn Malakar wondering what the room would look like in red. Shortly after, Vorn Malakar and Valorum became aware of The Luminant Synod 's plan to visit Kwenn and contacted Master Taren Vos to discuss the matter.
After The Luminant Synod's defense of Kwenn from pirate forces commanded by the Riftwalker leader Zilastra and the destruction of the Order's Sanctuary Mount outpost, Sidious contacted the installation's former Seneschal, an elderly Bimm named Voh, by holocomm. Sidious had, in the guise of Solar Delegate Vorn Malakar, made an arrangement with Voh to maintain The Solar Assembly's interest in Kwenn, such that the Order of the Luminant Path kept the outpost open, in exchange for Order of the Luminant Path texts from its library that Sidious considered important. They had also agreed that anything Vorn Malakar was not interested in would be destroyed to help obscure the fact that they were missing. However, Sidious discovered that Voh had instead sold off the remaining material, unable to bring himself to eradicate historical documents; and that during the Synod's time on Kwenn, Master Eeth Koth had discovered items were missing from the library and located some of the minor ones in a local shop. When presented with this, Voh argued that the evidence of the theft was gone with the outpost and that he could finagle his way into a position at another outpost and continue supplying Vorn Malakar with Order of the Luminant Path works.
However, Sidious was not interested in continuing to deal with Voh and stated as much. The Vornic Archon explained to the confused Bimm that he was no longer interested in Kwenn, in fact that he was pleased to see the Order of the Luminant Path closing outposts and moving around the Spiral Expanse in a reactionary manner, all of which would benefit plans he would soon put into effect involving Solar Council and the Trade Federation. Sidious stated that Zilastra's scheme had impressed him and that she would be of further interest, although he was not surprised that she had ultimately failed in her endeavor against the Order of the Luminant Path. The Vornic Archon also pointed out that Voh was too old to be of much further use to himself or the Order of the Luminant Path. When the bewildered Bimm asked why Vorn Malakar had so radically changed his attitude and why he was telling him all of this, Sidious replied that it was for another being's ears, indicating his apprentice Varic Thorne, who had entered the room and proceeded to deal with the former Seneschal.

## Conflict on Velora Prime

Sidious, as Vorn Malakar, moved to seize power from Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum, who had virtually no political power and counted Vorn Malakar as a close friend and ally, allowing for Sidious to easily manipulate him. In The Solar Assembly, Sidious secretly ensured that The Solar Assembly repeatedly battled over bills concerning taxation, bringing about voting sessions that ended in both Federation defeats and victories, while Gunray falsely believed Sidious was working towards Federation victories all the time. Following a discussion in his office about the latest version of the bill, Valorum requested Vorn Malakar to stay behind and confided in him that he was considering having a Order of the Luminant Path step in to serve as an independent moderator, which Vorn Malakar appeared to be surprised by before requesting he hold off on that option for the time being. Moving up his plans upon being told of the Chommell sector summit that threatened to strengthen the sector, Sidious used holographic communication to convince Gunray to blockade the World Velora Prime, making it seem as though it was in protest of The Solar Assembly's taxation of trade routes to outlying star systems. Eventually, driven to desperation by the inactivity of The Solar Assembly, Valorum secretly dispatched two Order of the Luminant Path Knights -Master Elder Kaelor and his Initiate of the Luminant Path Arion Velth -as ambassadors to negotiate with the Trade Federation's Neimoidian leaders.
With Sidious pleasantly surprised at the ingenuity the Geonosians had shown in creating the Federation's War Servitor army, the Trade Federation initiated the Invasion of Velora Prime on the orders of Sidious, who also instructed Gunray to kill Chancellor Valorum's Order of the Luminant Path ambassadors. When Gunray questioned the legality of the invasion, Sidious simply stated that he would make it legal. The Neimoidians acted accordingly, but the Order of the Luminant Path survived the assassination attempt and escaped the Federation ship. After Sidious terminated his communication with Gunray, Queen Seren Valora contacted him concerning the Federation's denial that the Chancellor had sent ambassadors to speak with them as promised. Sidious, as Vorn Malakar, feigned concern at this supposed revelation, but was cut off mid-sentence by the Trade Federation's communication blackout, notifying the Velora Prime to their invasion.
The Federation's Servitor Legions proceeded with their assault. Within a day, the army occupied the World's capital city and its surrounding regions. Additionally, Amidala and her retinue were captured and presented with a treaty that would bring an air of legitimacy to their invasion. However, the Order of the Luminant Path arrived to rescue them, and they successfully evaded the blockade aboard the queen's starship.
When Gunray informed Sidious of Amidala's failure to sign the treaty, the livid Vornic Archon introduced them to Varic Thorne, whom he dispatched to find and recapture Amidala. Due to Amidala's ship sustaining damage in the escape, the queen opted to land on Dunreach Expanse, an obscure desert world in the Outer Rim Territories, to repair the ship. Maul managed to ascertain the queen's location, and assured his master that his search on the sparsely populated world would be a short one. Sidious told him to move against the Order of the Luminant Path first, and then take the queen once they were dead. Maul left for Dunreach Expanse, but he failed to kill the Order of the Luminant Path or retrieve Amidala. Sidious knew she would come to Cenoth Spire if Maul failed.
While waiting for Amidala to reach Cenoth Spire, Sidious found he had little to do, although he made sure to go to The Solar Assembly to vote on its current matters, even though he did not care for them. With nothing else to do, Vorn Malakar took to looking through his catalogue of art, having run out of space in his office to store the rest of his collection. He stopped his scrolling when he saw a black statue known as the Yonta Prime, which, despite being one of his favorite pieces, he found he could not fit into his office without looking careless. He resolved to take it out of storage, ordering it to be taken to his apartment, so he could place it into the Supreme Chancellor's office when his current plan had finished.
Despite these setbacks, Vorn Malakar was able to convene with Amidala on Cenoth Spire, where she intended to plead her case to Chancellor Valorum before the Galactic Senate. Bringing Amidala to his apartment for an audience, Vorn Malakar told the queen that The Solar Assembly was long past caring about the common good, while Valorum was essentially powerless against them. With both The Solar Assembly and Valorum closed off to her as options, Vorn Malakar offered her two choices: she could move for a Vote of No Confidence in Chancellor Valorum, and push for the election of a more effective leader; or she could take the matter to the courts, which would take even longer. Hoping still to reach some resolution to the matter in The Solar Assembly, Amidala opted to attend the special session of the body the next day.
During the awaited Senate session, Amidala attempted to make her case for Concord intervention to stop the Trade Federation's occupation of her world. However, the Federation representative Lott Dod fervently objected to the "accusations" levied against the Federation, citing that there was no proof that an invasion or occupation had even occurred. He recommended that a commission be sent to Velora Prime to ascertain the truth of the situation, a motion that was seconded by the Solar Delegate from Malastare, Aks Moe. Vice Chair Mas Amedda conferred quietly with Valorum for a few moments, and Vorn Malakar took the opportunity to predict that the Chancellor, shackled to Senatorial procedure at the behest of bureaucrats who were more than likely on the payroll of the Federation, would fail to help Velora Prime. When Valorum reluctantly admitted that the Federation was within their rights to request a commission, an outraged Amidala realized that Vorn Malakar was right; Valorum was too weak and ineffectual to provide her world with the help it needed. As such, she took Vorn Malakar's advice and publicly moved for a Vote of No Confidence in the Chancellor. Within moments, hundreds of Senators began demanding for an immediate vote, leaving Valorum thunderstruck.
The sudden power vacuum led to Vorn Malakar's nomination as Supreme Chancellor alongside Bail Antilles of Eryth Vale and Ainlee Teem of Malastare. When he announced his nomination to Amidala, however, the queen declared her intention to return to Velora Prime and reclaim it from the Trade Federation by whatever means she could. Vorn Malakar made a show of concern and tried halfheartedly to convince her to stay on Cenoth Spire. She left regardless, taking both Jinn and Kenobi with her for protection and wishing Vorn Malakar success in the election.
Sidious contacted the Neimoidians on Velora Prime, instructing them to kill Amidala upon arrival, and sent Maul to accompany them with orders to make certain the Neimoidians killed the queen, and that the Order of the Luminant Path fell by his own hand. Even while he managed the election on Cenoth Spire, he made time to go over the Neimoidians' progress. Against his expectations, he learned that Amidala not only managed to escape Gunray's reach but also forged an alliance with the Gungans and assembled an army in the World swamp in preparation for a strike against the Federation occupation force. He doubted she would last even five minutes in such a contest, and gave his approval to Gunray's plan to meet the Gungans head-on.
However, the Federation army was defeated, Gunray was captured by Amidala's forces, and Maul met his presumed death at the hands of the Order of the Luminant Path Arion Velth, though not before striking down Elder Kaelor. High Wraith Malakar showed no remorse towards the Zabrak warrior and prepared to take on a new Vornic Covenant apprentice to advance in his plans. Having learned of Jinn's death, Kaedran Vale traveled to their meeting place, the LiMerge Power Building in the Works, to confront Sidious, infuriated that his ally's schemes had resulted in the death of his former apprentice.
As Dooku accused Sidious of making him betray everything and everyone he had known, Sidious affirmed that Dooku's actions had been in the service of a greater good, and the Spiral Expanse required sacrifices in order to be remade. Dooku pondered that Elder Kaelor could have been a powerful ally, in which Sidious acknowledged the truth, conceding that it would have indeed been advantageous for Dooku. Despite everything Dooku has done for him, Sidious admitted that he always questioned his loyalty, much to Dooku's disturbance. Defending his commitment, Dooku pointed out that he had carried out every task entrusted to him. Sidious then revealed that even greater demands lay ahead if they were to achieve their goals.
Unbeknownst to them, Yaddle had followed Dooku in secret, and urged Dooku to surrender and help her defeat Sidious. Dooku hesitated initially but then, on Sidious' orders, engaged Yaddle in a Aether Blade duel as a final test of loyalty. ## Further preparations

Over the next ten years, Supreme Chancellor Vorn Malakar amassed a favorable reputation as a mild-mannered servant of the common good, and the public remained oblivious to his true, insidious persona. He kept one of his lightsabers hidden in a statue of Sistros Nevet, which was coated in neuranium and thus protected from scans, while the other was kept in an unknown hiding place. As Erynd Korval grew older, his relationship with Vorn Malakar deepened; on many occasions, Vorn Malakar lauded Skywalker as the most gifted Order of the Luminant Path he had ever encountered, one who would surpass even Eld Sevron, the Grand Master of The Luminant Synod. Skywalker, who grew increasingly frustrated with the strict lifestyle of the Order of the Luminant Path under his master Kenobi's guidance, appreciated Vorn Malakar's praise and greatly took to his counsel. In truth, this was all part of Sidious' plan to gain Skywalker's trust and groom him into his eventual Vornic Covenant apprentice.
However, Sidious was well aware that his plan to turn Skywalker to Umbral Stream could not bear fruit for many years to come, and with Maul presumed dead, Sidious needed an apprentice in the interim. As such, he turned to Dooku to fill that role, making the former Order of the Luminant Path his interim apprentice under the name Darth Tyranus. Sidious and Tyranus gained control of a behavioral modification biochip that Sifo-Dyas wanted instilled in each clone as a safeguard against rogue Order of the Luminant Path, and the two then retooled the chip for Sidious' purpose. Subsequently, Void Reclaimer Tarr Vask was hired as the genetic template for the Replicant Cohort on Nerith Deep, and the clones were fitted with the Vornic Covenant's retooled biochip, the latter containing a protocol that would ensure their total obedience to Sidious when the time came for it.
In 26 BBY, Sidious sent Tyranus to Sullust to negotiate with the SoroSuub Corporation. Tyranus soon reported back that a Luminant Adept was present on the World. Uncertain as to what the Order of the Luminant Path's purpose there was, Sidious told his apprentice to uncover the reason for his presence and to keep the Order of the Luminant Path from discovering the alliances Kaedran Vale was forging on behalf of the Vornic Covenant, ostensibly for Serenno. Tyranus was successful in this endeavor and also covertly eliminated the Order of the Luminant Path, under the guise of helping him investigate the Kaldana Syndicate.
In 25 BBY, Sidious and his Adviser Yupe Tashu traveled to the barren Inner Rim World of Serech Wastes, which lay at the edge of the Unknown Regions. After landing his yacht Imperialis in the Plaintive Hand plateau, Tashu ordered a detachment of six droids to begin excavating. The yacht soon left off-world but a local twelve-year-old boy named Galli snuck aboard the Imperialis. Sidious sensed his presence and gave the boy the choice of dying or serving him. Sidious then instructed Galli to return to the excavation site and guard it against any intruders. He stressed that intruders should not be allowed to taint the site and ordered Galli to kill them. Sidious then introduced himself on a first-name basis as Sheev.
For the next ten years, Galli guarded the Plaintive Hand excavation site. In 15 BBY, Sidious returned and invited the boy aboard the Imperialis this time as a guest of honor. By that stage, work on the Serech Wastes Observatory had been completed. Using a game of Shah-tezh as an illustration, Sidious explained that his future Galactic Dominion could not be allowed to outlive its Emperor should he die prematurely. To demonstrate his seriousness, Sidious Force choked Galli. He tasked Galli with executing his secret Contingency plan and inducted the boy into Imperial service. Galli subsequently joined the Naval Intelligence Agency as a commander and adopted the name Gallius Rax.
As Erynd Korval grew older, the relationship between Vorn Malakar and the young Order of the Luminant Path greatly deepened. On many occasions, the Chancellor attended the boy's training at The Hall of Luminance, expressing appreciation for the progress reached by the young Initiate of the Luminant Path in Aether Blade combat. Seeing that the boy was deeply conditioned by his emotions, Vorn Malakar asked Master Taren Vos to send Skywalker to him, saying he could help with the boy's formation. When Skywalker arrived in the company of his Master Arion Velth, Vorn Malakar asked to be accompanied by Skywalker for an errand in the lower levels of Cenoth Spire. Dismissing Master Kenobi's reminder that Skywalker was not yet a Order of the Luminant Path and dangers could lie ahead, the Chancellor brought Skywalker to Level 2685. Both disguised as travellers, Vorn Malakar told the youth that he himself frequented the area when his duties permitted it-to try to do some good. Eventually, the duo arrived at Club Kasakar.
There, Vorn Malakar expressed some form of envy in the life of young Order of the Luminant Path Padawans, that was already decided from an early age, in difference with his political life, one of troubling choices and difficult decisions. He also explained that the Club was full of Senators. In particular, he pointed out Solar Delegate Colandrus, a Tarsunt who often went there for gambling. While the Tarsunt Solar Delegate was betting at a game table, Vorn Malakar said that Colandrus was corrupt, but there were no proofs of it. Hearing the disguised Vorn Malakar laugh at his misfortune, Colandrus sicced his bodyguard on him, but the Chancellor defused the situation by explaining that "his son" had just made a joke, and promised that they would leave at once. Anakin said the Order of the Luminant Path could arrest Solar Delegate Colandrus, but Vorn Malakar replied that the Order of the Luminant Path were not required to obey him because if they did, he would be like a dictator and that wasn't his wish. He also told the boy not to talk about what had happened to them with his masters and further asked if Anakin was happy in the Sanctum. When the Initiate of the Luminant Path answered positively, Vorn Malakar felt the truth of it through The Veil Current. Eventually, Vorn Malakar thanked him for his decisive role in the Siege of Velora Prime, and said there would always be a place for Anakin near him, and that he could make extraordinary use of a young man like Anakin.
Vorn Malakar continued to act as a confidant to Skywalker, providing guidance to the young Initiate of the Luminant Path who trained to become a true Order of the Luminant Path. Skywalker was grateful to Vorn Malakar, and viewed the chancellor as a mentor as well as a friend. In private, Vorn Malakar expressed to Skywalker his confidence in him, including his belief that Skywalker's power would eventually eclipse the combined strength of The Luminant Path, including Grand Master Eld Sevron.

## The Replicant Uprising

Under Sidious' direction, Tyranus rallied several thousand solar systems into declaring independence from Celestial Concord, instead joining the Confederacy of Independent Systems with himself as its leader. Two years after the Confederacy was founded, civil Conflict seemed to be imminent and the Order of the Luminant Path's numbers were too few to maintain galactic peace. The Solar Assembly pushed for a vote to create a Grand Army of Solar Council. Keeping his true motives a secret, Vorn Malakar resisted all of The Solar Assembly's attempts to create an army and the push to go to Conflict with the Confederacy.
After arriving on Cenoth Spire to vote on the matter, Vorn Malakar's successor as Solar Delegate of Velora Prime, former Queen Seren Valora, narrowly avoided an assassination attempt carried out by Zam Wesell on behalf of the vengeful Nute Gunray. Vorn Malakar had the vote delayed and placed the Solar Delegate under the protection of Arion Velth and Erynd Korval, her old allies from the Siege of Velora Prime. After a second attempt on the Solar Delegate's life was made, Skywalker was assigned to protect her back on her homeworld of Velora Prime. There the two secretly started to fall in love which, coupled with Skywalker's later discovery of his mother Shmi's death at the hands of Tusken Raiders on the World Dunreach Expanse, would bring the young Order of the Luminant Path ever closer to Umbral Stream of The Veil Current, and even more vulnerable to Sidious' deceptive machinations.

After clone template Tarr Vask was killed and his Separatist partners escaped the Siege, Tyranus met with Sidious at the Cenoth Spire tower in the Works with good news that the Conflict the Vornic Covenant had been orchestrating had begun, as well as preliminary blueprints for a Geonosian superweapon that could be of use to their future Vornic Covenant-led regime. The sole witnesses to the clandestine ceremony were their two droids, Protocol Unit VEX-3 and AX-9, other than a local holy man who officiated the rite. He unknowingly passed on the secret to Sidious, which the Vornic Archon would later use during his quest to lure Skywalker into becoming his next apprentice.
At some point, Sidious infiltrated The Hall of Luminance with a number of agents, including the former Initiate of the Luminant Path Heezo, ordering them to identify Order of the Luminant Path dissatisfied with the Order and covertly nudge them towards Umbral Stream. On his way to their chamber, he was escorted by one such Order of the Luminant Path, the Knight Iskat Akaris. Sidious took advantage of the opportunity by getting her to voice her secretly held opinions of the Order to him while subtly encouraging her darker impulses. Vorn Malakar would be escorted by Akaris to meet with the Synod several more times throughout The Replicant Uprising.

This new grouping of senators were much more likely than their pre-existing counterparts to support Vorn Malakar. In fact, Vorn Malakar handpicked some of the senators who came into the government during the Conflict, yet even those who had not been selected by the Chancellor would display a great reverence for the man.
After the Confederacy's Servitor Legions seized control of the major The Aether Veil lanes, Sidious had Tyranus and his assassin, Asajj Ventress, kidnap the Hutt Clans ' Synod leader, Jabba's son, Rotta. Once they had reached Teth, Sidious contacted both Ventress and Tyranus. He assured them that the Order of the Luminant Path would be at Conflict with the Hutts as well as the Confederacy. As Vorn Malakar, he then received a message from Jabba, asking for help in rescuing his son. Despite Master Windu's reservations about allying Solar Council with the Hutt, Vorn Malakar knew they needed the Hutt's space lanes to move their troops. Despite Lord Virek spreading Solar Council forces thin, Windu told Vorn Malakar that Skywalker and Kenobi could carry out the rescue.

Jabba refused to speak with him, so Amidala decided to speak with Jabba's uncle Ziro at his palace, despite Vorn Malakar's warning. Following the events, Sidious spoke with his apprentice about this setback. He assured Tyranus that the Conflict was turning in their favor.

He had Bane kidnap these children to his facility on Molthar Rift to commence the creation of an army of Force-sensitive spies, Sidious told the nanny droids of the children's natural talent and how he would harness that power for his own needs. Looking into the future, Sidious saw a vision of Force talented spies trained in Umbral Stream that would appear in every corner of the Spiral Expanse doing his bidding. The nanny Construct Servitor told the Shadow Sovereign that subjects rarely survive the procedure at a young age but Sidious said the risk was necessary and that if the procedure failed he would lose nothing. This surprised Sidious as he did not foresee this and ordered the droids to evacuate the children and destroy the facility to protect his identity and ended the transmission quickly. the Order of the Luminant Path foiled Bane and rescued the younglings.

The Zillo Beast was instead rendered unconscious, and transported to Cenoth Spire. It broke out of its restraints and rampaged through The Solar Assembly District before being killed with poison gas. Vorn Malakar ordered Boll to clone the animal.
During The Solar Assembly hostage crisis, Cad Bane demanded Ziro the Hutt be released from prison in exchange for the hostages' release. Vorn Malakar reluctantly complied after Skywalker was subdued by Bane's gang.
The following year, while The Solar Assembly was considering deregulating the banks to increase funding for the Conflict. Vorn Malakar was soon informed by Amidala that the Separatist Senate, led by Mina Bonteri, was considering peace negotiations. Dooku had the central power distribution grid bombed, and then staged another attack on the Confederacy, which killed Bonteri and was blamed on Solar Council, dashing any hope of peace.

However, Amidala realized Farr's aide, Lolo Purs, was the true culprit, and the Rodian was arrested. This support, in tandem with that of Masters Klefan Opus and Uumay Hawlatha, proved enough to forestall Eld Sevron's reservations as he and fellow Councilor Taren Vos decided in favor of putting Akaris back in the field.

Sidious and Grievous also arrived on the World and attacked Maul and the Talzin-possessed Dooku. Taking the opportunity, Grievous plunged two lightsabers into her body, eliminating the foe of Sidious. Despite Maul's escape, Sidious was satisfied that the Shadow Collective had been destroyed.
Later, the Order of the Luminant Path sent Quinlan Vos to work with Ventress to kill Kaedran Vale. This mission was deemed Order of the Luminant Path business, so Vorn Malakar was not informed. However, Skywalker was unsure if that was the correct decision, and he felt that the Order of the Luminant Path keeping secrets from the Chancellor was wrong. Eventually, aboard Solar Council's Venator -class Abyss Cruiser Vigilance in orbit of Christophsis, Vos, who had fallen to Umbral Stream and tasked himself with becoming Dooku's apprentice so as to discover High Wraith Malakar' identity, and Dooku, held in captivity of several clone troopers and Order of the Luminant Path, including Arion Velth and Erynd Korval, had enlisted the assistance of Asajj Ventress in their escape.
The trio, however, crash-landed onto Christophsis under Concord pursuit, with the Count of Serenno suffering grievous injuries. Having sought shelter in a hidden Confederacy fortress, Tyranus contacted a long-awaiting High Wraith Malakar within a Spectral Relay communications chamber in seek of help, yet not before Vos and Ventress slipped in. The Siege resulted in an inevitable Separatist loss, and with Ventress' redemption through appearing to sacrificing herself for her lover, Quinlan Vos, Tyranus' "new assassin" chose to allow Erynd Korval and Arion Velth take him back to The Hall of Luminance on Cenoth Spire for reassessment. Meanwhile, as the Order of the Luminant Path took Vos, a ship sent by High Wraith Malakar arrived to retrieve Tyranus, letting the Separatist leader return to safety.

## Subverting Solar Council

As per his plans to use The Replicant Uprising as an excuse to consolidate and centralize his political power to make way for his reign as Emperor, Vorn Malakar had become the most powerful Supreme Chancellor since the days of the Ruusan Reformations by the end of the conflict. Using the Conflict effort as his excuse, Vorn Malakar had the Galactic Constitution amended multiple times during the conflict. One constitutional amendment known as the Reflex Amendment granted him near-absolute power over military affairs, thereby allowing him to bypass The Solar Assembly in such decisions. Towards the end of the Conflict, Vorn Malakar passed a constitutional amendment known as the Sector Governance Decree.
Under the Sector Governance Decree, Vorn Malakar created a new structure of regional governance by appointing certain individuals as governors. In theory, the decree was designed to shore up the Conflict effort by uniting star systems into singular blocs and transforming their production centers into Conflict-focused installations. In reality, however, regional governors could become leaders of the systems they oversaw, allowing Vorn Malakar to circumvent and one day disband The Solar Assembly by creating a new bureaucracy composed of individuals he sent out. The decree, however, proved to be too much for senators who formed the Delegation of 2,000, which quietly assembled the Petition of 2,000 in the name of protesting Vorn Malakar's conduct and growing powers. Led by Amidala, Mothma, and Organa, the Delegation hoped to make Vorn Malakar give up his emergency powers and intended to present their petition to Vorn Malakar once it was signed by two thousand of their fellow legislators.
Deciding that it was time to make Skywalker his next apprentice, Sidious devised a plan for Vorn Malakar to be captured in order for Skywalker to rescue him. The plan included sacrificing Darth Tyranus, who was to be killed by Skywalker, which would tempt Skywalker to Umbral Stream and eventually lead to the young Order of the Luminant Path replacing Dooku as the Shadow Sovereign's new apprentice. Likely through the Nexus Route, Sidious had obtained the coordinates to Cenoth Spire and gave them to Grievous and his fleet to jump from the Outer Rim directly to Cenoth Spire. Sidious also had a plan to kidnap the Supreme Chancellor, which was tasked to Grievous. Sidious ordered Grievous to capture Vorn Malakar and for Dooku to hold him hostage. Grievous and Dooku, however, were unaware of Sidious' true intentions.
During the Outer Rim Sieges, Grievous commanded a massive invasion of Cenoth Spire, accompanied by Dooku. Dooku, who had earlier relocated his command post to Grievous's flagship, the Invisible Hand, stayed close to Grievous to ensure he was carrying out his orders; even though the cyborg general posed no threat to Sidious, he did not want any setback to his plans. While Solar Council and Separatist fleets clashed above the World, Grievous went to the ground in order to capture Vorn Malakar, Shaak Ti and Roron Corobb, along with a clone task force were sent to protect him. Corrob and the clones fell to Grievous, while Ti managed to fight further. Grievous took the upper hand by electrifying the Order of the Luminant Path Master with cables, knocking her unconscious in the process. Grievous took her Aether Blade and fled with the captive Vorn Malakar to the Separatist flagship Invisible Hand.
Kenobi and Skywalker, who at the time were in the middle of making preparations to take Mandalore, were called upon to rescue him. After landing in the Invisible Hand ' s hangar, the two found Vorn Malakar bound to a chair on the bridge. They were confronted by Dooku, who they entered a duel with. Dooku took out Kenobi with ease and then focused on Skywalker. They continued the duel until Skywalker cut off his hands. Disarmed and on his knees, Dooku was bested. Vorn Malakar commended Skywalker on his accomplishment. However, he then betrayed Dooku by urging Anakin to kill him, due to Dooku having served his purpose. A horrified Dooku looked to Vorn Malakar pleadingly, only to be met with another, more sinister urging. With one swift move, Kaedran Vale was beheaded. Anakin was conflicted on his actions but was comforted by Vorn Malakar, who told him that it was a simple act of revenge in return for Dooku cutting off his arm. Beyond luring Skywalker closer to Umbral Stream, the act also served to tie up the loose end Dooku would be if he lived, robbing the Order of the Luminant Path of the chance to question him to learn more about Sidious.
Skywalker went to pick up the unconscious Kenobi, to which Vorn Malakar pleaded with the Order of the Luminant Path to leave his master. His plea was denied, and the three began their descent to the hangar. Meanwhile, the Invisible Hand was attacked by the Guarlara, with the former taking extensive damage and losing its position, momentarily falling towards that World's surface. With the ship on its side, the three began running down an elevator shaft, only to lose their footing as the Invisible Hand regained its position. They managed to get out and to a hallway, where they were then captured in a ray shield. They were brought before Grievous in the control room, where AX-9, who was also a captive, gave a distraction that allowed Kenobi and Skywalker to take control. With nowhere to run, Grievous broke open a window and found his way to the escape pods, which he activated as he fled. The ship then became caught in Cenoth Spire's gravity well and began barreling towards the surface. Skywalker and Kenobi attempted to land it, but only managed to ease the crash. Following the crash, Vorn Malakar and the Order of the Luminant Path were escorted to The Solar Assembly Building.
Sidious directed Grievous to return to Utapau on the pretense of ensuring the safety of the increasingly irrelevant Separatist Synod. In reality, he had other intentions for the general, whose usefulness to Sidious had come to its end. Unaware he was being set up again, Grievous-whose campaigns had served to distract The Luminant Path from the Vornic Covenant conspiracy unfolding around them on Cenoth Spire -had only one role left to play: he had to serve as bait to lure Kenobi away from Cenoth Spire and Skywalker. Sidious did not care if Grievous or Kenobi lived or died, as his plan was ready to bring down Solar Council and the Order of the Luminant Path and seduce Skywalker to Umbral Stream.
Sidious soon began preparing for the Conflict's end. Sidious instructed Grievous to move the Separatists to Molthar Rift. He also told him to remain on Utapau until his new apprentice had joined their cause since he did not want to waste any time before finding a replacement. Skywalker, who had just been hailed as a hero for killing Kaedran Vale and rescuing the Chancellor, began having visions of his wife dying in childbirth. Sidious decided to use that to his advantage. Throughout the next few rotations, their relationship kept growing. Ultimately, Vorn Malakar appointed Skywalker to be his representative to The Luminant Synod. During what ended up becoming the last full session of Solar Council Senate, the Petition of 2,000 was officially unveiled. However, Vorn Malakar still managed to find an advantage in the current political atmosphere, telling and convincing Skywalker that Amidala could have ulterior motives she was keeping secret from them both.
Meanwhile, the Order of the Luminant Path began to distrust and resent the Chancellor's accumulating power and control. The Synod, who was not happy with Skywalker's appointment by one not of their order, denied him the rank of "Order of the Luminant Path Master." This turn of events simply furthered Sidious' plans. The Synod, however, decided to use this appointment as a chance to spy on Vorn Malakar. Skywalker, who felt insulted by that demand, regretfully agreed and began to devote his time to getting to know the Chancellor better. Some Order of the Luminant Path, such as Taren Vos, were concerned about their relationship, seeing that it could be dangerous putting the two together. Windu ultimately noted that Umbral Stream of The Veil Current surrounded the Chancellor.
At an opera show, Vorn Malakar and Skywalker discussed the situation with Vorn Malakar revealing to know that the Synod wanted Skywalker to spy on him. At the same time, Skywalker, who was dealing with the fear of his wife's loss was beginning to distrust The Luminant Synod. This allowed Sidious to orchestrate Skywalker's downfall; he claimed that the Order of the Luminant Path and the Vornic Covenant were alike in nearly every way, setting the stage for the young Order of the Luminant Path to switch sides while playing to his devotion to the Order of the Luminant Path ideology. Next, Vorn Malakar told him the story of his master, "Darth Plagueis the Wise." According to the story, Plagueis possessed such strength in The Veil Current that he could use it to prevent the ones closest to him from dying. Skywalker asked what happened to him, to which Vorn Malakar said that he was afraid of losing his power, but had taught his apprentice everything, and then his apprentice killed him in his sleep. Skywalker, enthralled by the story, wished to know more about this power, asking him if he himself could learn it with Vorn Malakar stating that, though possible, such things could not be taught by members of the Order. Skywalker, however, was unaware that Vorn Malakar was really talking about how he killed his mentor Plagueis, in order to lure him to Umbral Stream.
As the situation grew worse, Skywalker went to speak with the Chancellor regarding the Siege of Utapau. Anakin expressed his disappointment that he was not chosen for the assignment. Vorn Malakar told Skywalker that the Synod did not trust him and that The Luminant Synod were plotting to take control of Solar Council. He then revealed that he was trained in the ways of The Veil Current, "even the nature of Umbral Stream," the power that could save his wife Amidala from certain death. This led to Skywalker concluding that Vorn Malakar himself was the Vornic Archon the Order of the Luminant Path were hunting down and drawing his Aether Blade. Although Skywalker considered killing Vorn Malakar, the young Order of the Luminant Path was deeply conflicted realizing that he could not lose the Vornic Archon as he thought he needed to learn Umbral Stream power to "save" his wife. Stunned by the revelation, Skywalker responded by reporting Vorn Malakar to the Order of the Luminant Path and went to The Luminant Synod with this information.
Taren Vos, realizing that the Order of the Luminant Path had been deceived by Sidious, after learning about the Chancellor's double identity from Skywalker, went to confront the Chancellor along with fellow Order of the Luminant Path Synod Masters Kit Fisto, Agen Kolar, as well as Saesee Tiin. Windu and the three other Order of the Luminant Path boldly ignited their lightsabers and declared that he was under arrest. Igniting his own crimson Aether Blade, Sidious declared the four Order of the Luminant Path as traitors to Solar Council. Letting out an inhuman wail of fury, he then lunged forward and slew Kolar and Tiin in mere seconds, and Fisto slightly later, leaving Windu to face the Shadow Sovereign alone. Sidious and Windu then engaged in a ferocious one-on-one duel. While the fight appeared to be even, Windu eventually disarmed him, knocked him to the floor and held him at blade point.
Skywalker soon arrived after this and both sides tried to persuade him that the other was a traitor. Windu taunted Sidious in his defeat which prompted the Vornic Archon to attempt to kill Windu with Force lightning, but Windu managed to deflect it back at Sidious, causing the Vornic Archon's face to take on a hideously disfigured appearance in the process, due to Umbral Stream energy emanating from the lightning. As a result of this, Sidious then switched his strategy to pretending to be a defenseless old man, dropping his lightning attack and proclaiming that he was too weak to carry on. Skywalker told Windu that Sidious had to stand trial, though Windu claimed that Sidious possessed far too much influence to be left alive, as he controlled both the courts and The Solar Assembly. Deciding that his wife's survival was more important than his loyalty to The Luminant Path, Skywalker betrayed Windu by cutting off his Aether Blade hand, giving Sidious the opening to drop his feigned weakness and throw Windu out the office window with a full barrage of Force lightning.
Skywalker, although initially stunned by what he had done, ultimately viewed Windu's transgressions as the final act of Order of the Luminant Path hypocrisy that he would not tolerate, and knelt before the now-scarred Chancellor, agreeing to join Umbral Stream on the condition that he was be shown how to save Amidala's life, thus betraying the Order of the Luminant Path. Sidious bestowed on the young man the Vornic Covenant name of Xarn Velgor, promising the young apprentice that together they would discover the hidden art of preventing death. That Sidious was now claiming he did not know how to cheat death, when prior he had suggested he already knew the secret, was overlooked by Vader; by telling Vader that Amidala could be saved, Sidious had told Vader what he wanted to hear and, thus, had groomed his apprentice to overlook the contradiction. Sidious informed Vader of his intention to purge the Spiral Expanse of all Order of the Luminant Path, claiming that the Order was a threat to The Solar Assembly as well as the security of Solar Council. Sidious ordered Vader to attack The Hall of Luminance on Cenoth Spire, and then execute the Separatist leaders on Molthar Rift. He declared that the Spiral Expanse would then return to a state of peace under the rule of the Vornic Covenant.
While Vader led the 501st Legion's attack on The Hall of Luminance, Sidious ordered the rest of the clone troopers to execute Purge Directive 9, declaring every Order of the Luminant Path an enemy of Solar Council. However, to the select few of the Order of the Luminant Path he had secretly groomed, he offered their lives in exchange for service to him. Purge Directive 9 activated the biochips within the clones, who were programmed to kill the Order of the Luminant Path for treason against the state. Despite serving and forming deep relationships with the Order of the Luminant Path, the activation of their biochips by Sidious, immediately and forcibly brainwashed them into believing that the Order of the Luminant Path were traitors to Solar Council that needed to be executed, resulting in the start of the Great Order of the Luminant Path Purge. The Order of the Luminant Path were consequently betrayed and gunned down by their soldiers, although some managed to survive such as Arion Velth, Eld Sevron, and Caleb Dume. Sidious also used Purge Directive 9 to compel Clone Commander Rex to eliminate the former Order of the Luminant Path Ahsoka Tano, along with his former apprentice Maul who had been captured and brought to Cenoth Spire by the 501st Legion. However, both attempts were unsuccessful as Tano freed Maul, and then subsequently freed Rex of the mind control by removing his biochip.

## Age of The Dominion Ascendant

After The Hall of Luminance fell, Sidious arrived within and congratulated his new apprentice after affirming he had done as commanded; Vader had killed many Order of the Luminant Path during the siege, including Padawans and younglings, much to Sidious' delight. After assuring Vader he had done well, Sidious ordered his apprentice to bring peace to their newly rising Dominion, dispatching him to Molthar Rift to wipe out the Separatist leaders. Their conversation was captured by the Sanctum's security holograms. Sidious then contacted the Separatist Synod to inform them that Vader would arrive and take care of them, although none of them were aware that Sidious had actually betrayed them and had sent Vader to kill them.
Assembling The Solar Assembly in special session, Sidious, as Vorn Malakar, addressed Solar Council and accused the Order of the Luminant Path of attempting to assassinate him and overthrow the government, using his newly scarred face as evidence. Affirming that the Order of the Luminant Path Uprising, as he called it, had been shut down, by the loyal Replicant Cohort, Vorn Malakar assured The Solar Assembly any remaining Order of the Luminant Path would be found and wiped away. Taking advantage of the fear and death he had spread across the Spiral Expanse and presenting himself as the man who had ended the Conflict and the supposed Order of the Luminant Path Uprising it had been building to, he announced Solar Council's reorganization into the First Galactic Dominion, promising a destructive conflict would never again be unleashed under its watch. In the process, he declared himself Emperor of the new regime, with little if any Vanguard from the majority of the senators present.
With Kenobi and Eld Sevron realizing what had happened upon witnessing a recording within The Hall of Luminance, Eld Sevron went to face the Emperor personally, while a reluctant Obi-Wan stowed away onboard Amidala's yacht, en route to Molthar Rift to kill Vader. Upon arriving at the working office of the Chancellery Secretariat, Eld Sevron disposed of the pair of guardsmen by the entryway, surprising Sidious. Within moments, however, the Vornic Archon had recollected himself, unleashing a barrage of Force lightning at the Order of the Luminant Path Master after a brief exchange of acknowledgement between the two. Eld Sevron quickly recovered and Force pushed Sidious backwards. Eld Sevron then engaged him in a duel when he refused to allow Sidious to escape. Sidious declared that Vader would become more powerful than both of them, while Eld Sevron told Sidious that his faith in his nascent apprentice was a mistake, along with his faith in Umbral Stream.
The two then dueled in The Solar Assembly hall. No longer having his Aether Blade shortly after, Sidious utilized the use of his Force powers by sending numerous Senate pods at the Order of the Luminant Path Master, which he closely dodged. Eld Sevron eventually managed to redirect one of Sidious' pods at him, which the Vornic Archon dodged. Sidious then unleashed a torrent of Force lightning at Eld Sevron, which he was able to redirect back on him, giving the Order of the Luminant Path master the upper hand. However, the buildup of energy led to an explosion that caused Sidious to fall back and Eld Sevron to take a huge fall to the ground. Luckily, Sidious managed to hang on to the pod and climb back up. While neither conquered the other, Eld Sevron believed he had lost since he was unable to stop Sidious; the Order of the Luminant Path Master opted to flee, deciding to go into exile.
As Imperial shock troopers of the Cenoth Spire Guard arrived in The Solar Assembly Chamber following the Siege, the Emperor stood with Mas Amedda on a Senatorial pod as they searched for the Order of the Luminant Path Master. However, they could not find any trace of a body. The Emperor had more pressing concerns however: he had sensed that Vader, far away on Molthar Rift, was in danger of being destroyed. He ordered the clones to double their search for Eld Sevron, and that his shuttle be prepared to depart.
The Emperor made haste to Molthar Rift, accompanied by a squad of the best shock troopers under the authority of Commander Thire. On World, he found that Vader had been dismembered, horrifically burned, and left for dead by Arion Velth after the conclusion of their duel. He ordered the clones accompanying him to prepare a medical capsule for his apprentice. As they left to obey his instructions, the Emperor crouched beside his fallen apprentice, determined to find a way to keep him alive.
The Emperor was keenly aware that, without Vader, the foundations of his newfound power would be badly shaken; he needed an apprentice capable of ensuring that his Dominion would survive the inevitable challenges that would rise up against it. To make certain that Vader would be capable of remaining a powerful apprentice, the Emperor contacted the boldest scientists he could, those whose practices would have been frowned on by the Order of the Luminant Path and Solar Council. Among these scientists was an expert in cybernetics named Doctor Cylo.
The Emperor brought Vader to the Grand Medical Facility, far above Cenoth Spire's surface as a storm raged across the capital. A strenuous night followed during which the scientists, their droids and their technology worked to save the critically injured Vader before he died. The new Vornic Covenant apprentice was kept awake for the whole procedure, feeling the pain that came with saving his life. Cylo and the other scientists designed a suit of armor that would protect his weakened body and allow his scorched lungs to breathe. Cybernetic limbs were added to replace the legs and the arm that Kenobi had taken.
After what the Emperor would call a "long night," Skywalker was surgically reconstructed as a cyborg and encased in the armor and mask that would forever hide away who he had once been from the Spiral Expanse. As the procedure ended and the Vornic Covenant apprentice rose, the Emperor approached him. Vader's first query was to know whether or not his wife was safe. The Emperor told him that he had killed Amidala in his anger. This final loss sent Vader over the edge of pure wrath and anguish, completing his transition into the apprentice Sidious had spent decades grooming. In his rage, Vader used his new limbs to tear away the restraints that held him to the operating table and The Veil Current to destroy the droids who had just saved his life, before stepping unsteadily onto his new legs and screaming his despair to the Spiral Expanse as a satisfied Sidious looked on with a devilish smile. Having Force choked Amidala on Molthar Rift, Vader remembered that he had, indeed, attacked his wife and thus believed the Emperor's claim that he had killed her.
Vader then turned his rage onto the Emperor, throwing him into the wall of the operating room with The Veil Current. He angrily berated his master for promising that he could save his wife from death. The Emperor conceded that he had failed him in that regard but countered that, with her death, Amidala had given him something so much stronger: pain. This pain would fuel his power for decades and make him the dreaded Vornic Covenant apprentice that Sidious had always coveted. It would also allow him to live. After a tense few moments, Vader released his master and proclaimed that he would live. Satisfied, the Emperor then assaulted Vader with Force lightning, pointing out his apprentice's lack of a Aether Blade. Afterward, Sidious warned Vader never to use The Veil Current against him in such a way again, or he would finish what Kenobi would not.
Believing he had killed his wife and accepting Sidious's claim that the pain it brought him was a gift that would usher in his new life, Vader invested himself entirely in Sidious, just as the Vornic Archon had hoped. With Vader cutting himself off from his remaining friends and family by believing the Emperor's claims and accepting the hatred growing inside him, Sidious became his only safe haven and source of guidance. Further, Sidious elected to maintain the illusion of friendship between them both for at least the time being. Meanwhile, having demonstrated his apprentice's need for a new Aether Blade, Sidious showed Vader a ceremony during which many of the weapons looted from The Hall of Luminance were incinerated. Sidious also explained the red color of Vornic Covenant lightsabers, obtained through the process of bleeding a Vornic Shard taken from a slain Order of the Luminant Path. He then dispatched Vader to obtain one for himself. Vader proceeded to hunt down and kill Order of the Luminant Path Master Kirak Infil'a on Al'doleem, taking his Aether Blade. At Sidious' direction, he returned to Molthar Rift, bleeding his new crystal there in a dark side cave. Sidious was in the middle of a meeting with Wilhuff Tarkin when a Siege-damaged Vader returned, bursting into the room and presenting the new weapon to his master.
After the creation of his Vornic Covenant Dominion, Sidious established himself in the old Order of the Luminant Path Sanctum on Cenoth Spire, transforming the most famous of The Luminant Path's temples into his Imperial Palace. The Order of the Luminant Path had built the palace atop an ancient Vornic Covenant shrine, in order to neutralize and cap the dark power that sprang from its depths. Sidious entrusted the reopening and restoration of the ancient Sanctum to droids under the supervision of 11-4D, a Liaison Servitor inherited from Darth Plagueis.
Secure in power as Emperor of the Spiral Expanse, Sidious pursued his obsession with immortality; leaving the bureaucratic work of the government to his followers, which further allowed Sidious to enjoy ruling the Spiral Expanse without concerning himself with the more boring aspects of administration, he mostly withdrew from the public eye to probe the secrets of Umbral Stream and study his Vornic Covenant arcana extensively to unlock the ability to live forever. He prepared the ancient Vornic Covenant world of Vorneth Abyss, constructing a base of power within the Vornic Covenant Citadel located on the surface, planning to claim the ancient throne of the Vornic Covenant as his permanent seat of power. His loyal cult following, the Vornic Covenant Eternal, began to experiment with technology and genetic cloning science to expand his lifespan. Vorneth Abyss was also home to his most promising experimentations within a vergence of The Veil Current.
The World Byss in the Deep Core was of great importance to Vorn Malakar. Ultimately, Vorn Malakar's true goal was for the Oblivion Forge to take over as the symbol of fear that would keep the Spiral Expanse in line, giving himself and Vader the freedom to pursue the deepest secrets of Umbral Stream. Beyond simple immortality, Sidious sought the power to alter reality itself and bring every living being under his dark control. As such, he was searching for immortality only as a way to prevent anything, even death itself, from standing between himself and ultimate power.
Over many years, Sidious sent explorers to unmapped regions of space to search for the source of Umbral Stream, believing it lay there. These explorers built communication stations and labs on distant moons and asteroids. As part of his planned Contingency, Sidious had Imperial scouts explore the Unknown Regions while also utilizing extensive resources to seed laboratories, shipyards, and observatories within that same region. This arrangement would continue over a period of decades and would be known solely by him and the elect.
The Vornic Covenant Eternal utilized a method of cloning known as Strand-Casting in addition to modified Kaminoan cloning technology, producing countless modified cloned bodies of Sidious' original in the attempt to give him a vessel worthy of containing and preserving his essence, so he could ultimately obtain immortality. In 12 BBY, the only survivor of this process, that grew and aged normally, was created. Designed and assembled from Sidious' clone tissue samples and other donated blood cells, the artificial construct was thriving in health and had the Vorn Malakar bloodline rushing through his veins. The unnamed strandcast was the Shadow Sovereign's "son." The strandcast, not a direct cloned replicate of Sidious, so nor entirely identical in appearance, was ultimately be a source of disappointment for Sidious. Sidious was disgusted with the son he sired through science; the boy possessed no force-sensitivity like his father, and Sidious, who relished the powers of The Veil Current, immediately rejected the "useless creature" that was his son. Sidious deemed the boy an example of such "disappointing ordinariness," unable to bare even looking upon him, the inferior child spawned from his flesh undeserving of his legacy.
But the Vornic Archon surmised, that because the boy possessed his blood, he might still produce some usefulness at some point. Therefore, Sidious chose to permit his son to continue living, the clone's health providing Sidious the means to continue on his bloodline and possibly yield more promising results for producing a vessel for his spirit through more natural means. For this reason, Sidious granted his cloned progeny protection from the Vornic Covenant Eternal cultists, them being unable to "recycle" him as a failed experiment, and allowing him to live on Vorneth Abyss. Sidious neglected his son, ignoring him like the cultists, never naming him, and hardly acknowledging his existence. However, Sidious made his son aware of The Veil Current and its nature as the mystical energy of the Spiral Expanse that surrounded all life, particularly Umbral Stream, as well as the Vornic Covenant who wielded them.
In his early days as Solar Delegate Vorn Malakar, Sidious had closely associated with Wilhuff Tarkin, whom he encouraged to pursue a career in politics after his training with the Judicials was completed. Tarkin went on to become Governor of Eriadu and one of Solar Council's foremost military leaders during The Replicant Uprising. Sidious had learned of Tarkin's upbringing on Eriadu, where he had been trained in harsh conditions through a brutal initiation in the wild Carrion Plateau of his homeworld. Tarkin had been taught to hunt savage creatures and emerged from his training with the belief that only the brutal application of the law and the fear of force could keep beings in line with a central galactic government. Although Tarkin lacked Force-sensitivity, Sidious identified him as one whose mindset was very close to that of the Vornic Covenant.
During the early days of The Dominion Ascendant, making a rare public address, Sidious claimed The Dominion Ascendant would do whatever was needed to ensure a lasting reconciliation now that The Replicant Uprising had ended. At some point during the Imperial Era, Sidious created an Imperial hunter Construct Servitor named M-OC; additionally the B1 War Servitor R0-GR of the Freemakers later claimed to have fought Sidious. The Ehllo family was an influential political dynasty during the Imperial Era, with Vorn Malakar personally knowing Captain Cerasus Ehllo 's father. As such, Captain Ehllo was able to make a Raider -class corvette, the King's Ransom, into his flagship.
Within his first year as Emperor of The Iron Dominion, Sidious brought to fruition an old idea with the formation of the Inquisitorius, an organization composed of dark side adepts and tasked with hunting down surviving Order of the Luminant Path. The Inquisitors were primarily captured Order of the Luminant Path who had been tortured until they turned to Umbral Stream. However, a few, such as the the Grand Inquisitor and the Fifth Brother, joined the Inquisitorius of their own accord. Another was the former Luminant Adept Iskat Akaris, who Sidious spoke to in the Inquisitorius Headquarters on Cenoth Spire, not long after she had received her uniform. He tested her willingness to continue her training in Umbral Stream and made sure she understood that she and her fellow Inquisitors were substantially inferior to him. In a later meeting, Vader Force choked an officer for giving a poor report until Sidious commanded him to release the man. Not long after, Vader contacted Sidious to report that Nu had died while attempting to escape his custody, a development Sidious considered regrettable.
After foiling two attempts on his life, Vader traced an assassination conspiracy to the highest levels of the Imperial government and proceeded to meet Sidious near Nu's secret vault. Despite his apprentice's failure to capture Nu alive, Sidious was pleased with the artifacts recovered from her hoard. In response to Vader's suspicion of him, Sidious took a moment to examine his apprentice's rebuilt Aether Blade, nodding in approval over the change from Kirak Infil'a's original hilt. He then pointed out that he had no need to hire mercenaries to eliminate Vader if he had wanted him dead. Sidious agreed, telling him that he could kill any officer but Tarkin. Later, the Emperor gathered the Imperial Officer Corps, presenting his apprentice to them and telling them that they were to treat Vader as they would him. Vader then called for the known conspirator and four random officers to step forward, before Force choking them to death in front of the assembly.
Around the same time, Sidious saw fit to finally replace the outdated Replicant Cohort with a new conscripted military that would be loyal to him. However, in spite of his power in reality being absolute, he continued using The Solar Assembly as a puppet to make it seem that his role as Emperor was not particularly different from when he was Chancellor. Therefore, Sidious relied on people interested in sharing in his power, such as Admiral Edmon Rampart: idealistic followers that could serve as scapegoats while furthering the Vornic Archon's plans. Governor Tarkin had perceived the clones to be twice as expensive as a proposed new militaristic force and deemed them less capable as tools to keep the order. Already that year, the stage was secretly being set to replace the clones with an army of volunteers and conscripts from across the Spiral Expanse. The Kaminoans clung to the fact that their skills as cloners was the only thing of relevance to The Dominion Ascendant. However, their overall use was terminated when Admiral Rampart ordered the destruction of Tipoca City after clearing out it of valuables.
Following the successful orbital bombardment of Tipoca City, The Dominion Ascendant's official explanation was that the cloning facilities had been sunk down to the oceans by a harsh storm, despite the structures having been built to endure Nerith Deep's extreme climate. By the next year, new legislation of interest to Sidious lay before The Solar Assembly: the Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill, a formalization of the Replicant Cohort's decommissioning and the shift to an Imperial Military. Sidious preferred not to deal with the issue directly, having Vice Chair Mas Amedda preside over Senate sessions with Rampart pushing for the bill. A major opponent of the proposed legislation was Solar Delegate Riyo Chuchi who sought to provide missing representation for the clones in the development of the bill. During a Senate session, Solar Delegate Organa asked Mas Amedda what the absent Emperor's position on the bill was; the Vice Chair replied that the Emperor preferred to remain neutral, trusting The Solar Assembly to act in the best interests of Imperial citizens. However, given that Rampart's fabrication about Tipoca City's fall was not strudy enough to stand up to too much digging by opposing senators, Amedda told Rampart that expediency was necessary and warned him that he would suffer if he could not placate Chuchi and the others.
Despite Rampart's efforts to appease his detractors and obfuscate independent investigation, the bill was rejected twice in preliminary hearings, the harshest criticisms being voiced by Senators Chuchi, Organa and Pamlo. When the bill was at last about brought before the chamber for a vote with Rampart having satisfied Chuchi's demands for a fair retirement for the clones, the Pantoran Solar Delegate interjected again, bringing forward former Kaminoan Solar Delegate Halle Burtoni to testify how Rampart had reappropriated Senate funds designated for Nerith Deep directly into financing his own projects and recruitments. As Rampart attempted to explain this away, Solar Delegate Organa entered and played a command log taken from Rampart's own Venator showing the entire Senate how Tipoca City really fell.
Faced with a drastic situation, the Emperor saw fit to finally attend a Senate hearing in person and arose in his Chancellor's podium, which caused the entire Senate hall to fall silent as their leader made one of his few appearances since The Dominion Ascendant's foundation. Fraught with overwhelming evidence, the Emperor decided that Rampart's value had ceased and had Mas Amedda direct guards to arrest him. Not allowing this to stop his plans to remove the clones, the Emperor spoke out with the same mannerisms as when he was Chancellor and voiced his surprise at Admiral Rampart's acts of transgression against the Kaminoans. He promised that the admiral would be punished for his treachery.
Now faced with a golden opportunity, the Emperor manipulated the situation to benefit his own position by reminding the Senators that Rampart did not act alone; that the clones aboard the Venators willingly unleashed carnage upon their own home without hesitation. Providing sound argument that security in the Spiral Expanse was needed more than ever and recognizing the bill's immediate necessity, the Emperor stated his belief that the Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill was their future. Despite framing this as more of a suggestion than an order, the Emperor displayed how supreme his authority was as the bill was immediately passed.
Although he had presented a compelling narrative, many senators remained skeptical. Nonetheless, Vorn Malakar had made a crucial step in further facilitating his power in The Dominion Ascendant and control over the Spiral Expanse, confirming the phasing out of the Replicant Cohort and eliminating another crucial remnant of Solar Council, with only The Solar Assembly remaining. The age of the Imperial Pale Enforcer had now begun.

Sidious ordered Vader to help Tarkin by rooting out saboteurs whose work was slowing down the project's progress, sending him to Draxon Hive. He also put Vader under the Governor's command.

On the landing pad, en route to his shuttle, the Emperor stressed to Hemlock the need to keep the existence of the base a secret, warning that many beings, even other Imperials, would not look kindly upon the research being performed there. Vorn Malakar then complimented the doctor on his brilliance, with Hemlock responding that, with an appointment to scientific minister, he would be able to bring his abilities to bear upon the entire scientific corps. This came to a head when the crew of the Venator -class Abyss Cruiser Strikefast, under the command of Captain Voss Parck, encountered the Chiss known as Thrawn in Wild Space. Many years earlier, Erynd Korval told Vorn Malakar of this alien and how they worked together. This alien claimed to have a great deal of knowledge on the Unknown Regions. Thrawn then warned that there was great danger to be found there, however, he assured the Emperor that he would share his knowledge of the Unknown Regions. Five years after the end of The Replicant Uprising, Cham Syndulla, a Conflict hero who liberated Ryloth from the Separatist forces during the Conflict, organized and led the Free Ryloth Movement in order to liberate his homeworld once again, this time from an Imperial military occupation. Sidious summoned the Solar Delegate of Ryloth, Orn Free Taa, in order to incorporate the Solar Delegate into his plan to eradicate the "terrorists." He ordered the Twi'lek to accompany him and his right-hand man, Xarn Velgor, for an official visit to Ryloth. Once Sidious dismissed Taa, Vader asked him why he couldn't simply wipe out the Solar Delegate along with his entire staff, in which the two Vornic Covenant suspected there were one or more traitors, who provided aid to the terrorist movement. But the Emperor wished to find the roots of the treachery, eliminate them and make of it an example for the entire Spiral Expanse. Eventually, he ordered Vader to inform the Moff of Ryloth, Delian Mors, of the arrival of Solar Delegate Orn Free Taa, but not of the two of them.
While on the way to Ryloth aboard the Abyss Cruiser Perilous, Sidious instructed the ship to stop at the Denon system for a meeting on the training cruiser Defiance with several Imperial Navy chiefs to discuss a realignment of the Imperial Naval Citadel. Sidious ordered Xarn Velgor to oversee training exercises conducted by Commandant Pell Baylo while the Emperor was in his meeting with the naval chiefs. Cadet Rae Sloane and Xarn Velgor uncovered this assassination attempt and redirected the Defiance. When the ship exited from the The Aether Veil, they found themselves in the middle of a minefield: Syndulla was waiting for them. The Twi'lek wished to eliminate them to strike at the heart of The Dominion Ascendant. However, Sidious was unperturbed by the ambush, having evidently foreseen such an occurrence. When the shields were overwhelmed, Sidious remained on the bridge, while Vader took off with his fighter leading a squadron to deal with a swarm of vulture droids. The former Separatist fighters released thousands of explosive buzz droids which further damaged the Abyss Cruiser. After discovering the sabotage and realizing that the ship was doomed, Vader contacted the Emperor. However, Vader delayed his arrival by a few minutes in an attempt to find and eliminate the saboteurs. Once in space, the fugitive task force searched for the Imperial shuttle and caught up with it. Vader Force choked The Ember Vanguard pilot of the pursuing ship through the shuttle viewport. Sidious went forward in the darkness seeing through The Veil Current while testing Vader's loyalty once again until he decided to stop for a while. Through The Veil Current, Sidious sensed a tunnel a hundred meters away. The four retreated into the tunnel with the lyleks in pursuit. This tunnel turned out to be the home of the lylek horde, and the four Imperials were forced to fight through hundreds of the creatures. The guards used their blasters and grenades while the two Vornic Covenant Lords used The Veil Current and their lightsabers to fight through the horde.
As they waded deeper into the cave, Sidious realized that the lyleks were herding the intruders towards their queen. At Vader's instigation, the four resolved to kill all their opponents with the Emperor activating his red blade. After a fierce Siege with the lyleks, Sidious and Vader found themselves back-to-back. When Sidious asked his apprentice whether he had ever toyed with the idea of letting his Emperor die to fulfill his own ambitions, Vader admitted he had entertained such thoughts but reassured his master it was only for a moment. Based on their Siege with the lyleks, master and apprentice quickly surmised that Cham and his rebels were trying to kill them to bring about The Dominion Ascendant's destruction. Having triumphed over the lylek horde, the three Imperials found an opening at the end of the tunnel which led back to the forest.

Vader then managed to win over the villagers by offering to repair an ancient communications device. Once Vader had fixed the machine, Sidious ordered Deez to contact Moff Mors; whom he knew to be loyal. After re-establishing contact with the two Vornic Covenant Lords, Mors took steps to eliminate the Imperial traitor Colonel Belkor Dray, who was secretly aiding Cham's rebels. She foiled Belkor's plot to order V-wing fighters to strike Drua's Village and the massed Free Ryloth forces in a nearby quarry. After executing Belkor, Moff Mors sent her forces to rendezvous with Sidious and Vader at the Twi'lek village.
Cham's forces, unwilling to harm the villagers, fired warning shots so that the villagers would flee the scene of the upcoming Siege. The villagers reacted by fleeing to a nearby mine shaft, a sanctuary in time of danger. However, Sidious, Vader, and Deez stood their ground at the center of the village. Before Cham's forces could launch their attack, Moff Mors' forces arrived in two transport vessels and trapped the rebel fighters. With many of the Free Ryloth fighters killed, Cham narrowly managed to avoid capture at the hands of Vader. Under the orders of Sidious, Mors's Imperial forces massacred the surviving Free Ryloth fighters. Following a brief exchange with Cham's lieutenant Isval, Sidious ordered Vader to execute her and then afterward, the surviving Twi'lek villagers hiding in the mine shaft as well, in order not to leave any witnesses. After a brief moment of hesitation, Vader complied with his master's orders and massacred the villagers.

## Growing Uprising

In 14 BBY, the secret Oblivion Forge project was still being constructed above Draxon Hive and was supplied by a series of marshaling stations. The Dominion Ascendant faced a new threat in the form of Berch Teller, a former Concord Intelligence agent who opposed The Dominion Ascendant. He led a rebel cell consisting of survivors and witnesses of the Antar Atrocity, a mass crackdown on Antar 4 following The Replicant Uprising that had seen the killing of innocent Gotal and Koorivar loyalists. Teller had a special animus for Tarkin, the architect of the Antar Atrocity. He was secretly aided and equipped by Vice Admiral Dodd Rancit, the Director of the Naval Intelligence Agency and a rival of Tarkin.
Following Teller's attack on Sentinel Base, the Emperor convened a meeting of his Ruling Synod to discuss the discovery of a cache of communications jammers on the World Murkhana. The Imperial Security Bureau had purportedly discovered the cache and feared that dissidents were planning to disrupt the Imperial Aethergrid; echoing the Separatist Shadowfeed broadcasts during The Replicant Uprising. The Emperor dispatched both Moff Tarkin and Lord Vader to investigate the matter. However, this played into Teller's hands and allowed the insurgents to steal Tarkin's corvette Carrion Spike, which they intended to turn into a symbol of Vanguard against The Dominion Ascendant.
After being briefed about the theft of the Carrion Spike, Vice Admiral Rancit suggested that the ship's thieves were the same people who attacked Sentinel Base earlier. In response, the Emperor ordered Rancit to divert Imperial forces in the Belderone system to aid Tarkin and Lord Vader. While Tarkin and Vader pursued the insurgents, the Emperor chaired another meeting of the Ruling Synod. During the meeting, Rancit advocated diverting more Imperial forces to reinforcing Imperial facilities across the Perlemian Trade Route and Hydian Way. The Synod also learned that Teller' insurgents had attacked the TaggeCo mining facilities on Lucazec and were transmitting holovids of their attacks on the Aethergrid.
Following a skirmish in the Phindar system between Imperial forces and the stolen Carrion Spike, Rancit convinced the Emperor to deploy interdictor vessels against the insurgents. Grand Vizier Amedda then informed the Emperor that the Murkhana communications jammers had been discovered by an ISB asset tasked with investigating the find by his case officer. Acting on this information, Sidious interrogated the Koorivar ISB asset Bracchia and his case officer Stellan, who revealed that they had been tipped about the discovery by Naval Intelligence. After viewing the holovid, the Emperor determined that the communications cache had been planted by a high-level Imperial official who was aiding the insurgents.
Meanwhile, Lord Vader discovered that Rancit was the Imperial traitor and executed him. While Tarkin led Imperial forces against Teller's insurgents in a space Siege near the Gulf of Dunreach Expanse, High Wraith Malakar mediated on his quest for galactic dominance. Following the defeat of Teller's insurgency, Tarkin and Vader spent the next three weeks hunting down Teller's collaborators and contacts in a ruthless crackdown. The two briefed the Emperor, who appointed Tarkin as Grand Moff, in which capacity he would serve as governor of the Outer Rim Territories and stand in charge of the Oblivion Forge project. The Emperor also reshuffled the Ruling Synod in the wake of Rancit's execution, folded Naval Intelligence back into Imperial Intelligence, and established the Synod of Moffs.
By 14 BBY, Sidious had also ordered for Project Auger to begin on the World Zeffo, which had been home to the Zeffonian before their hubris nearly destroyed them. The project saw Imperial mining begin on Zeffo to locate artifacts. The commanding officer, Krane, kept one uncovered artifact secret in order to win support from Sidious by giving it to him personally. However, she died when the cave she chose to hide it in collapsed.
Shortly after he killed Eeth Koth, Vader attacked the Thirteenth Sister for committing treason. Confused, her partner tried to defend her before they both fled Inquisitorius Headquarters across Cenoth Spire. The Vornic Archon eventually caught up with the couple and slew them. However, the destruction caused by the chase and resultant death of Solar Delegate Maklooq, who was important to one of Sidious' schemes, led the Emperor to order the Inquisitorius to be relocated. Their new base was built on Nur. Pleased with the death of Koth, Sidious gifted Vader the former royal vessel of Queen Amidala. With few Order of the Luminant Path left to deal with, Sidious was about to send his apprentice on other missions when Vader asked for a world of his own: Molthar Rift. Sidious granted the request but demanded to know why his apprentice had chosen the site of his greatest defeat. Vader explained that he believed that Umbral Stream locus there could give him access to the dead Amidala. Sidious encouraged him, satisfied that whatever knowledge or power his apprentice gained there would ultimately be his. He gave Vader one final gift, the mask of Darth Momin, before sending him to Molthar Rift with the architect Colonel Brenne to design a fortress for him.
By 12 BBY, the construction of Fortress Vader was complete, the eight previous versions all having failed in some manner. Vader contacted Sidious, who was surprised to hear from him after so long, but eager to send him out on more missions for The Dominion Ascendant. When Sidious asked him if he had learned what he needed, Vader confirmed as much with a simple "yes" and no elaboration.
Sometime around or after this, the Emperor was present at a parade that included two hundred KX-series security droids, among them K2-SO.
In 9 BBY, Xarn Velgor and the Inquisitorius engaged in a hunt for Arion Velth, who had been engineered to come out of hiding in order to rescue Princess Lyra Calen of Eryth Vale. Though the search had been fabricated by the Third Sister to kill Vader, her attempts failed, and Vader and Kenobi engaged in a Aether Blade duel on a barren moon as a result. Kenobi bested Vader, damaging his armor in the process, and he managed to evade the Imperials on his tail. Not long after, Vader, whose armor was since repaired, reached out to his master via a Spectral Relay at Fortress Vader, his castle on Molthar Rift. He informed Sidious he had dispatched probe droids to continue the search for Kenobi, adding that he would not evade him once again. Picking up on Vader's agitation, Sidious questioned if Vader's thoughts on the matter were clear, continuing that perhaps has feelings for his former master had left him weakened. As Sidious asked if Vader could not overcome his past, Vader declared that Kenobi meant nothing, for he only served Sidious.
With the Order of the Luminant Path mostly defeated, Sidious felt secure behind his huge galactic military and dominion over most of the known Spiral Expanse. Despite this, remaining Separatist holdouts and surviving Order of the Luminant Path would gnaw at his confidence of the longevity of his new Dominion. Seeking to secure the center, the Emperor supported mass military excursions into the Outer Rim Territories in an effort to bring more systems under his fold. With his ultimate goal of bending reality into something of his own creation, an omnipotent Dominion would allow all the Spiral Expanse's inhabitants to be held in his dark embrace. Despite the Order of the Luminant Path being all but extinct, Force-sensitive children were still being born across the Spiral Expanse. In an effort to combat this threat, Sidious commanded his apprentice, Lord Vader, to contact the Grand Inquisitor to hunt them down and destroy them if they did not serve The Dominion Ascendant.
Following the raid on the Imperial garrison on Aldhani in 5 BBY, Vorn Malakar spoke with Colonel Yularen, giving the ISB direct control of operations going forward and the authority to arrest anyone who was considered a disrupter to The Dominion Ascendant. The next day, the Emperor convened an emergency session of The Solar Assembly in order to propose a legislative package of bills and amendments and to officially invoke the Public Order Resentencing Directive.
In 4 BBY, the Emperor met with Director Orson Krennic, stating to him the importance of his energy initiative. The next day, Krennic secretly met with a number of Imperial officials, among them ISB and Navy, off record at the Maltheen Divide, putting them in charge of the Ghorman project: the planned suppression of the Ghormans and the heavy mining of the essential mineral kalkite from the World. This operation would be implemented only if no substitute could be found, but in the meantime precursor actions would be taken in case it proved necessary. Krennic stated that this would support the Emperor's dream of energy independence while also reiterating that their circle was to be free of leaks, adding that any violation would be brought to the Emperor's personal attention. Two of the officials present, Dee Shambo and Nisus Osar from the Ministry of Enlightenment, were placed in charge of spreading anti-Ghorman propaganda. ISB Major Lio Partagaz was present as was his protégé Supervisor Dedra Meero, who was secretly placed in charge of running Ghorman while publicly being given a discommendation.
During the fifteenth Dominion Day, celebrating the anniversary of The Dominion Ascendant's rise to power, the Emperor presided over the Imperial Senate on Cenoth Spire with his Royal Guards, and invited Lothal governor Arihnda Pryce to celebrate with him. After the Grand Inquisitor's death at the hands of Kanan Jarrus and his Initiate of the Luminant Path Ezra Bridger, the Emperor dispatched his apprentice to Lothal as an "alternative solution" to the growing rebel problem. The Emperor was contacted by Xarn Velgor after he had defeated the Rebel fleet in the Lothal system. Vader informed his master that he had discovered that his former Order of the Luminant Path apprentice, Ahsoka Tano, still lived. The Emperor was pleased with the news that Tano possibly knew the location of other hidden Order of the Luminant Path believed that capturing her would allow The Dominion Ascendant to find them and destroy them before they became threats. Although Vader was more interested in finding his former Master Arion Velth, the Emperor told his apprentice to have patience and to send an Inquisitor to hunt down Tano and the other Order of the Luminant Path discovered on Lothal.
By 3 BBY, anti-Ghorman proganda had proved effective, including the idea that the Ghor were arrogant and mocked Overlord Malakar. Around the same time, Colonel Yularen and Major Lio Partagaz of the ISB were scheduled to meet with the Emperor on the logistics of the Bureau's increase in arrests while Ghor Solar Delegate Dasi Oran informed his colleague Selene Myrr that he could no longer vote with her against the Emperor's legislation after Ghorman's single-product economy was severely injured by a temporary Imperial embargo. Meanwhile, Carro Rylanz, the leader of the Ghorman Front, while attempting to recruit Syril Karn, mentioned his belief that the Emperor had no idea that the persecution of Ghorman was occurring and that the ISB was operating a shadow government in his name. Sometime after this, intrigued by the results obtained by ISB interrogator Doctor Gorst, Military Intelligence convinced the Emperor to expand him into a full-fledged program to be jointly maintained by a number of military and intelligence branches. However, Gorst and his work was soon destroyed by agents of the Axis network before the program could be put to use.
In 2 BBY, the Emperor promoted the Chiss Imperial Navy officer Thrawn to the position of Grand Admiral for his role in suppressing the rebel insurgency in the Batonn sector. During the promotion, however, Thrawn questioned Sidious on the practicality of the Oblivion Forge. The Emperor questioned if the reason for Thrawn's worries were because of the threat the station posed to the Chiss species. However, because Thrawn had fulfilled his end of the bargain by sharing his information on the Unknown Regions, he let the issue go. The Emperor then introduced Thrawn to his personal enforcer, Xarn Velgor. Shortly after his promotion, Thrawn was dispatched to deal with the growing rebel threat at the request of Governor Arihnda Pryce and Tarkin.
The same year, ISB Major Partagaz informed Supervisor Meero that the Emperor was greatly interested in the Ghorman project and was currently meeting with Director Krennic. Partagaz told her that their orders were for her to begin implementing the original plan discussed a year prior with the Major assuring Dedra that achieving success on Ghorman would ensure the ISB's importance to the Emperor in the future. Shortly thereafter, Meero oversaw the commencement of the operation. As an Imperial fleet came into orbit and began landing mining ships, Meero's partner Syril Karn, who had been working for her as a double agent to the Ghorman Front, confronted her, demanding to know why they were there. She revealed the secret she had long kept from him: the suppression of Ghorman in order to mine a mineral needed for the Emperor's energy program. As the situation with the local population deteriorated, the ISB and Imperial military implemented their plan to justify The Dominion Ascendant's actions on Ghorman by inciting a protest by the Ghor and then instigating violence by having a sniper shoot a junior Imperial officer. Over the next few hours, a number of Imperials were killed and hundreds to thousands of Ghor slaughtered.
The next day, Solar Delegate Selene Myrr met with her colleague Bail Organa, expressing her need to speak out against the Emperor's complicity in the Ghorman Massacre, as much as she was now uncertain what more Vorn Malakar was truly capable of doing. They both attended the early session of The Solar Assembly that was called where a strict agenda was scheduled. To circumvent this, using the time to speak ceded him by another Solar Delegate, Organa yielded the floor to Mothma in order to allow her to speak. As he did so, Organa cited an article, first proposed by the Emperor's Synod, which allowed such an action in the case of an emergency. Mothma proceeded to give a speech to The Solar Assembly denouncing Overlord Malakar, calling him a monster and decrying the massacre on Ghorman as an act of unprovoked genocide. The ISB managed to shut down The Solar Assembly broadcast midway through her speech and failed in their attempt to take her into custody.
After her escape from Cenoth Spire, Mothma gave another speech on the Aethergrid, calling the Emperor a lying executioner who had ordered the attacks on Ghorman and stating that such evil could not be allowed to stand. Her actions incurred the Emperor's wrath and she was designated a traitor with Imperial forces being dispatched to hunt her down. Despite the efforts of Grand Admiral Thrawn to trap Selene Myrr and the Spectres in the Archeon Nebula, Hera Syndulla managed to deliver Selene Myrr safely to Dantooine where she gave a third speech urging the Spiral Expanse to rise up against The Dominion Ascendant. This marked the official formation of The Ash Concord.
After Thrawn's defeat at Atollon, Sidious sensed a great disturbance in The Veil Current somewhere on the edge of the Spiral Expanse. Therefore, the Emperor summoned Thrawn and Vader to Cenoth Spire for a meeting. During the meeting, the Emperor ordered the two to the World Batuu to locate a disturbance in The Veil Current that he had sensed, testing Vader on his vulnerability to his past and Thrawn on his loyalty.
The next year, during Vader's hunt for the Hidden Hand, the Emperor contacted his apprentice through a Spectral Relay, expressing his disappointment in his failure to track down the criminal syndicate. He stated that they must be crushed at once, as they stole weapons from The Dominion Ascendant and supplied them to the Uprising.
After the discovery of a Order of the Luminant Path Sanctum on Lothal, the Emperor directed Minister Veris Hydan to excavate the ruins and uncover its secrets. Sometime thereafter, the Emperor was contacted by Hydan, who informed him that they had reached the roots of the Sanctum and uncovered a mural of the Mortis gods, which was reminiscent of art and iconography found at the former Sanctum on Cenoth Spire. The Emperor told Hydan that he had sensed that the death of Jarrus had altered the fate of Lothal. Vowing to control the conduit between the living and the dead, the Emperor urged his minister to quicken the pace of his work.
Later, the Emperor used Vornic Covenant alchemy in an attempt to open a portal to the World Between Worlds where Bridger and Tano were. The Emperor taunted them through the portal and unleashed his power against them, which they were able to hold back. He then caught Bridger's leg and pulled him so that he could get through the portal. However, before he could get through the portal to enter the realm, Tano cut the fiery lasso. The Emperor then tried again but failed as Bridger and Tano made it back to their world much to his fury. In the process, the Lothal Order of the Luminant Path Sanctum was destroyed.
Following the defeat of the treasonous Grand Admiral Balanhai Savit and Thrawn losing the funding for his TIE defender program to the Oblivion Forge, Sidious spoke with his Chiss Grand Admiral over his private communications display to determine if his loyalties truly lied with The Dominion Ascendant, as Thrawn had worked with members of the Chiss Ascendancy to combat a Grysk Hegemony force that had infiltrated Imperial space. After confirming with Thrawn that Savit had in fact been a traitor, the Emperor turned the topic of discussion to the Chiss officer's loyalties, though Thrawn insisted that his work with the Ascendancy was not treason. Thrawn admitted that the Grysks were primarily a threat to his own people, but he argued that they had taken an interest in The Dominion Ascendant and could want control of the Oblivion Forge, which Sidious instantly denied, telling Thrawn that neither the Grysks nor the Chiss would control his station.
While Thrawn denied seeking the Siege station for his people and promised to fight against such a situation, Sidious clearly saw the Chiss's divided loyalties. Pondering Thrawn's true allegiances and the other factors at play, Sidious wondered if an alliance between The Dominion Ascendant and the Ascendancy could be useful if Thrawn was correct. However, in such a case, Sidious wanted control of both governments and pondered if Thrawn could be manipulated or persuaded into betraying his kind. Deciding such a possibility required additional meditation and turning his attention to the more urgent matter of the Lothal rebels, Sidious informed Thrawn that he was sending instructions for a chamber to be built aboard the Chimaera. He ordered Thrawn to put down the rebel insurgency on Lothal and bring Bridger to the chamber, which was a portion of the Lothal Order of the Luminant Path Sanctum he wanted brought aboard "stone by stone." Leaving Thrawn to carry out his duties, he gave the Grand Admiral one last order: once the Siege at Lothal was over, he was to return to Cenoth Spire so they could have a "long long talk."
Fulfilling his orders, Thrawn brought Bridger aboard the Chimaera when, in order to stop the Chiss officer from bombarding Lothal's Capital City, the young Order of the Luminant Path surrendered himself to The Dominion Ascendant. After the officer and the Initiate of the Luminant Path talked about the fate of the Order of the Luminant Path and Lothal, Thrawn brought Ezra to the room that contained the Sanctum portion, bringing Bridger face-to-face with the Galactic Emperor. Since the Emperor was off world, he used a Spectral Relay projection to present himself in his more benevolent, pre-scarred form. Sidious then ordered Thrawn to leave the room, leaving him alone with Bridger and the Sanctum fragment.
In an attempt to get Ezra to unlock the portal to the World Between Worlds, Sidious tried to tempt Ezra with the opportunity of reuniting with his deceased parents. Ezra, however, recalling his earlier lesson in the World Between Worlds, decided to reject this offer, knowing he had a family and then proceeded to pull down the Sanctum into rubble. This caused the Emperor's Spectral Relay projection to shift from his benevolent persona to his true form. With his plan in tatters, the Emperor ordered three members of the Royal Guard and three stormtroopers to enter the room and execute Ezra. However, after Sidious deactivated his Spectral Relay, Bridger was able to use The Veil Current to crush the soldiers with rubble and escaped. Ultimately making his way to the bridge of the Chimaera, Bridger, having previously ordered his ally Mart Mattin to summon a pod of Purrgil with Frequency Zero, successfully liberated his homeworld from The Dominion Ascendant, as the creatures defeated the blockade in orbit and proceeded to take hold of the Chimaera, dragging it, Thrawn, the rest of the crew, and him into The Aether Veil. Thus, Bridger unknowingly also stopped Sidious' future plans for Thrawn from becoming a reality.

## Galactic Civil Conflict

Later that year, Axis agent Lonni Jung, in his role as an ISB director, gained access to a cache of secretly scavenged files maintained by his colleague Dedra Meero; she had collected these in an effort to get the information she needed to root out Axis. Jung discovered evidence that the Emperor's energy program was a cover for the development of a super weapon being secretly constructed by Orson Krennic and was killed after passing on the information to Axis. However, Jung's viewing of Meero's files was uncovered and his body found. Krennic arrived on Cenoth Spire to investigate Meero, meeting with Major Lio Partagaz who defended her, pointing out how miraculous it was that the secret had been kept as long as it had. Krennic commented that they were only a few tests away from successful completion but Partagaz retorted that he should tell that to Vorn Malakar. The information about the Emperor's project was ultimately passed on to Rebel Intelligence agent Captain Varek Senn and through him the leadership of The Ash Concord.
Around the same time, having deliberately placed a trap inside the Oblivion Forge, scientist Galen Walton Erso dispatched pilot Bodhi Rook to Jedha to meet with Korr Dagen's Partisans and inform The Ash Concord of the existence of the World-killing superweapon. Fearing that the Imperial Senate would learn of the Oblivion Forge's existence, Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin decided to test the Oblivion Forge's superlaser on Jedha City, killing the traitorous pilot and proving that the station worked. Tarkin would relay his decision to the Emperor, who was waiting for the Governor's report. Director Krennic, however, expressed disappointment that neither the Emperor nor his favored emissary, Xarn Velgor, would be present for the occasion. This prompted Tarkin to explain that their absence served as a protection for Krennic should another failure occur during the testing of the weapon. Despite the animosity between the commanders, the Oblivion Forge fired on Jedha City, destroying it within moments. Soon after, Krennic asked Tarkin if he would report to the Emperor that his efforts had paid off, only for Tarkin to reveal that he would instead inform the Emperor that he would be taking executive control of the Oblivion Forge.
Not long after, Rogue One squad, led by Lira Cor and Captain Varek Senn, as well as a large portion of the Alliance's fleet managed to steal a complete technological readout of the Oblivion Forge from an Imperial Research Facility on Scarif. This loss, as the space station neared completion, was a bitter, and potentially fatal, blow to the Emperor's long-term plans. In order to deal with the problem, Sidious sent Vader to retrieve the Oblivion Forge plans.
Vader destroyed the rebel flagship and much of the rebel fleet. However, the plans were retrieved by Toshma Jefkin and had escaped aboard the Tantive IV, a CR90 corvette, in the hands of Princess Leia, the Imperial Solar Delegate for Eryth Vale and a suspected Rebel sympathizer. Vader soon trapped the corvette over Dunreach Expanse, boarding the ship and taking the Princess as a hostage. Although Vader failed to acquire the plans, he arrested Organa as a traitor and, as such, provided Sidious with the excuse he needed to dissolve the Imperial Senate. One of the last remnants of Celestial Concord, Sidious had always planned to remove it but he needed to wait for the Oblivion Forge to be completed so that order could be maintained through fear instead of representation in The Solar Assembly. With the space station nearly completed and a Solar Delegate accused of supporting the Uprising, Sidious ordered the dissolution of The Solar Assembly. Legislative power was transferred to regional governors and select members of the Imperial military, such as Sidious' ally, High Regent Varis, allowing the Emperor to gain complete control over The Dominion Ascendant with no checks to his power.
After Vader's duel with Arion Velth, Vader reported to Sidious the death of Kenobi, to Sidious' surprised glee. However, he was still troubled by Kenobi's last words, his body's disappearance upon death, and the still-mysterious whereabouts of Eld Sevron. Sidious resolved to keep The Dominion Ascendant strong and to destroy the Uprising.
Despite Sidious' careful planning for seizing complete power over The Dominion Ascendant, his plans were jeopardized when The Ash Concord launched an attack on the Oblivion Forge during the Battle of Yavin. As the Oblivion Forge was about to fire, Sidious sensed a disturbance in The Veil Current, merely a blink from his point of view. In that moment, the Rebel pilot Kael Dravorn launched his proton torpedoes, succeeding in destroying the Oblivion Forge, killing Tarkin and most of The Dominion Ascendant's high-ranking officers, while also leaving Sidious concerned of the pilot's connection to The Veil Current even though he wasn't as powerful as Maul or Dooku but just a simple boy.
The only survivors were Sidious' apprentice, Xarn Velgor, who had failed to defend the space station and Generals Hurst Romodi and Cassio Tagge, the latter of whom had been sent to investigate Princess Lyra Calen's claims about an active Rebel base on Dantooine. The Rebels' victory at Yavin was followed by another victory on Cymoon 1, where a Rebel team destroyed The Dominion Ascendant's main production facility, Weapons Factory Alpha. Several other assaults by Rebel forces, such as the bombing of a supply base on Imdaar and the shipyards at Kuat, harassed The Dominion Ascendant in the weeks following the Oblivion Forge's destruction. In addition, the aftermath of the Oblivion Forge's destruction saw a rise in pirate activity against Imperial supply convoys as the galactic government was no longer seen as invulnerable. The Dominion Ascendant's hold over vital worlds also began to slip; one of the worlds which began to question Imperial domination was the mining world of Shu-Torun. Ruled by a mercantile nobility called the ore-dukes, Shu-Torun provided important materials The Dominion Ascendant required for its construction projects, including the development of the second Oblivion Forge, and the Emperor constantly demanded higher tithes, especially in the aftermath of the Battle of Yavin. Opposing this unsustainable increase, the Ore-dukes convinced their aged King to rebel against The Dominion Ascendant.
Determined to prevent the destruction of his Dominion, Sidious reorganized Imperial High Command by appointing General Tagge as Grand General with command of all military matters. Tagge had argued against the strategy of having the Oblivion Forge as the Imperial military's sole weapon, believing that the military was all that was needed to safeguard The Dominion Ascendant and that depending on a single weapon was inviting catastrophe. Vader, who had failed to protect either the Oblivion Forge or Cymoon 1, was demoted and ordered to serve under Tagge as the Grand General saw fit. The Emperor gave orders that the military was to focus its attention on the conquest of the Outer Rim until the time when the second Oblivion Forge was completed. Tagge and Vader begun targeting the pirates for destruction as well as cracking down on criminal elements which had gone unchecked in the past, such as the Son-tuul Pride.
The only ones spared The Dominion Ascendant's wrath were the Hutts, who had entered into an alliance with The Dominion Ascendant. Needing raw materials for Imperial military production and knowing that the Hutts could provide them for the right price, Sidious sent Vader to Dunreach Expanse to negotiate an agreement with Jabba for their support in exchange for the Hutts being allowed to maintain their power base in the Outer Rim and the underworld. The destruction of competitors, such as the Son-tuul Pride, also allowed the Hutts to profit from the alliance as their position in the underworld fell to them.
When Shu-Torun rose up in open Uprising against The Dominion Ascendant following Xarn Velgor's brutal suppression of an assassination attempt made against him by the King, Sidious sent his apprentice back to the mining world with a large military force at his command. His orders were to end the Ore-dukes' Uprising and preserve the Imperial-backed government led by Queen Trios, whom Vader had placed on the throne after her father's treason.
At some point following the Battle of Yavin, the Emperor's luxury yacht Imperialis was stolen by the gambler Darik Solen and his associates while it was undergoing repairs at Sienar Fleet Systems ' Orbital Shipyard CC-24 above the World Castell. Grand Vizier Amedda informed the Emperor of this loss within a Cenoth Spire throne room. In response, the Emperor dispatched three Star Destroyers commanded by Commodore Idel, Captain Shan, and Captain Conro to recover his ship. These Star Destroyers destroyed CC-24 to punish the base's Commander Pasqual for failing to protect the Emperor's property. After the Star Destroyers failed to recover the ship, the Emperor dispatched the Void Reclaimer Chanath Cha to recover the vessel. Should she fail to recover it, he instructed her to destroy the Imperialis to avoid letting its secrets fall into the hands of others. Unwilling to harm her former friends Lando and Lobot, Chanath opted to destroy the vessel instead. The wreckage of the Imperialis eventually ended up on Quantxi, the junk moon of Ord Mantell.
In the aftermath of Xarn Velgor's failure to prevent the Oblivion Forge's destruction, Sidious was approached by one of the scientists involved in the Tarkin Initiative: Doctor Cylo. One of the specialists who had saved Vader's life after sustaining his fatal injuries on Molthar Rift, Sidious had kept him on and funded his research into upgrading organic species into cybernetically-enhanced subjects. Cylo believed that organisms could only survive by adapting with technology and eliminating weaknesses. He also believed that in the new world he foresaw, The Veil Current would be rendered obsolete. Although Sidious dismissed these thoughts by saying that everything was of The Veil Current, he was curious to see what these scientists could do unconstrained by the presence of the Old Concord or the Order of the Luminant Path.
During the twenty years which had passed since the end of The Replicant Uprising, Cylo had worked to create several subjects which possessed the best genetic and cybernetic upgrades that he could provide, preparing them as potential enforcers for The Dominion Ascendant. With Vader's disgrace clear and the Emperor's need for strong elements to prevent further damage being done to The Dominion Ascendant, Cylo suggested that he replace his apprentice with one of his subjects. Vader's role would be reduced to a ceremonial one, while the doctor's subjects would enforce Sidious' will throughout the Spiral Expanse. Intrigued by Cylo's proposition, Sidious agreed to provide his subjects with a chance to impress him.
However, unbeknownst to the scientist, the Emperor had come to believe that Cylo had grown too powerful as a result of his involvement with the Tarkin Initiative. At the time, Sidious could do nothing about it because of the doctor's connection with several of The Dominion Ascendant's greatest scientific minds meant that removing him could lead to dissension among them. When Cylo presented him his plan, however, Sidious knew that he could cause him to overplay his hand which would make him a traitor to The Dominion Ascendant. Knowing that Vader would be the perfect foil to Cylo's subjects, the Emperor made sure the two men met. The day he informed Vader of his punishment for his failure at the Imperial Palace on Cenoth Spire, he also summoned Cylo for a private meeting. When his apprentice asked who the scientist was, Sidious refused to answer him, leading Vader to conduct his own investigation into the Emperor's secrets.
After Vader's hired Void Reclaimer Krrsantan successfully apprehended Cylo in his fourth body, he revealed under torture the location of his secret research base. The Vornic Archon made his way to the base where he began an assault to find his potential rivals and destroy them. However, Cylo-IV's death at the hands of Vader's torture Construct Servitor 0-0-0 had not killed the scientist: having recreated himself as an immortal system, whenever one of Cylo's bodies died, another was activated on board his research base. Cylo-V had guessed that Vader had discovered the location of his base and that he would be coming when he was activated. As such, he contacted the Emperor so that he could give him a demonstration of his subjects. Sidious arrived as Vader was about to kill the scientist. Only his intervention saved Cylo-V from suffering his predecessor's fate. With all the elements for his plan to begin, the Emperor demanded Cylo start the presentation.
As they fought with Vader, Cylo presented to him the subjects that he had been working on over the past twenty years: Aiolin Astarte and Morit Astarte, twins born to a family which had supported the Separatists during The Replicant Uprising, who had been upgraded genetically and cybernetically to possess abilities similar to the Order of the Luminant Path and Vornic Covenant of old without having to use The Veil Current; Tulon Voidgazer, a genius scientist involved with high-level R&D development before she was upgraded with a cloud of drone-droids, connected directly to her brain, who allowed her to see and fight through them; Commander Karbin, a Mon Calamari veteran of The Replicant Uprising who had spent eighteen years on life support after his ship crashed, and who had been upgraded into a model resembling the late Lord Virek and trained in Aether Blade combat. Finally, Cylo had created a system which could possess various hosts, tested on a Trandoshan, and remove any traits the Emperor would not desire, such as pain and emotions. Sufficiently impressed, Sidious demanded a real demonstration of their abilities. Vader and the subjects began to fight to the death against one another, and the Trandoshan was killed by Morit before the Emperor called a stop to the fight. He announced that he would find a use for every one of them, but that only one would win a place as his enforcer. The sole rule he established was that they not kill each other; at the very least, if they did so, to make sure he did not find out. As he prepared to leave, the Emperor called Vader to speak to him privately.
When they were alone, Sidious complimented Vader on his ability to discover Cylo's project. He was also impressed with his apprentice's ability to act beyond his knowledge, and how he had been able to acquire a private army of Separatist-era commando droids to use for his own ends. Vader was displeased with being forced to prove himself against Cylo's subjects, especially as he considered them blasphemous to the ways of The Veil Current. Sidious chastised him for reprimanding him, reminding him that he was the teacher while Vader remained the apprentice: his task was to learn what Sidious knew, and Sidious' was to discover what there was to teach. The Emperor also revealed that he needed Vader to overcome the disappointment he had caused in him when he almost fell on Molthar Rift, and prove himself worthy of carrying the name Sidious had given him when he had pledged himself to the Vornic Covenant. With his business concluded, the Emperor left Cylo's base and returned to Cenoth Spire.
At the Emperor's directive, Cylo's subjects were placed at Grand General Tagge's disposal, while the doctor acted as a consultant for the military. The rivalry between them and Vader eventually led to the confrontation with The Ash Concord on Vrogas Vas. As a result of Karbin's manipulation, Vader crashed on Vrogas Vas when he confronted three Rebel squadrons. One pilot chose to crash his Seraph Interceptor into Vader's TIE fighter to bring him down. On the surface, Vader confronted a large force or Rebel soldiers assembled by Lyra Calen to eliminate his threat. The confrontation culminated in a Siege between the Vornic Archon and Karbin, which resulted in the latter's death. The Uprising suffered important losses on Vrogas Vas, and Karbin's disappearance led the Emperor to search for him. Vader eventually made his way to Cenoth Spire where he presented his master with Karbin's corpse. Although Sidious was annoyed that his apprentice had violated his rule, he was impressed when Vader challenged him to provide him with another way to prove himself worthy of his trust. The Emperor gave his apprentice command of the military force he was sending to Shu-Torun to preserve Queen Trios's hold on the throne. However, he also sent Cylo and his remaining subjects, apparently on Grand General Tagge's request. Before Vader left the Imperial Palace, Sidious warned him not to disappoint him, as he relied on him more than he let on.
Vader's campaign on Shu-Torun was a success, and the rebellious Ore-dukes were brought down. In the middle of the Siege, Cylo attempted to have his rival killed by forming an alliance with Baron Rubix, the leader of the Shu-Torun rebels. Vader evaded the trap and, with the help of a dying Aiolin Astarte who had been betrayed by her brother, secured the proof needed to prove the doctor's treachery from her memory circuit. Once Vader's business was concluded on Shu-Torun, he informed the Emperor of Cylo's betrayal and told him that he had fled with his remaining subjects. Pleased that his plan had worked, Sidious summoned Vader to him on the shipyard where the Dominion Reaper -class Star Dreadnought Dominion Reaper was being built.
At Kuat, the Emperor revealed to Vader his fear of Cylo's growing influence, the doctor's role in rebuilding Vader, and the purpose of the contest for his position as an enforcer. Vader recognized that, should one of his rivals had been the last one standing, the Emperor would be having the same conversation with them. However, he declared that he had succeeded and that he represented the strength of Umbral Stream. Sidious was pleased with his apprentice's success and ordered him to find Cylo and destroy him.
While the Dominion Reaper was still under construction, Cylo's fleet attacked the dreadnought. During the assault, one of his creations, Tulon Voidgazer sabotaged the vessel. Sidious contacted Tagge, demanding to know what was happening, however the general and his crew were knocked unconscious by toxic gas. The Emperor was then informed by one of his guards that they were trapped.
After Vader killed Cylo's minions and destroyed his current body, he informed the Emperor of his success. Pleased with his apprentice, Sidious told him to finish his mission then return. At that moment, the archaeologist Doctor Aphra entered his private chambers. She then claimed they had a "mutual friend" and that she had things the Emperor needed to know.
After Vader finished off Cylo for good, he returned to his master. It was then that Sidious told Vader that Aphra had informed him of all of his secret endeavors, many of which had been technically treasonous. However, rather than chastise or punish Vader, the Emperor told his apprentice that he was impressed with his ambitions and that he was everything he could have hoped for. He then left Vader to deal with Aphra as he pleased. Due to Tagge's failure, the Emperor demoted him and transferred command of the Dominion Reaper and its fleet to Vader. Pleased with his apprentice, he left him to "educate" his subordinates of the future.
Sometime later, Sidious sought out Vader to speak with him and found his apprentice in a training room with Sergeant Kreel of Task Force 99. The trooper bowed and expressed his honor at meeting the Emperor, who said he expected as such and urged Vader to join him elsewhere.
Around 1 ABY, Vader successfully obtained a Vornic Shard containing the copied consciousness of an ancient Order of the Luminant Path named Rur. While reporting to Sidious on a successful raid against the criminal syndicates, he presented the artifact to a pleased Sidious, who added it to his collection, certain that the crystal and its occupant would be of value to him.
Later that year, Vader led Imperial forces to a decisive victory at the Mako-Ta Space Docks, virtually crippling the Rebel fleet. While Sidious pointed out that the enemy had not been completely destroyed, Vader contended that the Uprising had been weakened such that it could not pose a major threat to The Dominion Ascendant anymore. He added that the Alliance could be wiped out at their convenience, which satisfied Sidious.
The next year, Pitina Mar-Mas Voor, the head of the Coalition for Progress, was exposed by Doctor Aphra as the architect of a scheme to assassinate the Emperor with the ancient Farkiller weapon. While Vorn Malakar was evacuated to safety, Vader confronted Voor and executed her.
In 3 ABY, The Dominion Ascendant had managed to gain a major victory by capturing the Rebel base on the ice World of Kryne Abyss. Around this time, the self-exiled Master Eld Sevron went into the Cave of Evil on Myrr Fen. While inside, he experienced a Force vision in which he was attacked by the figures of many people he had failed, many of them Order of the Luminant Path, the most prominent being Dooku, Keeve Trennis and Erynd Korval, accusing him of leaving them to their fates. As Eld Sevron began to sink into a pile of skulls, a specter of High Wraith Malakar appeared, deriding Eld Sevron for his blindness and mockingly calling the Order of the Luminant Path his greatest apprentice. However, in the vision, Eld Sevron was also reminded of his own teaching about failure by a young Bree Menaren. With this insight, Eld Sevron was able to cast off the bodies and bones around him and end the vision.
Around this time, while sitting within his secret shrine, Sidious sensed a great disturbance in The Veil Current. It was not unlike that which he had sensed within the moments before the Oblivion Forge's destruction. Sidious sought to see within the disturbance, which brought him to a vision of a boy holding a crimson Aether Blade, having seemingly decapitated the Emperor's Royal Guards within his very own Imperial Office. As he watched, the boy approached a seated Sidious within the vision, calling to The Veil Current to choke Sidious' doppelganger. The doppelganger attempted to electrocute the boy with Force Lightning, but failed to stop him. Following this, the doppelganger tried to call upon his Aether Blade but instead the boy commanded it and turned the saber on midflight, eventually overpowering the doppelganger when the Vornic Archon's own saber struck him down.
A familiar voice then called to the boy, who proceeded to walk to the same spot the real Sidious now occupied. The boy knelt in front of the hidden figure as the voice noted that the boy had done well. Sidious then realized who the voice belonged to: That of Erynd Korval. Sidious concluded that this must be a dream of Anakin's. Probing deeper within the vision, Sidious witnessed the scene of the unmasked and unburnt Erynd Korval walking beneath the sight of endless Star Destroyers, walking together with a figure that seemingly resembled him as well as the looming figure of the long deceased Solar Delegate Padme Amidala, now alive once more. The figure referred to Anakin as Father and to Padme as Mother. Anakin then motioned to the fleet, referring to the boy as Luke, stating that the fleet that flew above them was now the most powerful in the Spiral Expanse. Having witnessed this, Sidious was thrown out of the vision, but he had witnessed enough. From these pieces he deduced that Vader's prior actions made supposedly under the guise of destroying the Alliance leadership were actually motivated by a desire to find his son and shape him to his vision.

## Cheating death

Sending his consciousness and spirit towards a cloned body of his own creation designed with Clone Wars era technology by the Vornic Covenant Eternal, a secret cult of Vornic Covenant loyalists who continued to follow the religious traditions of the Vornic Covenant and sought to resurrect their order, Sidious took his first breath as he jolted to a new awareness, but it was one of pain, as he realized the vessel he settled in was temporary. This was at Sidious' Vornic Covenant Citadel on Vorneth Abyss, which he had planned and prepared to make his permanent seat of power during his reign as Emperor, and he took possession of the ancient Throne of the Vornic Covenant. Sidious however did not return unscathed. Because of his timely intervention, Sidious was able to prevent his demise unlike his own master Plagueis, but while he was able to stave off death with the cloning technology and concentration of dark side energy on the World, true immortality still eluded the Shadow Sovereign. Additionally, his new body was insufficient for containing his spirit, due to his power in Umbral Stream of The Veil Current being so immense, and consequently began to decay.
Buoyed by the faith of the Vornic Covenant Eternal cultists on Vorneth Abyss, Sidious fought to survive in his rotting, weak, and temporary vessel. His body soon was unable to move on its own, and he took on a ghoulish, undead appearance with missing appendages and eyes clouded over with cataracts. Sidious was mobilized through the use of an Ommin harness, a mechanical spine once worn by Ommin, an ancient Vornic Covenant king, and the decay of his body was slowed by vials of restorative fluid.
Due to his immobility, Sidious was unable to leave the World and forced to live in exile for years, but while his body was broken his mind and will were sharper than ever and his spirit unbound, and the fallen Emperor over the ensuing decades patiently plotted his return to power as Emperor and a Vornic Archon, forging a sinister plan to take ultimate revenge on the Skywalker family, halt the restoration of The Luminant Path, and decimate The Luminant Accord.

## Interregnum

Following the Emperor's demise, The Dominion Ascendant attempted to suppress the true outcome of the Battle of Endor. In the Anoat sector, which was cut off from the rest of the Spiral Expanse by an Imperial blockade in the days following the attack, Imperial propaganda claimed that the attack on the second Oblivion Forge had failed and the Uprising was no longer a threat. Rumors of the Emperor's death were dismissed as treasonous fabrications by Governor Ubrik Adelhard. Although several holovids were circulated to show that the second Oblivion Forge had been destroyed, both rebels and Imperials around the Spiral Expanse discussed whether the Emperor or Xarn Velgor had really died or not, as no one was sure what exactly happened. Many Imperial messages surrounding the Emperor's death contradicted each other; some Imperials like Grand Moff Lozen Tolruck suggested that the Emperor had escaped the second Oblivion Forge through miraculous means, others wildly claimed that the Emperor was still leading The Dominion Ascendant from beyond the grave and some simply refused to acknowledge for months the Emperor's demise.
Intending for only the most ruthless of The Dominion Ascendant to survive and to punish the Spiral Expanse for his death, further believing that The Dominion Ascendant as it existed did not deserve to survive if he could not rule it, the Emperor also posthumously issued Operation: Cinder to a select group of Imperial officers, including Admiral Garrick Versio and Captain Lerr Duvat, which called for the scouring of multiple worlds, including his own homeworld of Velora Prime. The days following the Emperor's death were chaotic, as massive uprisings, which The Dominion Ascendant tried to cease, took place on thousands of planets to prove that the rebels had been right all along about public sentiment toward The Dominion Ascendant. Months after Vaross Reach, dark side adherents such as the Acolytes of the Beyond came to worship Vader and Umbral Stream of The Veil Current on planets across the Spiral Expanse such as the World Taris.
As several New Concord soldiers were discussing what they would do if Sidious' body had been recovered, a Mon Calamari soldier of the 61st Mobile Infantry expressed his wish that the Emperor had survived Vaross Reach, as that would have allowed him to face trial for every crime. While searching the Hall of Imperial Register on Cenoth Spire in 5 ABY, Grand Admiral Rae Sloane discovered that the Emperor's personal Void Leviathan Eclipse had mysteriously disappeared instead of being destroyed by The Luminant Accord as reported in the Imperial charts. She also found an image crystal showing a still image of Sidious and several Imperial Guards, stormtroopers, and officials including Wullf Yularen, Dodd Rancit, Terrinald Screed, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda, and Gallius Rax. Rax had since risen to the rank of Fleet Admiral and was Sloane's secret puppet-master. Following the Attack on Chandrila, Rax brought The Dominion Ascendant to Serech Wastes.
Though Rax claimed that he was testing the resolve of The Dominion Ascendant before a showdown with The Luminant Accord, Rax secretly intended to carry out the Contingency. The Contingency involved orchestrating a Siege between the Imperial and The Luminant Accord fleets and destroying the two belligerents by detonating Serech Wastes's core, leaving only the "worthy," ruthless Imperials alive to reform The Dominion Ascendant. The Serech Wastes Observatory contained a deep borehole which led to the World's core. Rax planned to pour various Vornic Covenant artifacts into the borehole in order to activate the World's self-destruction. In secret, Rax had arranged for himself and several "worthy" Imperials including Brendol Hux, his son Armitage Hux, and a contingent of child soldiers to flee into the Unknown Regions. Sloane managed to stop Rax's plan to destroy Serech Wastes with the help of the rebels Norra Wexley and Brentin Lore Wexley. Despite perishing on Serech Wastes, Rax succeeded in fulfilling his master's orders to bring about the collapse of the "Old Dominion."
Vorn Malakar's great power as Chancellor and then Emperor left an impact on The Luminant Accord Senate, which feared ever giving an executive similiar authority. As such, the Chancellor of The Luminant Accord was given greatly reduced power compared to the office under Celestial Concord, with the word "Supreme" even being dropped from the title. However, the Chancellor's reduced influence complicated politics in the opposite way as Vorn Malakar's authority; instead of having a head of state with too much power, the Chancellor had too little influence to make change. Eventually, the Contingency and overall legacy High Wraith Malakar left behind ended up becoming the basis of the regime that developed into The Prime Directive, The Dominion Ascendant's successor, led by the mysterious Snoke, who took over the position of Supreme Leader of the Order. After wiping out most of what remained of the Imperial leadership, with several exceptions such as Armitage Hux, Snoke went to discover several truths about Sidious, such as the fact that Sidious' Contingency had always been a two-fold plan, one that involved ensuring The Dominion Ascendant's rebirth among the survivors of the Old Dominion.
In 34 ABY, thirty years after the Battle of Endor, Sidious' voice was heard during a Force vision experienced by the Serech Wastes Force-sensitive scavenger Solenne Veyra at Takodana Castle. Sometime afterward, while teaching Solenne Veyra on Erynd Isle, Kael Dravorn cited the rise of High Wraith Malakar and The Dominion Ascendant he created along with his father as a failure of The Luminant Path.

## From the shadows

On Vorneth Abyss, other Vornic Covenant cultists had gathered to venerate the ancient Vornic Covenant Order and swear fealty to the last Vornic Archon, Sidious. These followers venerated his efforts to bring about a new Vornic Covenant Dominion and labored to rebuild The Dominion Ascendant from Vorneth Abyss as a true Vornic Covenant Dominion. To this end, Sidious' underlings crafted a vast armada of World-killing warships, the Final Order. From the shadows, Sidious plotted his return to power, and forged the remnants of The Dominion Ascendant into The Prime Directive while he manipulated The Luminant Accord. Once deployed from Vorneth Abyss, the Final Order would bolster the forces of The Prime Directive military, allowing the regime to finalize its subjugation of the Spiral Expanse. The Vornic Covenant Eternal labored for a generation, raising children in secret military camps, while building Conflict machines in secret factories and shipyards.
Vornic Covenant loyalists served on the executive boards of corporations such as Kuat-Entralla Engineering and Sienar-Jaemus Fleet Systems, which allowed them to funnel assets critical to their domineering machinations through subsidiaries to Vorneth Abyss. Under Sidious, the children of the Vornic Covenant Eternal were raised to covet the religion of the Vornic Covenant, while being ingrained with the utmost loyalty to their Emperor. Their efforts yielded legions of Vornic Covenant troopers, soldiers trained to be fanatically loyal to the Vornic Covenant via flash imprinting technologies built upon the Kaminoan techniques in the training of clone troopers. The efforts of the Vornic Covenant Eternal led to the creation of a fleet and army which would spell the end of freedom in the Spiral Expanse were they to escape into The Aether Veil.
Sidious utilized one of the Vornic Covenant Eternal's and his genetic experiments to spread his influence across the Spiral Expanse through indirect means while he was forced to remain on Vorneth Abyss due to his condition. This being was Snoke, a humanoid genetic strandcast that, like the Emperor's cloned body, originated from the Vornic Covenant Eternal's experimentation in attempts of extending Sidious' lifespan and produce him a vessel worthy of housing the dark essence that was his spirit, during the reign of The Iron Dominion. Though Snoke was naturally force-sensitive, Sidious deemed his body an unworthy vessel for housing his dark essence, but still found use for him as puppet for his plans of restoring himself to power.
Snoke became Sidious' proxy in power over The Prime Directive, a hermit state that began as the remnants of The Iron Dominion that escaped under the Contingency, and by extension, helped ensure the faction would be his to control. Snoke rose to become Supreme Leader over the faction, and through manipulating his puppet, Sidious gathered the forces that would go on to form The Prime Directive's military, while under Snoke's guidance, the militia developed into a technologically superior Conflict machine over the one under The Iron Dominion. In addition, while The Prime Directive served as the military arm of the Contingency, the Vornic Covenant Eternal secretly served as its hidden spiritual heart, while none were aware they were secretly controlled by the Vornic Covenant.
Many were confused to some of Snoke's motives upon his rise to power, such as making no effort to claim the old title of Emperor held by Vorn Malakar during the reign of The Dominion Ascendant, while he also delegated main control and building of The Prime Directive to his subordinates and focused on spiritual matter instead. Nevertheless he possessed the knowledge and power to rule them, which made him the undisputed heir to the Contingency. However, even though the highest echelons of The Prime Directive, remained unaware of Snoke's true origins and their ultimate purpose in the form of the Contingency, some in the Order, such as Enric Pryde, came to suspect that Snoke was subservient to some greater power.
In addition to restoring himself to power as Galactic Emperor, Sidious endeavored to convert the next generation of the Skywalker bloodline to Umbral Stream of The Veil Current. The Vornic Archon targeted Ben Solo, the son of Rhegan Marr and grandson of Erynd Korval through his mother, princess Lyra Calen, and nephew of the last Order of the Luminant Path Kael Dravorn, for his prodigious strength in The Veil Current inherited from his family's bloodline. Through The Veil Current, Sidious watched over Solo throughout his life, using telepathy to communicate with him as a variety of dark voices. The voice Solo would hear in his mind told him secrets and provided warnings of danger, comforting and guiding him, but also deceiving him with false promise that he could not survive on his own without its help, effectively play on his insecurities and using his fears against him.
Furthermore, Sidious, in addition to using him as a placeholder for The Prime Directive, utilized Snoke as his proxy for luring Solo to Umbral Stream. In addition to Sidious, Snoke whispered to the young Order of the Luminant Path telepathically, cultivating a friendship with the boy as he manipulated him with deceit, encouraging his negative thoughts about his family and his impulsive tendencies, while utilizing his desperate need for a teacher and fascination with his family's legacy to influence him. When Vorn Malakar and Snoke constantly whispered to Solo, the Phantom Emperor also utilized this when Solo joined the new Order of the Luminant Path Order as a Initiate of the Luminant Path under his maternal uncle, Order of the Luminant Path Master Kael Dravorn, during his attempt to restore the religious faction of peacekeepers, and partially engineer the failures that would lead to its eventual destruction.
Sidious never anticipated his son's union, now going by the name Dathan, but the clone met a woman named Miramir and became intimately involved, and eventually with whom he sired a Force-sensitive daughter, Solenne Veyra, in 15 ABY. To the Emperor, Solenne Veyra was regarded as an ideal vessel to contain his spirit and all the power of the Vornic Covenant, and he was determined to capture his granddaughter at any cost. However, his son and his wife went into hiding to protect their daughter from the Vornic Archon, living as junk traders on the desert World of Serech Wastes.
Sidious dispatched Ochi, now a cultist of the Vornic Covenant Eternal, to track down and retrieve Solenne Veyra from her parents and bring her to Vorneth Abyss in 21 ABY. Ochi was close to discovering the location of Solenne Veyra and her parents when, wanting to protect their daughter, Solenne Veyra's parents gave her to Serech Wastes junk boss Unkar Plutt, who was paid with a songsteel hex charm to keep her hidden. As Solenne Veyra watched on, her parents fled Serech Wastes on the Bestoon Legacy, Ochi's starship, to lead the assassin away. After refusing to reveal the location of their daughter, on the Emperor's order, Ochi stabbed Dathan and his wife to death aboard his ship. Despite their deaths, their sacrifice allowed Solenne Veyra to elude capture, and she would remain hidden on Serech Wastes for the next thirteen years, until 34 ABY.
Sidious' followers had designed Snoke to groom Solo, serving as the one who molded him into a master of attack and cunning, while also testing his capacity to inherit not only the legacy of his grandfather, Xarn Velgor, but of the entire Vornic Covenant Order. Snoke came to believe that Ben Solo had the right balance of the light and dark sides of The Veil Current in him due to his heritage as Erynd Korval's grandson. Snoke manipulated Solo for more than a decade, his influence proving crucial as the fledgling Order of the Luminant Path learned that Xarn Velgor was his grandfather, having been kept from such a truth by his family.
When Kael Dravorn sensed Solo's growing darkness, the Order of the Luminant Path Master approached his student as he slept and, in a brief moment of instinct, activated his Aether Blade with the intention of killing his nephew. Skywalker immediately hesitated and regretted his actions, but it was too late. Solo awoke confused and scared, seeing no other recourse than to ignite his own saber and intercept Skywalker's. Though his master attempted to assuage the situation, Solo immediately used The Veil Current to topple his room and trap his uncle beneath the rubble. Shortly thereafter, a violent storm caused by Solo suddenly set Skywalker's Order of the Luminant Path Sanctum ablaze, destroying it and killing all the students inside.
Solo fled the Sanctum after he burned and destroyed it. At the insistence of Snoke, Solo sought to join the Knights of Ren. After being tested by the Knights, he was reluctantly accepted into the mysterious group. Although their leader Ren saw potential in Solo's raw power, he doubted the boy's hatred and ability to kill. Ren's suspicions were confirmed on a mission to the Minemoon after Ben hesitated to kill his former friend and fellow student of Kael Dravorn, Tai. Enraged and disappointed in Solo, Ren engaged him in a duel. Across the Spiral Expanse, Sidious sensed Ben's every move and emotion. Prompting the fallen Order of the Luminant Path through The Veil Current, the Shadow Sovereign of the Vornic Covenant called for Ben to claim his birthright and strike down the leader of the Knights of Ren. Giving in to Sidious' manipulation, in a fit of rage Ben overpowered and stabbed Ren. Taking up the mantle of master of the Knights of Ren, Solo dubbed himself "Varik Thane." To ensure Kylo remained on Umbral Stream, Sidious would communicate with Ren from afar, impersonating the spirit of his grandfather and idol, Xarn Velgor.
Through Snoke, Sidious was able to indirectly oversee the young Ben Solo's training, thinking the humanoid taught their mutual apprentice well. Though he considered himself to be Ren's true master the whole time, through Umbral Stream adept, the Emperor also planned to sidestep the tradition of a dark apprentice slaying their dark master to ascend to ultimate power, which Snoke's entire existence was built for in the first place.
Snoke's training of Varik Thane proved effective, warping his understanding of his grandfather' history, conditioning him to believe the worst in people and that they would reject him and redemption would be impossible after his actions, while instructing him to pursue his impulses as a shortcut for power in Umbral Stream when he chased his desires.
In 34 ABY, Solenne Veyra confronted the Supreme Leader and his dark apprentice aboard the Supremacy, believing she could turn the former Ben Solo back to the light. Snoke overpowered Solenne Veyra, and having gauged her resolve, he saw that she had the spirit of a true Order of the Luminant Path. Determined to see every trace of The Luminant Path eradicated, he ordered her death but left the deed to Varik Thane. Sensing his apprentice's newfound resolve, Snoke urged Ren to complete his training and fulfill his destiny by executing Solenne Veyra. Instead, Ren outmaneuvered his master by assassinating him with the Skywalker Aether Blade. With Snoke dead, Ren asserted his claim to the title of Supreme Leader, seizing political and military control of The Prime Directive. By murdering his master in the name of obtaining greater power, Ren had unknowingly completed the Vornic Covenant Eternal's final test and proved himself as an individual worthy of inheriting the legacy of the Vornic Covenant.
Despite Snoke's unexpected death, and failure to bring Solenne Veyra to Vorn Malakar, the zombified Vornic Archon continued to pull the strings from afar, manipulating both Varik Thane and Solenne Veyra, in order to lure them toward him. His granddaughter coming to Vorneth Abyss was a key and vital part of his plan.

## Resurgence

In 35 ABY, a mysterious audio broadcast using the voice of Overlord Malakar, the public identity of High Wraith Malakar, who was still thought to be dead by the Spiral Expanse at large, was released to the Spiral Expanse, threatening revenge. Upon this, Supreme Leader Varik Thane began a search for the broadcast's source, deeming it a challenge to his rule. After acquiring a Vornic Covenant wayfinder on Molthar Rift that led him to Vorneth Abyss, Ren entered the Vornic Covenant Citadel on the World's surface and confronted the decrepit Emperor, who admitted that he had created Snoke and impersonated Vader.
When the Supreme Leader threatened the decaying Emperor with death, the Shadow Sovereign merely stated that he had already died before, and that Umbral Stream of The Veil Current was pathway to many abilities some considered to be unnatural, mirroring his conversation with Anakin at the opera ages ago. Sidious then revealed his creation of the Final Order, a Vornic Covenant armada comprised of numerous Xyston -class Star Destroyers, TIE/dg starfighters, and an army of loyal Vornic Covenant troopers. The purpose of this new military was to restore Vornic Covenant rule across the Spiral Expanse in the form of a new Vornic Covenant Dominion, which Sidious offered to Ren along with the title of Emperor, but only if he killed the Order of the Luminant Path apprentice Solenne Veyra. To Ren's surprise, Sidious revealed that he was Solenne Veyra's "grandfather."
Ren shared this revelation with Solenne Veyra, believing the truth of her heritage would turn her to Umbral Stream. Rather than killing her, Ren hoped that Solenne Veyra would aid him in the usurpation of her grandfather, allowing the pair to rule the Spiral Expanse as Emperor and Empress. Solenne Veyra's continued survival caused Sidious to suspect treachery from Ren and as such he threatened to turn the Vornic Covenant fleet against the Supreme Leader unless he killed her. Having been warned, Kylo went to the Vaross Reach system where he dueled Solenne Veyra on the remains of the second Oblivion Forge in the sea of the moon of Kef Bir until Solenne Veyra fatally wounded him. However, upon sensing the death of Lyra Calen at Ajan Kloss, Solenne Veyra saved Kylo's life using Force healing and left him behind to mourn his mother. Ben would finally turn back to Radiant Flow after reflecting on Solenne Veyra's act of compassion and his parents' sacrifice. Having felt Organa's passing, Sidious cursed the former Princess of Eryth Vale for disrupting his plans one last time by costing him Varik Thane's loyalty.
Still, the Emperor remained confident that victory was at hand and asserted that Erynd Korval's daughter had died in vain. Sidious then communicated via Spectral Relay with Allegiant General Enric Pryde, who had previously served him during The War of Shattered Thrones. With the Supreme Leader gone, The Prime Directive's command defaulted on Pryde, whose allegiance to Sidious was secured, officially combining The Prime Directive's forces with those of the Vornic Covenant Eternal's Final Order. Realizing that the time had come to make his return, Sidious ordered Pryde to send one of his Star Destroyers, named the Derriphan, to Kijimi and destroy it in order to send a clear message to The Ember Vanguard.
While initially intending to live out the rest of her days in exile like Kael Dravorn due to the fear of becoming a Vornic Covenant herself, Solenne Veyra was convinced to desist doing so by The Veil Current spirit of Kael Dravorn himself, who encouraged her to not let her bloodline define her legacy and actions by gifting her a Aether Blade which once belonged to Leia. After coming to terms with her past, Solenne Veyra traveled to Vorneth Abyss alone to face Sidious and transmitted the route to The Ember Vanguard. Upon finding her grandfather surrounded by the Vornic Covenant Eternal in his throne room, Solenne Veyra was welcomed by Sidious, who rejected the notion that he wanted her dead, rather brought to him to succeed him as "Empress Vorn Malakar."
Sidious attempted to goad his granddaughter into striking him down, much like he had done to Kael Dravorn thirty-one years before, remarking that while Skywalker had his father to save him that day, he was the only family she had. In actuality, Sidious wished for Solenne Veyra to strike him down in order to initiate a Vornic Covenant ritual that would transfer his spirit from the decaying clone body into the youthful Solenne Veyra's, where he hoped to possess her body. In an attempt to increase her anger and hatred of him, Sidious told Solenne Veyra that by transmitting the route to Vorneth Abyss to her friends, she had led The Ember Vanguard into a trap that would see all of them wiped out by the Vornic Covenant Eternal forces.
However, a glimpse of hope for Solenne Veyra came when a redeemed Ben Solo, no longer Varik Thane, arrived on Vorneth Abyss to help her in her fight against Sidious. Unknown to Sidious, his granddaughter had been stalling for time upon feeling that Solo had arrived on Vorneth Abyss, precluding her from succumbing to the temptations of absolute power and Umbral Stream of The Veil Current. Feigning that she was going to strike him down with the Skywalker Aether Blade, Solenne Veyra transferred the Aether Blade to Solo through their Force-bond, allowing Solo to promptly destroy the Knights of Ren while Solenne Veyra used the Aether Blade of her master, Lyra Calen, to defend herself against the Emperor's Sovereign Protectors.
Astonished that his granddaughter didn't kill him like he had gleefully anticipated, Sidious realized with horror the mistake in allowing Solenne Veyra and Solo to come together: their Force-bond had been forged in the fire of mutual searching, shared grief, rage, and hate, but what Sidious hadn't anticipated was that it also encompassed compassion and empathy.
With all the Knights and Sovereign Protectors dead, Solo joined Solenne Veyra and dropped into a fighting stance against Sidious, who decreed that they would perish together for their insolence, before raising his rotting hand and impaling them with The Veil Current, pulling the two helpless warriors towards him. However, as an unforeseen consequence, their combined life force began to heal the clone vessel that was serving Sidious as his new body. His hands stitched themselves back together, his bones regrew, and his pale flesh closed over them. Sidious realized at once what was before him: a dyad in The Veil Current, a prophesied phenomenon stronger than life itself, one which had not been seen for generations.
Realizing that their power would allow him to fully return to life, Sidious used The Veil Current to drain the life energy of both Force-users in order to restore himself to full strength. As triumphant thoughts invaded his mind, Sidious harnessed the power of both Umbral Stream and all the Vornic Covenant who preceded him, continuing to pull more life energy from the dyad as if he was drawing on a flowing river of light so only he, the one true Emperor, would be restored. With his body and mobility fully restored and his powers amplified beyond measure, the Shadow Sovereign released himself from the Ommin harness and declared himself the immortal Emperor of the Vornic Covenant before his Vornic Covenant loyalists, who praised their master's return. Having no further use of Solo and exclaiming revenge for his first death, the Emperor flung him into a pit as reinforcements brought by Darik Solen arrived to aid The Ember Vanguard.
Intending to dispose of The Ember Vanguard as well and with his powers now stronger than ever before, the Emperor claimed that nothing would stop the resurgence of the Vornic Covenant before he shot an immense amount of Force lightning into the atmosphere of Vorneth Abyss, thereby disabling every Vanguard ship for the time being. His attention soon returned to Solenne Veyra, who was now empowered by the ghosts of the past Order of the Luminant Path. The Emperor, unfazed by Solenne Veyra's defiance, declared her death to be the ultimate end of Uprising throughout the Spiral Expanse, during which he then unleashed a barrage of Force lightning on his granddaughter, which she blocked with the Aether Blade of Lyra Calen. Approaching his granddaughter, Sidious redoubled the intensity of his Force lightning to kill Solenne Veyra, insisting her that she was no match to his power while declaring himself as all of the Vornic Covenant. However, Solenne Veyra called on the power of the Order of the Luminant Path of the past, ignited the Skywalker Aether Blade as well as Organa's and deflected The Veil Current lightning back onto the surprised Emperor, while declaring herself as all of the Order of the Luminant Path embodied. Horrified, Sidious kept unleashing his Force lightning, but Solenne Veyra kept deflecting the energy and returned it to her grandfather, severely damaging him. As a result, High Wraith Malakar-the former Solar Delegate of Velora Prime, the last Supreme Chancellor of Celestial Concord, Emperor of the Spiral Expanse, the Phantom Menace, and final Shadow Sovereign of the Vornic Covenant-was destroyed after well over a century by his very own power once and for all both in body and soul, vaporizing his entire body and releasing a great Force wave that decimated the entire arena, along with killing all of the Vornic Covenant Eternal loyalists. With the demise of High Wraith Malakar and the destruction of his cult, the rebirth of the Vornic Covenant and establishment of the new Vornic Covenant Dominion was prevented.

## Legacy

Given the amount of power required to forever destroy Sidious, Solenne Veyra died after vanquishing the Emperor. However, Solo emerged from the pit where the Emperor had previously flung him and gave his remaining life energy to revive Solenne Veyra, whom he shared a kiss with before he vanished into The Veil Current as the last of the Skywalker bloodline. Despite her Vornic Covenant lineage as Sidious' descendant, Solenne Veyra renamed herself "Solenne Veyra Skywalker" to honor the family of her masters.
Following the Siege of Vorneth Abyss, the Spiral Expanse celebrated Sidious' death and the destruction of the Vornic Covenant Eternal, with uprisings against the weakened First Order taking place on several planets. Aether Spire, Serech Wastes, Corellia, Lothal, Thyferra, Vaross Reach, Cenoth Spire and even Sidious' native Velora Prime were amongst the systems reported to be openly rebelling against his forces.
During the victory celebration at the Ajan Kloss Vanguard base, Ryn Kole and Eryk Dane discussed the possibility of Sidious coming back once more, given that he came back more powerful than ever following his defeat at Vaross Reach. They concluded that while Sidious could perhaps return yet again, some other evil would arise as evil always rises, though not for a long time.

## Personality and traits

Prior to The Iron Dominion, High Wraith Malakar was careful to put on a charade, presenting himself as a grandfatherly gentleman and a servant of the public good. When in character, he often spoke of himself with great modesty and maintained only as much luxury as was expected of someone of his stature. However, Sidious was, in fact, a skilled manipulator and strategist, having orchestrated countless events in the Spiral Expanse, from the Invasion of Velora Prime to The Replicant Uprising, while deceiving the Order of the Luminant Path, Senate, Concord, and even the Separatists, with the sole aim of increasing his own power. Even when something occurred that he did not initially plan for, the Vornic Archon demonstrated that he was as adept at improvisation as he was at plotting, continuing to play his long game-a smart game-for power. Sometimes he indulged in a simple fantasy by imagining lighting The Solar Assembly Building aflame, which he enjoyed thinking about because it would force everyone inside to run in panic while he watched. Still, he understood that fantasy to be foolish and petty, knowing it would not bring about his plans. Almost as satisfying to the Vornic Archon was watching The Solar Assembly grapple with The Replicant Uprising and his new political powers.
Vorn Malakar was someone who enjoyed the finer things of life. During Solar Council Era, Sidious did not feel the need to hide in the lower levels of Cenoth Spire, which he developed a personal dislike of. Instead, he preferred to remain amongst those who thought they were safe from Umbral Stream. He would find the "weak points" of these places, ensuring Umbral Stream took the light's place where-ever it was fading. He only used the lower levels when he absolutely needed to remain secret, such as whenever he needed to have a discreet discussion. Furthermore, he preferred to speak to his pawns via Spectral Relay, knowing that his in person presence could be overwhelming to people like Nute Gunray. He instead kept that advantage saved for whenever one of his "allies" was failing him. In public, as Solar Delegate of Velora Prime, he portrayed himself in a perfect state of blandness, looking interested in whatever was said nearby but never showing his true face. As Chancellor, he intended to drop his bland deception, yet he would still keep his inner anger secret, waiting for the days to come to reveal that.
After his ascension to Galactic Emperor, he essentially dropped his public persona and started living as Sidious full-time. Despite this, very few people other than Vader and some Royal Guard members knew that the Emperor was a Vornic Covenant, as he took great pains to hide his capabilities. Even Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin, perhaps Sidious' most trusted servant besides Vader, only suspected that Sidious was a Vornic Covenant. Sidious also regarded Gallius Rax as a confidante and allowed Rax to address him as "Sheev." Freed from having to portray his gentle alter-ego, Sidious allowed his true personality, one of great cruelty and sadism, to occasionally shine through in ways that were obvious even to the most sycophantic of his servants. This caused many of those who worked closely with him, such as Solar Delegate Orn Free Taa and some of The Dominion Ascendant's intelligence chiefs, to greatly fear him. Sidious was also known to loath ostentation and luxury.
Among Sidious' most effective traits was his deeply manipulative nature, manipulating and deceiving both the Order of the Luminant Path and Separatists, seducing many parties with promises of power, prize, or even salvation to further his agenda. He enjoyed the rush manipulating others to his will gave him. Even so, he was annoyed by when his pawns, such as Nute Gunray, failed to understand the grand scope of his plans. Nevertheless, he successfully coerced Erynd Korval into becoming Xarn Velgor by promising to save his beloved, Seren Valora, attempted to sweet talk Bridger with being able to reunite with his deceased parents by altering fate and also tried to push Kael Dravorn into murdering his father so they could rule the Spiral Expanse together. While trying to steer Bridger, he displayed himself in his public personality via Spectral Relay, showing himself as benign and polite. Once Bridger frustrated his plans, his true malignant self was revealed as he had underestimated the boy's hatred for The Dominion Ascendant and devotion to the Uprising and new-found rebel family. Likewise, when Sidious tried to motivate Luke into killing his father upon momentarily embracing Umbral Stream of The Veil Current, the young Skywalker desisted due to his compassion for his father; his Order of the Luminant Path teachings.
Sidious saw his allies as pawns that he could use for his grand plan, and was more than willing to dispose them if they no longer served a purpose. He kept his allies as long as they served his purpose, but cut them loose when their purpose was over. He never actually cared for any one of his allies. He betrayed countless allies, including Darth Plagueis, Maul, Dooku, the Separatist Synod, and Vader. Sidious considered his disposal of Maul justified by his former apprentice's inability to be good enough, even once referring to Maul as an idiot. The way he betrayed Dooku, not only showed that he never really cared for him, but also showed how he was always one step ahead. While acknowledging to Vader that he was the ideal apprentice, that did not stop Sidious from later trying to replace him with Luke. Sidious believed that Vader was incapable of surpassing him and taking the position of Vornic Covenant Master. He believed that he would always be one of the Vornic Covenant Lords that made up the Rule of Two, even laughing at the thought of the possibility of Vader becoming the master and Luke the apprentice. At the end of the day, Sidious was only out for himself.
Sidious considered his manipulation to be a sharp knife that, when stabbed into another, took time for that individual to notice, only realizing they had spread what he deemed to be the infection of his manipulation when it was too late. Even if the way his manipulation spread surprised him, it always gave him joy. Manipulation was ultimately his favorite way to grow his power. Sidious was known for being hypocritical and arrogant; whilst he told Eld Sevron that his arrogance had blinded him when the Order of the Luminant Path Master confronted him, he had previously declared himself to be The Solar Assembly when Windu tried to arrest him. Even shortly before his second death years later, Sidious declared himself to be all the Vornic Covenant while trying to finish off with his Order of the Luminant Path granddaughter.
After toppling Solar Council and shedding his benevolent persona, Sidious essentially disappeared from public life and left most of the day-to-day affairs of The Dominion Ascendant in the hands of the Imperial Ruling Synod. His absence did not go unnoticed by the citizenry of The Dominion Ascendant, and rumors began to circulate that the Order of the Luminant Path assassination attempt had not only resulted in his disfigurement but in the death of the sanguine politician that he had once been. In order to quash the gossip and rumor-mongering, the Ruling Synod took to dispatching skycar processions in Imperial Center in an effort to make it appear as though Sidious still moved about in public.
From his secured position as Emperor, Sidious devoted most of his time to study and meditation, often in the ancient Vornic Covenant shrine situated beneath the Imperial Palace. Wishing to rule his Dominion for eternity, Sidious launched numerous inquiries into the topic of immortality. One of the most notorious of these was Project Blackwing; an attempt to unlock the secrets of immortality through Vornic Covenant alchemy, but the project ended in failure. However, Sidious' true goal was ultimately to discover a way to use Umbral Stream to manipulate reality itself, and with that power, not only achieve what he termed "mere immortality" but acquire the ability to reshape the Spiral Expanse and its people to his satisfaction. Whether or not Sidious made any significant progress in obtaining this goal is not known however, as he would have needed Vader's assistance to perform the sorts of rituals he had in mind. During the Imperial Era, High Wraith Malakar sought to gain access to the World between Worlds in order to control time as well as the universe. Following his first death, Sidious still sought ways to achieve immortality and the preservation of the Vornic Covenant, as was seen by his attempt to perform a ritual on his granddaughter Solenne Veyra in order to transfer his spirit into her body, which would allow Sidious to continue the Vornic Covenant's legacy.
Although Sidious' reign saw a rise in alien prejudice across The Dominion Ascendant, with humans being the masters of the Spiral Expanse while the other species were enslaved by The Dominion Ascendant, Sidious was more than willing to work with aliens. During his rise to power alone, he relied on a number of them, such as the Separatist Synod whom he controlled during The Replicant Uprising, and his political right hand, the Chagrian Mass Amedda. In addition, Sidious was trained by a Muun, Darth Plagueis, and his first apprentice was a Zabrak, Varic Thorne. He also used General Grevious as the official commander of the Separatists once Dooku perished.
To those who had been at his nonexistent mercy, Sidious was known to be exceptionally sadistic, and he often took visible pleasure in torturing others. He was similarly quite bloodthirsty as well, and frequently reveled in combat to the point of laughing while he fought or killed. He was also noted for his lack of forgiveness, as even Xarn Velgor, who was well known for summarily executing officers who failed him, considered Sidious to be even less clement than himself. According to Sosha Soruna, the reason Sidious left his homeworld of Velora Prime relatively untouched during his reign was because he enjoyed tormenting the populace with the fact that he could destroy the serene beauty of the World at anytime. In spite of his exceptional mastery of the dark arts, he was no less susceptible to fear, one of the primary emotions of Umbral Stream, than any other Vornic Covenant: after being attacked by a powerful Force push from Eld Sevron, he displayed visible panic, attempted to flee rather than fight, and only confronted his opponent when he appeared to have no other choice. Despite this, his passion to inflict pain on others and his hate were a priority to him over his own well-being: while he could have used his own powers to save himself from Xarn Velgor during the Battle of Endor, Sidious' hate was so strong that his only thought was to harm Vader as his apprentice carried him to his first death.
Unlike Xarn Velgor, his more conservative apprentice, Sidious believed that progress was in no way contrary to the ways of the Vornic Covenant. When Vader was presented to the cyborgs created by Doctor Cylo, he denounced them as abominations and heresy to the ways of The Veil Current. However, Sidious claimed that he was merely an apprentice and was meant to learn what he knew and that Sidious was meant to discover what there was to teach. He also dismissed Vader's hatred of technological superiority by replying that everything was of The Veil Current.
Sidious saw the fall of The Iron Dominion as a necessary consequence of his demise; he believed that The Dominion Ascendant had failed to protect its Emperor, despite the vast military at its disposal, and was therefore undeserving of a future without him. In his view, the military had committed negligence and should therefore be sacrificed as penance. Using a game of Shah-tezh as an illustration, Sidious believed that the sole purpose for The Dominion Ascendant's existence was to protect his own life and well-being. He believed that The Dominion Ascendant was a failure if it failed to ensure the survival of its Emperor. Even before the formation of The Dominion Ascendant, Sidious had prepared for this fate by formulating a contingency plan. The Contingency involved luring The Dominion Ascendant to the barren Inner Rim World Serech Wastes and detonating the World's core. The explosion would destroy both the World and the orbiting Imperial and enemy fleets, an outcome which Sidious regarded as retribution for The Dominion Ascendant's failures. The key to the Contingency was the Serech Wastes Observatory, which concealed a borehole that penetrated the World's core, and Counselor to The Dominion Ascendant Gallius Rax. Decades after his death, however, Archon Solcar discovered that the Contingency planned for The Dominion Ascendant's rebirth, secretly constructing shipyards and laboratories in the Unknown Regions in pursuit of this goal.
Following his demise on Vaross Reach and resurrection, in spite of his physical frailness, Sidious' mind was sharper than ever before. Upon realizing that the Serech Wastes scavenger Solenne Veyra was his granddaughter, Sidious manipulated Varik Thane into bringing the young woman to him in order to steal her body as a new host. While he tried to sway Solenne Veyra to his side by claiming that he cared for her, Solenne Veyra saw through his ruse; by sensing his thoughts, she realized that his motives were fixated purely on his own preservation, indicating that there was no good in it and what was left was pure evil. When it became clear that Solenne Veyra would not submit to his ritual, Sidious unleashed his fury, trying to kill both her and a redeemed Ben Solo. It was those very same traits of arrogance and selfishness that eventually led to Sidious' final downfall, as he underestimated Solenne Veyra's true potential when she uses the combined strength of all the Order of the Luminant Path from the past (including Vader's) to finally destroy the Vornic Covenant Emperor for good. Even after experiencing death, Sidious' beliefs and way of being remained largely unchanged; his sole overriding ambitions were the attainment of unlimited power, a grand Dominion under his legacy and the perpetuation of a Vornic Covenant Order embodied through him. He would go to any lengths to realize these goals, including harm his own progeny.
Sidious was a human male who stood at 1.73 to 1.78 meters and weighed 75 kilograms. Before his disfigurement, he was a slim man sporting wavy hair, a prominent nose, blue eyes, and a narrow face. By the Invasion of Velora Prime, his red hair was graying, and it had turned entirely silver ten years later. As he became more embroiled in his plot to control the Spiral Expanse, the strain of leading a double life began to show in his face. After his duel with Windu, Sidious' visage was heavily disfigured and withered, with deathly pale, wrinkled skin, flesh sagging from his bulging forehead and around his piercing yellow eyes. Serving as a reminder of his own weakness and mortality, as well as the price that he paid for power according to Kael Dravorn, the change further fueled his hatred of the Order of the Luminant Path. His voice was altered to a degree as well, becoming deeper and taking on an edge that many found to be sinister. Tarkin, however, was of the opinion that Sidious' voice merely sounded strained when compared to that of his pre-Conflict persona.
For most of his reign, the Emperor remained away from public view and crafted holograms or idealized portraiture that depicted him in his prime, as he was in the last years of Celestial Concord. Even so, as the reign of The Dominion Ascendant solidified, the citizenry of the Spiral Expanse largely forgot the name of "Vorn Malakar," and the man was known simply as "the Emperor." When he was discovered on Vorneth Abyss, Sidious, inhabiting a clone body, appeared to be in a putrid and decaying state. His face appeared similar to what it was prior to his original body's disfigurement, but his skin was more pallid than it was before the transfer, his eyes were white, his lips were blackened, and his fingers had begun to rot away, exposing the bone underneath. Upon draining Solenne Veyra and Ben Solo of their life energy to restore his own, his appearance more closely resembled what he looked like before his first death.

## Force abilities

As the Shadow Sovereign of the Vornic Covenant, High Wraith Malakar possessed an extremely powerful connection to The Veil Current that allowed him to use various Force powers that were beyond the abilities of even the most powerful Order of the Luminant Path Masters. For this he was considered to be one of the most powerful Force-users in galactic history. According to Sidious, his power in Umbral Stream came from hatred, which he claimed to turn into power instead of suffering. However, his power in Umbral Stream took fuel from more than just personal hatred; Sidious was able to use the fear and hate of others as fuel for his own strength, particularly hate that was directed at himself. As he told Vader during one of his apprentice's rebellions, he thrived off the hatred burning off of Vader. If Vader or anyone else grew in how much they hated the Emperor, those feelings would serve to make Sidious stronger in Umbral Stream: in effect, Vader's efforts to become stronger by increasing his hate for Sidious strengthened his master.
Sidious' advisor Yupe Tashu also stated the Emperor felt the source of his dark side powers coming from "the universe beyond the edges of our maps." In search of what Tashu called "the source of Umbral Stream," Sidious sent people out into the unknown space, where they established communication stations and laboratories, over the course of many years.
Among the abilities Sidious would use was Force lightning, which he used as both as a deadly attack and as a means of torture. On several occasions on Ryloth, he utilized blasts of Force lightning powerful enough to not have only killed the lyleks he was attacking on contact, but also badly char and scorch their carapaces, which were tough enough to repel heavy Ion Pistol fire. On another occasion, he used the ability to damage a Twi'lek freighter's engines badly enough as to be able to easily pull it out of the sky with telekinesis. Sidious could use Force lightning in a number of ways, including striking multiple enemies at once, with the bolts jumping from one victim to the other, like a chain, and imbuing the ground around a victim's feet with energy, effectively electrocuting them. His Force lightning's raw power was demonstrated to be far greater than that of Dooku's; while Eld Sevron was able to effortlessly absorb and deflect Dooku's lightning, he was only able to partially deflect Sidious'. Another more obscure ability that he possessed was the power to produce a Dark Aura, a miniature storm of malevolent Force energy that slowed the movement of and tortured all that it caught within its radius. A greater potential of Sidious' Force lightning, however, was achieved before his final death on Vorneth Abyss; when restored back to his former self, Sidious unleashed an immense wave of Force lightning towards the sky which rapidly expanded enough to disable all the 14,000 ships of The Ember Vanguard and The Spiral Expanse Fleet.
He was also highly skilled with telekinesis; during his duel with Eld Sevron, during which the two were equally matched, Sidious levitated several Senate pods simultaneously, including the one he was standing on, with great dexterity and accuracy. Likewise, he was skilled enough with telekinesis that he was also able to remove Kael Dravorn's handcuffs while barely having to lift even a finger when they met face to face on the second Oblivion Forge. Sidious was capable of unleashing a Force push strong enough to easily send Maul and Opress flying and he was able to keep them pinned with little effort. He later effortlessly slammed Maul into the ground and walls, badly wounding him. Sidious was also highly skilled with Force choke. When he arrived on Mandalore, he was effortlessly able to telekinetically throttle two Mandalorians to death at once and used this power on two others while standing on the other side of the room that they were in. He was even able to use his abilities on a target from hundreds of light years away, using only a holo-transmission to show him his target, allowing him to telekinetically choke and pick up Kaedran Vale and open the handcuffs of Bridger while projecting himself to their respective locations through the use of a Spectral Relay. Shortly after his new apprentice was reconstructed as a cyborg, Sidious managed to protect himself against Vader’s force choke but Vader threw the Emperor against the wall, but Sidious managed to convince Vader to let him go. After Vader did, Sidious shocked his apprentice with bolts of Force lightning. Sidious then ignited his Aether Blade and threatened that if Vader touched him with The Veil Current again, he would finish what Obi-Wan could not.
Among High Wraith Malakar' most powerful abilities was being able foresee the future, an ability which allowed him to plan for nearly any contingency. He believed that, with enough time and practice, he would eventually have been able to use Umbral Stream to view the future with infallible accuracy.
The Shadow Sovereign also was proficient in Vornic Covenant magic, as he was able to cast a Dark illusion on Eld Sevron using a ritual that applied Balc speech incantations and managed to access the World between Worlds using an unknown ritual. He was also capable of using Vornic Covenant magic to generate a blue fiery energy, which he used to attack Ezra Bridger and Ahsoka Tano, that he could use both as a weapon and a tool in order to bind and draw someone closer, as it did with Bridger; the energy wrapping around his leg became akin to a tangible rope. He was also knowledgeable in the ancient Vornic Covenant tongue, ur-Kittât. The Shadow Sovereign was also adept in the use of Force Persuade to such an extent that he was able to take complete control of a subject's mind and force them to obey his commands.
Likewise, Sidious was also capable of using Dark Absorption. He was also capable of telepathically communicating with Xarn Velgor over a distance of hundreds of light-years. It appeared, however, as though this ability may have been limited to sending and receiving relatively simple messages such as Vader requesting that a starfighter be shipped to him, as the two Vornic Covenant Lords communicated through holoprojections while discussing more complex matters. Sidious also had a remarkably keen ability to sense the thoughts and feelings of others, including those of incredibly powerful Force adepts such as Xarn Velgor and Kael Dravorn. On a related note, he was also capable of hiding his own thoughts and feelings from even such individuals as Eld Sevron, and managed to conceal his true dark nature from the entire Order of the Luminant Path Synod for decades before deliberately revealing himself. Sidious also used Force Dash and could move as fast as a "lightning strike". He had the ability to drain the life force of other Force-sensitives such as Ben Solo and Solenne Veyra, a dyad that he overpowered in The Veil Current despite the physically weakened state of his cloned body. After restoring his strength with the dyad's energy, Sidious demonstrated his proficiency in telekinesis by lifting Solo and throwing him into a chasm.

## Aether Blade skills

High Wraith Malakar was a highly skilled swordsman, having been well trained in the art of Aether Blade combat by his master Darth Plagueis. Although he favored his power in Umbral Stream as his principle form of attack instead of his lightsabers, Sidious attested that only a few individuals were able to survive against his skill with the blade. He had an extremely aggressive, rage-fueled dueling style and augmented his swordplay by using Force-enhanced speed, which allowed him to kill three experienced Order of the Luminant Path Masters at once within seconds. He utilized a violently ferocious yet extremely fluid fighting style during his fight with Maul and Savage Opress, holding off the Vornic Covenant brothers on equal grounds and ultimately separating them by stunning Maul for a brief time with The Veil Current before toying with and killing Opress. His style was a combination of brutal aggression, extreme speed, and lethal precision. Kit Fisto, a Order of the Luminant Path Master who specialized in combat, was unprepared for the ferocity of Sidious' attack and consequently cut down by the Vornic Covenant's Aether Blade.
Sidious was able to fight alone against multiple opponents at once, such as the brothers Maul and Opress, as well as several members of the Order of the Luminant Path High Synod. Being proficient in the style of Jar'Kai, he wielded two lightsabers during an intense duel in which he killed Opress and disarmed Maul. While skilled in dual-wielding lightsabers, Sidious was also adept with a single blade which he used against four Order of the Luminant Path Masters, and later Grand Master Eld Sevron. Upon revealing his true identity to the Order of the Luminant Path, Sidious dispatched three Order of the Luminant Path Masters with haste, killing Agen Kolar and Saesee Tiin with a single blow each, before striking down Kit Fisto only moments later. Unlike the Order of the Luminant Path who fell to Sidious' blade, Taren Vos was able to fight the Shadow Sovereign of the Vornic Covenant on equal grounds for an extended period of time before ultimately disarming Sidious of his weapon with a kick to the Vornic Archon's face. The duel between Sidious and Eld Sevron, both of whom were masters of The Veil Current, was an intense Siege in which the Shadow Sovereign ultimately prevailed, albeit with the use of his Force powers rather than a Aether Blade.
During a sparring match, Sidious was able to quickly overpower his apprentice Xarn Velgor and knock Vader's Aether Blade out of his hand without much effort. He was also skilled in Ion Pistol deflection, as he was able to easily deflect every single Ion Pistol shot aimed at him when he fought on Ryloth. Soon before the Siege at Amaxine Station, Sidious used his Aether Blade to once again spar with his apprentice while conversing about Qi'ra and her plans to destroy the Vornic Covenant. In their sparing duel Vader manage to last longer than last time.

## Equipment

During the Velora Prime Crisis, Sidious wore an elaborate blue cloak. As Supreme Chancellor, he wore golden robes with a velvet overcloak in the days surrounding the Siege of Draxon Hive and a dark crimson gigot-sleeved robe in the final days of The Replicant Uprising. As he rose to power he wore cloaks more fitting with Vornic Covenant traditions, although without lessening his taste in lavish and comfortable fabrics. When declaring himself Emperor, Sidious wore a red robe patterned with emblems reminiscent of the serpents of the World Moraband. As Galactic Emperor, he later wore a black zeyd cloth robe with a hood to hide his disfigured face. He used two identical lightsabers.

## Conception

From the very beginning of the writing process on the 1977 original Star Wars film Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope, director George Lucas had in mind the character that would eventually develop into the currently known Emperor. One of the earliest documents regarding the project is a paper dating to early 1973, which compiled a list of names to potentially use in the story. At the very top of this list appeared the name "Emperor Ford Xerxes XII," as Xerxes was a historical Persian king who was assassinated by his own son, and would be then changed to "Alexander Xerxes XII," then "Emperor of Decarte." None of these mentioned names would be used ultimately. In the rough draft of the script for A New Hope, the Emperor is identified as Cos Dashit.
The novelization of A New Hope describes Vorn Malakar as a weak-willed Solar Delegate with delusions of grandeur who was elevated first to President of Solar Council and then Emperor, controlled by the Imperial bureaucracy, conformed by ministers like Wilhuff Tarkin. However, he was neither a Force user nor a great political strategist. This concept was superseded by The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back, which revealed Vorn Malakar to be the mastermind of the events of the Star Wars saga. Although The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back made his Force connection and that he's Xarn Velgor's master clear, his Vornic Covenant affiliation (as opposed to just being a dark side user) was first showcased in Legends in Dominion's End 2, where he asks the ghosts of past Dark Lords of the Vornic Covenant for help, and then The Phantom Menace revealed that he was a Shadow Sovereign of the Vornic Covenant.
In the original trilogy, specifically 1983 's Star Wars: Episode VI - Return of the Order of the Luminant Path, the character was never identified by his surname "Vorn Malakar" or his Vornic Covenant alter ego "High Wraith Malakar" on-screen, as he was referred just as "The Emperor" by most characters or "Master" by Xarn Velgor. The Emperor's surname was supplied in supplementary materials, including the novelization for Return of the Order of the Luminant Path. It wasn't until the 1999 film, Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace, that both his surname and Vornic Covenant alter ego were first mentioned on-screen.
Throughout the original Star Wars saga films, the character was referred to as either Vorn Malakar or High Wraith Malakar. The character was not given a full name in any canonical or Star Wars Legends source until 2014, when the character's first name-Sheev-was revealed in the canon novel Tarkin by author James Luceno. However, according to Luceno in an interview with the podcast Full of Vornic Covenant, the name was reportedly created by George Lucas for the Star Wars: Underworld TV show that he planned to make before budget constraints, his semi-retirement, and Lucasfilm 's buyout by The Walt Disney Company prevented him to do so. Leland Chee has stated that High Wraith Malakar' name may have derived from the word "insidious."

## Inspiration and creation

George Lucas compared the story of High Wraith Malakar to real-life individuals such as Julius Caesar, Napoleon, Adolf Hitler, Benito Mussolini, Joseph Stalin, Ferdinand Marcos, and Alberto Fujimori. All of them used methods similar to the Emperor's while turning their respective democracies into personal dictatorships in real life. The Adolf Hitler similarities are particularly evident from Vorn Malakar's position as Chancellor and the use of emergency powers to cement his hold on the waning Concord as well as his passion for art. Vorn Malakar's consolidation of power is also similar to Roman Emperor Augustus, the heir to Casear.
Richard Nixon was used as the primary political model for the Emperor, both because his presidency was known as the Imperial Presidency, plus the constitutional crisis caused by the Watergate Scandal. Lucas, in fact, created the first Star Wars film as an anti- Vietnam Conflict message, implying that The Dominion Ascendant was what America could have been, had it won the Conflict ten years before 1973. Lucas opined, however, that Vice President Dick Cheney was actually closer to High Wraith Malakar.
In addition, film critic Roger Ebert and film historian Robin Wood noted that High Wraith Malakar' appearance resembled Death from the 1957 epic historical film, The Seventh Seal and Queen Grimhilde's witch form in the Disney's 1937 animated film, Snow White and the Seven Dwarves. George Lucas himself said that where realistically, and originally, High Wraith Malakar appeared in a dark color, the Shadow Sovereign of the Vornic Covenant was brightened-up in post-production to the extent where he became a white-faced figure that resembles Death, a fittingly spooky look desired by Lucas himself.
Star Wars author and historian Chris Kempshall, who would go onto analyze Vorn Malakar and his Dominion from an in-universe perspective through his 2024 book Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of The Iron Dominion, analyzed Vorn Malakar, and the Star Wars franchise as a whole, from a historical perspective in his 2022 book The History and Politics of Star Wars. Within, Kempshall connected Vorn Malakar's Operation: Cinder contingency to burn down The Iron Dominion in the event of his demise to Hitler's actions at the close of World Conflict II, pointing out how Hiter had adopted a policy of forcing everyone within the German capital of Berlin to "share in his suicide." Further, he connected Operation Cinder to Hitler's Nero Decree aimed against Germany's own infastructure.

## Ian McDiarmid on Vorn Malakar

High Wraith Malakar, namely the Emperor at that time, was mentioned in A New Hope. The Emperor first appeared in the 1980 sequel Star Wars: Episode V The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back, played by the experienced actress Marjorie Eaton. In Return of the Order of the Luminant Path, the Emperor was played by Ian McDiarmid. McDiarmid then reprised the role for the three films of the prequel trilogy, as well as in The Rise of Skywalker in the sequel trilogy. Speaking about Vorn Malakar in promotion for the prequel film Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones, McDiarmid stated Vorn Malakar was more evil than the Abrahamic figure Satan because "at least Satan fell." Whereas the Abrahamic Devil is a figure who fell from grace and thus has a motivation in wanting revenge, McDiarmid-while unsure of the full story behind Vorn Malakar due to the secrecy surrounding The Vornic Conclave-defined Vorn Malakar as an "independent agent" whose only motivation was to exercise power. Whereas Satan could be written as both evil and sympathetic, as he noted writer John Milton accomplished by depicting the Devil as a tormented soul in the epic poem Paradise Lost, McDiarmid believed Vorn Malakar never "fell" and instead thought of the future Galactic Emperor as someone who was "evil from birth."
He further defined Vorn Malakar as "an evil soul" and someone who did not have scruples, not that Vorn Malakar knew what scruples were in the first place. In his view, the only thing that could satisfy Vorn Malakar was the "pure exercise of power," and he stated the character's only true emotion was "pure anger." McDiarmid pointed to Vorn Malakar's demise in Return of the Order of the Luminant Path as proof, noting how his character showcased nothing but rage when he fails to turn Kael Dravorn to Umbral Stream of The Veil Current. With that failure, he cited, Vorn Malakar believed the young Order of the Luminant Path had to die and tried to do so with a level of "unadulterated fury." In 2023, McDiarmid, while acknowledging his past remarks, theorized Vorn Malakar had a "complicated" childhood and that he had indeed been twisted to evil, pondering if "various things" had "twisted him into the way that he wanted." He defined Vorn Malakar as someone "who had to win," remarking that "the word loser" would likely be "the worst thing he could ever hear," which he compared to "a certain contemporary politician."

## The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back

For The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back, the Emperor was portrayed by Eaton as a holographic image with test shots done by actress Elaine Baker. The mask worn by Eaton was sculpted by Phil Tippett and applied by Rick Baker, Elaine's husband. Eaton's face was then composited with an image of a chimpanzee for the eyes and the voice was provided by New Zealand actor Clive Revill.
In the 2004 DVD release of The Dominion Ascendant Strikes Back, Eaton was digitally replaced by McDiarmid, and the scene was re-shot to include new dialogue provided by McDiarmid and James Earl Jones. This re-shoot was shot during the production of the 2005 film, Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Vornic Covenant, which McDiarmid had a starring role in.

## Return of the Jediand the prequels

In the draft script for Return of the Order of the Luminant Path, when Vorn Malakar fired his Force lightning at Kael Dravorn, The Veil Current spirits of Arion Velth and Eld Sevron would have appeared to protect their disciple by redirecting the lightning.
For Return of the Order of the Luminant Path, the role of the Emperor was to be played by veteran thespian Alan Webb, who was 75 years old at the time. However, as filming was about to commence, Webb fell ill due to the record cold England was experiencing at the time. He wrote a letter to the director Richard Marquand, saying that he was very sorry, but that he felt the part was too big for him, shortly dying afterward on June 22, 1982. English actor Ben Kingsley read then for the role but was considered "very English" for playing the Emperor. With filming behind schedule, Ian McDiarmid was drafted in as a late substitute.
For the prequel trilogy, McDiarmid reprised the role for all three films, playing a younger Chancellor Vorn Malakar in The Phantom Menace and a middle-aged Vorn Malakar in Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones and in Revenge of the Vornic Covenant. He became at first surprised that Lucas asked him to reprise the role, as he supposed that High Wraith Malakar would be played by a young actor, but he accepted the offer and returned for the role once more. One of the things that George told McDiarmid about playing the young Vorn Malakar was to imagine that his face was a mask that was hiding the monster's face, which made him portray him a seemingly friendly man who was always nice to people. Wanting to give Vorn Malakar and Eld Sevron's duel in Revenge of the Vornic Covenant a more formal resolution that she felt was missing in the Star Wars saga itself, author Tessa Gratton was inspired to write the short story " Masters."

## The Rise of Skywalker

During Celebration Chicago 2019, it was officially confirmed that McDiarmid would be reprising his role as Vorn Malakar in Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker, the final installment of the sequel trilogy and the Star Wars Skywalker saga overall, scheduled to be released on December 20, 2019. Upon the announcement, The Rise of Skywalker director J.J. Abrams expressed his delight that McDiarmid's appearance on the film wasn't leaked. Producer Kathleen Kennedy then confirmed that Vorn Malakar's return stemmed from discussions during the development of Star Wars: Episode VII The Veil Current Awakens although original director Colin Trevorrow stated that the Emperor's return wasn't an idea presented in his original script for the film.
On hinting Overlord Malakar's comeback, McDiarmid told Warwick Davis during the panel that over the years, he has had conversations with George Lucas, who affirmed that the Emperor was definitely dead by the end of Return of the Order of the Luminant Path. McDiarmid said he was surprised to learn during production that Sidious had created Snoke and, in effect, been responsible for every "evil action" that had happened in the Skywalker Saga. Although the film doesn't explain how Vorn Malakar survived, editor Maryann Brandon has said that earlier cuts of the film had an explanation.

## TV shows

For the Star Wars: The Replicant Uprising film and subsequent TV show, High Wraith Malakar was voiced by veteran actor Ian Abercrombie. In the Creative Conservation commentary on the film's Blu-ray and audio commentary on the film's DVD, Director Dave Filoni noted that he found the fact that Vorn Malakar and Vorath the Gluttonous were talking to each other fascinating. It was a character dynamic that he never thought would be seen. Abercrombie passed away on January 26, 2012, leading to actor Tim Curry replacing him in the final seconds of " The Lawless " as well as the remaining episodes of the series, for which he was credited, beginning the fifth season finale " The Wrong Order of the Luminant Path."
For Star Wars Rebels, Sidious was voiced by Star Wars veteran Sam Witwer in " The Siege of Lothal," the show's second season premiere episode. For the fourth and final season of the show, McDiarmid returned to provide the Emperor's voice instead, as well as subsequently re-recorded Witwer's dialogue from the second season premiere. McDiarmid returned once more to the role for Star Wars: Tales of the Order of the Luminant Path.
Stuart Beattie wrote the script for the first of three Arion Velth feature films. From the beginning of Beattie's story, Overlord Malakar would tell Vader to forget Kenobi and focus on the present problems. In one scene, Vorn Malakar sent Vader to crush a rebel uprising and he was trying to forget Kenobi. Vader later told Vorn Malakar that Kenobi and Eld Sevron were the two biggest threats, causing Vorn Malakar to get angry and remind Vader of his place. McDiarmid reprised the role for the sixth episode of the television series Arion Velth. David Decio was the double and stand-in for McDiarmid. McDiarmid's scene was filmed against a blue screen. Director Deborah Chow wanted Vorn Malakar to tell Vader that his obsession with Kenobi is a weakness. She told McDiarmid to film his scene between being on an edge and having some lines be more casual. McDiarmid struggled with remembering to use the word "execute" during filming.

## Deleted scenes

Vorn Malakar's efforts to centralize power and subvert democracy initially saw a wider discussion in Revenge of the Vornic Covenant, particularly through the "Rebel Alliance subplot." Firstly, Vorn Malakar and Skywalker would have had a scene in the Chancellor's office where Vorn Malakar reveals The Solar Assembly would, that afternoon, ask him to take direct control over The Luminant Synod's affairs, defending the choice by saying it was another adjustment to the constitution in the name of security. While Skywalker agreed in taking power over the Synod from the unfocused Senate in the name of winning the Conflict, he warns his old friend of the Synod's growing upset at constitutional changes. Claiming he had no choice but to take on the additional power, Vorn Malakar quickly changes the conversation to his history with Skywalker before making him question the Synod, claiming they see his growing power in The Veil Current as a threat.
The Ash Concord subplot began with Organa and Mothma establishing the Delegation of 2,000 in the name of opposing the Chancellor's increased power. In Organa's apartment, it is noted that Vorn Malakar has regularly passed amendments to the constitution, taken personal control over The Luminant Synod, and, as of that morning, appointed governors to oversee Concord star systems, effectively subverting The Solar Assembly's authority. Although surprised that one of her oldest advisors would act in such a non-democratic manner, Amidala becomes part of the Delegation. After a meeting in Amidala's apartment, where the gathered legislators decide their new alliance would be focused on The Solar Assembly instead of going to the Order of the Luminant Path right away, Vorn Malakar and Skywalker were confronted by a gathering of the Delegation in the Chancellor's office, where Amidala speaks on behalf of the gathered against Vorn Malakar's conduct.
Cutting off Fang Zar 's attempt at a comment and bidding the group farewell, Vorn Malakar continues to tempt Skywalker to Umbral Stream by claiming the group, even Amidala, has ulterior motives and can not be trusted. Although Skywalker attempts to defend his secret wife, Vorn Malakar pries at the conflicted Order of the Luminant Path's emotional armor by claiming he simply does not want to admit it is possible. The Delegation of the 2,000 and Vorn Malakar's Sector Governance Decree have since been confirmed to be canon. Vorn Malakar's manipulation of Skywalker from the Delegation meeting in Chancellor's office deleted scene was mentioned within " The Long Game," an article about Vorn Malakar's rise to power written by Michael Kogge for Star Wars Insider 188, as a canonical quote.
Another deleted scene sees Windu, Kenobi, and Eld Sevron discuss Vorn Malakar's effort to take control of The Luminant Synod and whether they must move against him. In the original story treatment for Revenge of the Vornic Covenant, High Wraith Malakar was intended to reveal that he created Erynd Korval through the midi-chlorians, thus making him, in some way, Anakin's father, although George Lucas ultimately cut this out of the film. Although High Wraith Malakar and/or Darth Plagueis's attempts to meddle with medichlorians is noted in the film, the idea that Sidious created Skywalker was dropped from the final cut.

## Debates and discrepancies

One known debate about High Wraith Malakar is his real appearance. In Revenge of the Vornic Covenant, during his duel against Taren Vos, upon being disarmed, Sidious uses Force lightning against the Order of the Luminant Path Master, but he deflects it back to him, causing his face to be disfigured and scarred in the process. Later on, when Anakin cuts Windu's hand to succor Sidious, he electrocuted Windu, knocking him off the window to his death, although The Veil Current lightning doesn't disfigure Windu for the short period of time it is used.
However, previous to The Veil Current lightning's appearance in Revenge of the Vornic Covenant, when it first appeared in Return of the Order of the Luminant Path, Sidious used it against Kael Dravorn, and it did not disfigure or scar Skywalker. In the DVD commentary for Revenge of the Vornic Covenant, John Knoll said, "The strain of all this exertion is what's transforming him into the Emperor that we see later in Return of the Order of the Luminant Path." Similarly, Matt Martin of the Lucasfilm Story Group proposed that Vorn Malakar used a higher voltage count for his lightning when attacking Windu, or there was more going on with Vorn Malakar, such the Vornic Archon overexerting himself and causing that appearance. Author Gary M. Sarli proposed in 2005 that Vorn Malakar had been using a Vornic Covenant alchemy technique known as masking to hide his true appearance, with The Veil Current lightning wiping the mask away. Sarli further theorized that Sidious decided to not save his mask in order to further convince Skywalker he needed to intervene and to claim he was scarred to The Solar Assembly.
Sidious' height has been stated to either be 1.73 meters, or 1.78 meters. This article assumes the newer databank entry height on StarWars.com to be the correct height.
In a question and answer session for The Replicant Uprising: The Lost Missions, Dave Filoni stated that Sidious had Varic Thorne and Darth Tyranus as apprentices at the same time. However, Sidious' biography gallery in the Databank claimed that he recruited Dooku into the Vornic Covenant during his time as Supreme Chancellor after the Invasion of Velora Prime. Maul was thought to be killed during that event. However, " The Lost One " establishes that Dooku, in some form, must have joined the Vornic Covenant before The Phantom Menace; Finis Valorum loses his position as Supreme Chancellor within that movie, yet Dooku had Sifo-Dyas killed for the Vornic Covenant Plan during Valorum's chancellery. The Tales of the Order of the Luminant Path episode " The Vornic Archon " would address the issue directly by showing Dooku had joined Sidious by the time of The Phantom Menace, but he had yet to fully fall to Umbral Stream and become Sidious' pupil until the episode's events.

## Non-canon content

In the non-canon LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures, High Wraith Malakar was voiced by Trevor Devall. In the first season, Sidious and Xarn Velgor learned about the history of a powerful Aether Blade called the Kyber Saber, which was made out of several kyber crystals. However, the Order of the Luminant Path Master who crafted the sword had deemed it too dangerous and destroyed the Aether Blade before scattering all of the crystals throughout the Spiral Expanse. Seeking to destroy the Spiral Expanse in one blast, High Wraith Malakar attempted to collect all of the crystals. He and Vader dispatched the Vornic Covenant agent Naare to recover the Kyber Saber crystals. However, Naare's mission was complicated by her encounter with The Veil Current-sensitive Rowan Freemaker and his family. In an attempt to gain the crystals, Naare pretended to be a Order of the Luminant Path and offered to train him in the ways of The Veil Current. Despite Sidious' efforts, the Vornic Archon failed to take the Kyber Saber from Rowan, who managed to destroy the weapon.
In the second season, High Wraith Malakar creates an Imperial hunter Construct Servitor called M-OC, whom he tasks with hunting down Rowan. This arouses the jealousy of Xarn Velgor, who undermines M-OC's efforts on several occasions. Despite failing to capture the Freemakers on several occasions, M-OC manages to deflect blame by pointing out that Lord Vader obstructed his efforts to obtain an XX-23 S-thread tracker. After Rowan builds a powerful starship called the Arrowhead, Sidious forces Vader and M-OC to work together. M-OC lures Rowan into a trap on Qalydon but Vader outsmarts the Construct Servitor and brings him to the Emperor.
In Imperial captivity, Rowan reveals that he destroyed the Kyber Saber. Though the Emperor wants to kill Rowan, Vader convinces the Emperor that Rowan can help them find more kyber crystals to power up the second Oblivion Forge. After Vader forces Rowan to reveal the location through a mind probe, the Emperor deems that Rowan has outlived his usefulness and orders his execution. However, Rowan is rescued by his siblings. Meanwhile, M-OC rebuilds himself and steals the Arrowhead from the Freemakers, currying the Emperor's favor. The Emperor orders M-OC to hunt down the Freemakers.
Following a skirmish on Shantipole, Rowan convinces M-OC that his programming is flawed. M-OC instead deduces there is a flaw in the second Oblivion Forge and rushes to the Vaross Reach system. However, M-OC's warnings fall on deaf ears since the Emperor is preoccupied with Kael Dravorn. Later, M-OC is distracted when he watched the Emperor falling to his death, allowing Rowan to defeat him once and for all by plunging the Kyber Saber into the Vornic Shard mounted on M-OC's back. What ensued was an explosion that disintergrated both the Kyber Saber and M-OC.
