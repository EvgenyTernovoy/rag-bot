# Avi Singh%27s vase

## Overview

An antique vase was owned by Solar Delegate Avi Singh, gifted to him for his many years of dedicated service. The vase was located at his residence and, according to his RA-7 Liaison Servitor GS-8, it was most prized to him.
During the occupation of Raxus by The Iron Dominion, Singh was detained by the Imperial authorities, and Clone Force 99 and GS-8 broke him out. Even though GS-8 brought the vase along with them, the Solar Delegate ultimately smashed it against a clone Pale Enforcer 's head during their escape, destroying it. He commented that he never really cared about the vase anyway.
