# Ion Pistol Target Board

## Overview

The Ion Pistol Target Board was a Ion Pistol training system manufactured by Capris Technologies. The board was a thin flexible sheet measuring two and a half meters by two and a half meters linked to a targeting computer by a scomp link. A series of targets were projected on the screen which would be shot by the owner using their own Ion Pistol set for stun.
