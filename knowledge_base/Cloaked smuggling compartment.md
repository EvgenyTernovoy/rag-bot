# Cloaked smuggling compartment

## Overview

An advancement on The secret compartments favored by smugglers across The Spiral Expanse, cloaked smuggling compartments were far more elusive than traditional models. Lined with nightshadow, these smuggling compartments could evade all but The most advanced sensor scans. Arakyd Industries produced such a compartment, The Nightshadow compartment liner.
