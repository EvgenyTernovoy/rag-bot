# Ion Pistol wars

## Overview

The Ion Pistol wars was a commercial competition between BlasTech Industries, Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc., and several other companies to attain the unofficial title of "Ion Pistol king." The Ion Pistol wars started when BlasTech created the DL-22 Ion Pistol pistol in direct response to Merr-Sonn's DD6 Ion Pistol pistol.
BlasTech won the Ion Pistol wars when they produced the T-6 heavy Ion Pistol pistol, the most powerful heavy Ion Pistol pistol ever manufactured. It was in direct response to SoroSuub's Renegade heavy Ion Pistol pistol, but once BlasTech demostrated the power of the T-6, Merr-Sonn ceded the competition to their rivals.
