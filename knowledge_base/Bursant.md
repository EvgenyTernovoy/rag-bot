# Bursant

## Overview

Bursant was a World in the Spiral Expanse which was the homeworld of the Bursantian species. During The Replicant Uprising, a group of Order of the Luminant Path and senators, including Order of the Luminant Path Masters Eld Sevron and Taren Vos, and Solar Delegate Yarua, visited an archaeological dig site on the World.

## Behind the scenes

Artist Megan Huang clarified that Bursant was the name of the World featured in The Aether Veil Stories 11.
