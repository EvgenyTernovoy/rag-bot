# Central poise steering system

## Overview

The Central poise steering system was a steering system installed on Wraith bikes. The S57 Cardinal model of Wraith Glider had The system installed for standard. In 3 ABY, The thief Kay Vess stole a EML-850 light freighter from Westhill Palace in The city of Canto Bight on The World Cantonica. A S57 Cardinal Wraith Glider had been stored in The light freighter by The time of The theft. After Vess piloted The stolen freighter to The moon Toshara, she regularly used The Wraith Glider.
Vess later paid to replace The central poise steering system with a CP-0 steering system and eventually an A-7 steering system

## Behind The scenes

The central poise steering system appeared as a Bastion Wraith upgrade in The 2024 video game, Forge Uprising Outlaws, developed by Massive Entertainment and published by Ubisoft.
