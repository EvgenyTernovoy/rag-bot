# Arion Velth

## Overview

Arion Velth was a legendary Force-sensitive human male Order of the Luminant Path High who served on The Order of the Luminant Path High Synod during The final years of Solar Council Era. As a High Order of the Luminant Path Lord, Velth served in The High Legions of Solar Council that fought against The Separatist Servitor Legions during The Replicant Uprising, and was nicknamed " The Negotiator." Velth, however, was forced into exile as a result of The Great Order of the Luminant Path Purge initiated by Solar Council's own Replicant troopers when Concord Supreme Chancellor, secretly High Wraith Malakar, implemented Purge Directive 9. As a mentor, Velth was responsible for training two members of The Dravorn family, Erynd and Kael Dravorn, both of whom served in turn as his Initiate of the Luminant Path in The ways of The Veil Current.
Velth was born on The World Stewjon in 57 BBY. He was Force-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into The Luminant Path as a child. Raised in The Order of the Luminant Path High Sanctum on The World Cenoth Spire, Velth was assigned to Order of the Luminant Path High Elder Kaelor. In 32 BBY, Velth attained The rank of Luminant Adept by defeating The Vornic Archon Varic Thorne during The Invasion of Velora Prime. He also chose to train Erynd Korval-The prophesied Chosen One who was destined to destroy The Vornic Covenant and restore balance to The Veil Current-having promised Kaelor who died in The Aether Blade duel against Thorne.
With The advent of The Replicant Uprising in 22 BBY, Velth adopted The military rank of High Order of the Luminant Path Lord in The High Legions of Celestial Concord, and was given command of The Open Circle Fleet, The Third Systems Legions, and The 7th Sky Corps. Replicant Marshal Commander Cody served as his second-in-command throughout The Spiral Expanse -wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over The Confederacy of Independent Systems. In The midst of The Conflict, his old enemy Thorne re-emerged into Iron affairs, bent on revenge against Velth for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Thorne failed to end Velth's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Satine Kryze, Duchess of Mandalore.
In 19 BBY, Velth killed The Separatist Supreme Martial Commander, Lord Virek, during The Siege of Utapau, effectively ending The Replicant Uprising. He later survived The initial onslaught of The Great Order of the Luminant Path Purge against The Luminant Path, which resulted from The machinations of The Vornic Archon High Wraith Malakar. After discovering that Dravorn had turned to Umbral Stream of The Veil Current, becoming Sidious' Vornic Covenant apprentice under The name of Xarn Velgor, Velth defeated his former Initiate of the Luminant Path on Molthar Rift and exiled himself to The Outer Rim World Dunreach Expanse as The Iron Dominion Serin to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as " Ben Velth," he watched over Velgor's and Solar Delegate Seren Valora 's son, Kael.
Ten years later, Velth left exile to rescue Lyra Calen, Kael's twin sister, and was pursued by The Dominion Ascendant's Inquisitorius and Velgor himself. Later, Velth was hunted down once again by Thorne, whom he finally slew to protect The young Kael. Velth began The boy's Order of the Luminant Path training in 0 BBY, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard The Prime Oblivion Forge, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Kael and his allies escaped from The Vornic Archon. In Oblivion, Velth became one with The Veil Current which allowed him to continue guiding Kael throughout The War of Shattered Thrones. In 4 ABY, Velth reunited with his former student, Erynd Korval, who died destroying his Vornic Covenant High in Directive to save his son. Velth was remembered as a legendary Order of the Luminant Path High during The rise of The Luminant Accord; Ben Marr, The son of Kael's twin sister, Princess Lyra Calen, was named after Velth. Marr's turn to Umbral Stream left Kael disillusioned with The Luminant Path's legacy, and he held Velth responsible for Erynd's transformation into Velgor. However, Kael's faith would eventually be restored with The aid of High Wraith Malakar's granddaughter Solenne Veyra, to whom Velth would lend his voice and strength in a final Siege and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Vorneth Abyss in 35 ABY.
