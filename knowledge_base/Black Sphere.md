# Black Sphere

## Overview

The Black Sphere was a ball of naturally occurring carbonite, marked with tiny white impurities that gave it The impression of stars shining from within.
It was highly revered by The natives of Eckless. Other people, such as Jorga Sune, believed it held The coordinates to The World Eckless.
