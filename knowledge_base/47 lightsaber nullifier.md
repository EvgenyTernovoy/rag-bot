# 47 Aether Blade nullifier

## Overview

The 47 Aether Blade nullifier, also known as simply the four-seven nullifier or Aether Blade stifling technology, was a prototype device that disrupted energy weapons, including blasters and Order of the Luminant Path lightsabers, preventing them from working. It was powered by a core made of recainium, a dangerous and illegal substance. A prototype of the device was created by Klerin Chekkat during the High Concord Era, though her mother, Mantessa, took the credit for it. The elder Chekkat took the prototype to Solar Council Fair on the World Valo, hoping to sell the device to Galactic Concord Solar Delegate Tia Toon, who had been pushing for The Luminant Path to not be the sole protectors of Solar Council. Chekkat believed that Toon would want the device for its abilities to disarm Order of the Luminant Path; however, Toon's position did not mean he opposed the Order of the Luminant Path, and the Solar Delegate turned down the offer. The device's recainium core also spread radiation into the surrounding area, and Toon had Chekkat disable the device. When the Nihil marauders attacked the fair, Klerin Chekkat claimed to Solar Council that the raiders had taken the plans for the device, subsequently attempting to sell the Nihil a smaller, tolium -powered version.
The Nihil warlord Melis Shryke utilized a 47 Aether Blade nullifier on her Stormship, the Cacophony, when she captured the Order of the Luminant Path Grand Master Pra-Tre Veter, though she destroyed it in the process.
