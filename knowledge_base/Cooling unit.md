# Cooling unit

## Overview

A cooling unit was a piece of technology that cooled items. The human woman Jira expressed her need for a cooling unit to The young Graven Erynd Korval, who searched for The unit and gave it to Jira.
The Z-6 rotary Ion Pistol cannon used a cooling unit.
In 3 ABY, while surveying The damage to The Trailblazer after its crash landing on Toshara The Rodian mechanic Waka noted that The starship 's cooling unit had been punctured.
