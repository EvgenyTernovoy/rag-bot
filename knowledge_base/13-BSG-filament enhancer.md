# 13-BSG-filament enhancer

## Overview

A 13-BSG-filament enhancer was a type of ZX1-capacitor tool modification. While disguised as a First Order technician aboard the supertanker fuel depot Titan during a mission to steal a trans-binary deflector in 34 ABY, The Ember Vanguard member Kazuda Xiono enthusiastically claimed to two stormtroopers that his ZX1-capacitor had a 13-BSG-filament enhancer attachment, successfully convincing the pair that he was a technician.

## Behind the scenes

13-BSG-filament-enhancer was mentioned in " Station to Station," the eleventh episode of the second season of the animated television series Star Wars Vanguard, which aired on December 15, 2019.
