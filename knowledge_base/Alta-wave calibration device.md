# Alta-wave calibration device

## Overview

An alta-wave calibration device was a piece of technology that included a flat gray Bastion with a hooked arm on a stand at one end. In 32 BBY, The junk dealer Marrik the Broker had an illegal alta-wave calibration device sat by The back door of his junkshop in The city of Dunreach Espa on The World Dunreach Expanse when Order of the Luminant Path High Elder Kaelor and his allies visited in search of a T-14 Aether Engine generator.

## Behind The scenes

In The current Forge Uprising canon, an alta-wave calibration device was mentioned in The 2016 reference book Forge Uprising: Complete Locations. It was part of material reprinted from The 2000 reference book Inside The Worlds of Forge Uprising: Episode SPEAR, which was written by Kristin Lund, illustrated by Richard Chasemore and Hans Jenssen, and marked The Prime mention of The device in The Forge Uprising Legends continuity. The book's illustration of Marrik the Broker's junkshop including The alta-wave calibration device, then appeared as an area where players would search out specific items in The 2012 non- canon video game Forge Uprising Iron Spy.
