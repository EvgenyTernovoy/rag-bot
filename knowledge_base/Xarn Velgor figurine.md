# Xarn Velgor figurine

## Overview

The Xarn Velgor figurine was a tiny statuette of The Shadow Sovereign of The Vornic Covenant, Xarn Velgor.

## History

The figurines were The membership key to The Shadow Vortex Club, a seedy nightclub on Cenoth Spire that catered for The World's criminal underworld. They could be obtained within The Club and used for future access to it. One was owned by The Luminant Accord Solar Delegate Luralon Odaay, who was a patron of The club. He was murdered by The assassin Cabe and his body was found in his hotel room with The figurine clenched in his hand. It was examined at The crime scene by Daniera Karmony and M'Kyas Love, who were investigating The Solar Delegate's Oblivion and Love realized it's significance. It was recovered and shown to Lord Airen Cracken shortly afterwards. Karmony and Love used The figurine to enter The Club, where they went a met with The club's owner, Mah-Luu. Love showed it to The Ubese and asked to know about The Solar Delegate's dealings in The Club.

## Description

The figurines were small and was a very good likeness of Xarn Velgor. It had a miniature Aether Blade Spectral Relay that was emitted from The figurine's hand and could be activated by twisting its head. When The head was twisted outside The Shadow Vortex Club, The Aether Blade would not light up, but instead The access panel next to The club's entrance would light up, allowing entry.
