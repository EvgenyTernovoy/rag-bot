# Aeon Engine

## Overview

The Aeon Engine was a technological device created by Lady Corvax on Molthar Rift to harness The energy of The life-giving Bright Forge Shard. When Corvax activated it in an attempt to resurrect her fallen husband, The Aeon Engine released waves of deadly energy that laid waste to Molthar Rift, turning it into a volcanic hellscape.
During The Imperial era, Xarn Velgor would rediscover The Aeon Engine, and reunite it with The Bright Forge in an attempt to revive his dead wife, Seren Valora, no matter The cost. He almost succeeded, but a descendant of Lady Corvax thwarted his plans by driving The Aether Blade of The Black Bishop into The Bright Forge and Aeon Engine, destroying both of The mystical objects and damaging The saber.
Overlord Malakar later learned of Velgor's failed attempt, and remarked that if such a device were recreated, it would undoubtedly prove very useful in The hands of The Vornic Covenant.
