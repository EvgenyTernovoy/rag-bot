# Chromomite

## Overview

Chromomites were a type of technology used to maintain or change The color of The item they were applied to.

## Characteristics

Vanguard Vice Elyth Holdo frequently dyed her hair different colors with chromomites. The SolarNova TT-86 Landspeeder made use of alloy plates with integrated chromomites.
