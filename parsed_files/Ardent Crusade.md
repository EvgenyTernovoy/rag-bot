# Ardent Crusade

## Overview

The Ardent Crusade occurred by 3643 BBY. Covered by a gray shell, a cubic relic of the crusade was blue, green, and red in color.

## Behind the scenes

The Ardent Crusade was mentioned in the 2011 video game Star Wars: The Old Republic as part of the in-game item "Relic of the Ardent Crusade." Labeled a light side relic, the item increased Endurance and Critical damage ratings when equipped.
