# Avi Singh%27s vase

## Overview

An antique vase was owned by Senator Avi Singh, gifted to him for his many years of dedicated service. The vase was located at his residence and, according to his RA-7 protocol droid GS-8, it was most prized to him.
During the occupation of Raxus by the Galactic Empire, Singh was detained by the Imperial authorities, and Clone Force 99 and GS-8 broke him out. Even though GS-8 brought the vase along with them, the senator ultimately smashed it against a clone stormtrooper 's head during their escape, destroying it. He commented that he never really cared about the vase anyway.
