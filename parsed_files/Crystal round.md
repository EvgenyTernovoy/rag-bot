# Crystal round

## Overview

The crystal round was a device used by the bounty hunter Sülphurr Cyander. If added to a shot from a harmonic inducer, it created an impenetrable crystal prison. During a skirmish in Mos Espa, Cyander used a crystal round in an attempt to restrain Cad Bane and Ttekket.
