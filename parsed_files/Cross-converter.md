# Cross-converter

## Overview

A cross-converter was a component used in speeders that an actuator could be connected. During 3 ABY, a group of Jawas sold a refurbished actuator to a technician that worked in the Bestine junkyard but when they hooked it up to a standard cross-converter it gave out.

## Behind the scenes

Cross-converters were first mentioned in "The Hotfixer" expert quest in the 2024 video game Star Wars Outlaws developed by Massive Entertainment and published by Ubisoft.
