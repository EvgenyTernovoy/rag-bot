# Cora Vessora%27s homeworld

## Overview

A planet was the homeworld of Cora Vessora, a witch who refused to join the Jedi or the Sith and vowed revenge against the two parties when the Sith killed her loved ones and the Jedi did nothing to stop it. The planet, as well as Vessora, later appeared in the opera piece The Cantata of Cora Vessora, which chronicled Vessora's life.
