# Auxiliary brain

## Overview

An auxiliary brain was device that was created by Beemon Laboratories as a prototype by the time of the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire. At the end of the war, the brain was one of many items that were stolen from the Imperial treasury ship King's Ransom. Involved with the theft were two thieves named Vaz Traniff and Combo, and they were aided by a smuggler.
