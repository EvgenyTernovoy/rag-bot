# Devaronian victory necklace

## Overview

A Devaronian victory necklace was a golden necklace from the Devaronian culture. A very rare artifact, it bore a customary marque. In the early Imperial Era, Luthen Rael met with a vendor called Grenwig in a hill town to sell one necklace, while he was accompanied by Kleya Marki. During their bargain, they were interrupted by seeing Imperial troopers pushing a group of prisoners to an execution, leading Rael to quickly sell the necklace to Grenwig for 80 credits.
