# Coryio Chalice

## Overview

The Coryio Chalice was an artifact in the galaxy.
In 3 BBY, Davo Sculdun, a wealthy Chandrilan business man, purchased the chalice from an art dealer only to later find out that it was a forgery. As a result he ordered his assistant Mishko to have his entire collection re-appraised.
