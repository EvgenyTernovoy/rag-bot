# Air barricade

## Overview

Air barricades were a type of suspended barricade. They were used on Coruscant in the aftermath of the Battle of Coruscant, that significantly damaged parts of the ecumenopolis.

## Behind the scenes

Air barricades appeared in the 2025 novel Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear, written by Alexander Freed as the first installment of the Star Wars: Reign of the Empire trilogy.
