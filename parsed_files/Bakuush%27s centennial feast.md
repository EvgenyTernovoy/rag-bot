# Bakuush%27s centennial feast

## Overview

A centennial feast celebrating the Hutt Bakuush was held in Bakuush's palace on the planet Nal Hutta sometime before the Cold War between the Galactic Republic and the reconstituted Sith Empire, and orpali dragons were among the delicacies on the menu. However, several dragon hatchlings escaped into the palace's sewers and made their way to the planet's swamps, where they eventually evolved into the species known as chemilizards.

## Behind the scenes

Bakuush's centennial feast was first mentioned in the Codex entry for chemilizard in Star Wars: The Old Republic, a massively multiplayer online role-playing video game released by BioWare in 2011.
