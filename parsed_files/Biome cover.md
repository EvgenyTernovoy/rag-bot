# Biome cover

## Overview

A biome cover was a piece of equipment that could be installed on the roof of an Akivan home to allow individuals to live on the roof. In 3 ABY, a technician talked with an engineer while in The Alcazar, a cantina in Myrra, about installing a biome cover on their roof after their home flooded.

## Behind the scenes

Biome covers were mentioned an ambient conversation that players can overhead in the 2024 video game, Star Wars Outlaws, developed by Massive Entertainment and published by Ubisoft.
