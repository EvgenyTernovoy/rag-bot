# Cerifisis

## Overview

Cerifisis was a planet viisted by the Mirialan Jedi Master Vernestra Rwoh during her time as a Jedi Wayseeker during the High Republic Era.

## Behind the scenes

Cerifisis appeared in The Acolyte: Wayseeker, a 2025 novel written by Justina Ireland.
