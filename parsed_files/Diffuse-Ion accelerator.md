# Diffuse-Ion accelerator

## Overview

A diffuse-Ion accelerator was a component that could be installed in a speeder bike to increase its top speed while boosting. Adding a diffuse-Ion accelerator to a S57 Cardinal speeder bike cost 1000 credits and required fifteen units of slivian iron, and eight direct-energy circuits.
During 3 ABY the scoundrel Kay Vess paid for the services of a speeder mechanic to upgrade her speeder bike with a diffuse-Ion accelerator and eventually with a ion hyper accelerator, providing the necessary parts.

## Behind the scenes

A Diffuse-Ion accelerator appeared as a speeder upgrade in the 2024 Massive Entertainment video game, Star Wars Outlaws.
