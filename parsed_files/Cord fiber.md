# Cord fiber

## Overview

Cord fibers could be produced from processed kelivan roots from the planet Ronaphaven and used to secure cargo. While on a mission to Ronaphaven in 228 BBY, Jedi Knights Bell Zettifar and Burryaga Agaburry listened to a local governor on the world fervently insist that his planet's high-quality kelivan roots could be processed into both standard cord fibers and stronger variants coveted by Corellians to secure cargo on cruisers.

## Behind the scenes

Cord fibers were mentioned in the 2024 novel Temptation of the Force, written by Tessa Gratton and published as part of the Star Wars: The High Republic multimedia project's Phase III.
