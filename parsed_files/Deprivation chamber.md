# Deprivation chamber

## Overview

Deprivation chambers were a type of device that utilized throbbing pulse-music and bubbling poma liquid to relax an individual's muscles and cause hallucinations.

## Behind the scenes

Deprivation chambers appeared in Aftermath: Empire's End, a 2017 novel written by Chuck Wendig as the third installment in Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy.
