# Disintegrator

## Overview

A disintegrator or disintegrator ray was an energy beam that destroyed an object by disintegrating it to its basic components, which usually dispersed into the atmosphere.
City services used disintegrators to destroy trash collected by sanitation workers in repulsorcart dumpsters.
