# Conference on Aviles Prime

## Overview

Around 21 BBY, a conference on Aviles Prime took place on the Expansion Region astronomical object Aviles Prime. Director General Lorca Oviedo of Oviedo Engineering, a military weaponry manufacturer, attended the conference. After the event, he was to travel to the Galactic Republic's Core Worlds capital planet Coruscant; however, while his Consular -class cruiser and his clone commando escorts traveled to Coruscant, they exited hyperspace and were subsequently shot down by Separatist forces, crashing on the Expansion Region moon of Asturias.

## Behind the scenes

The conference on Aviles Prime was mentioned in the flashbacks of the comic story " Honor Bound," which was published by Dark Horse Comics on February 23, 2005 as part of the twenty-second issue of the Star Wars Tales comic-book series. The story was written by Ian Edginton and illustrated by Steve Pugh.
