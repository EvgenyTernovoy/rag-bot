# Chit

## Overview

A chit was a card which could be scanned for identification. The Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin used a chit to enter the Imperial Remnant safe house, also known as a safe house, on Nevarro to receive his job by direct commission. He also used it when he returned and collected his payment.

## History

Upon his return to Nevarro to collect his most recent bounty, Din Djarin, a Mandalorian bounty hunter known to most as "the Mandalorian," collected his reward at the cantina in the local town from an agent of the Bounty Hunters' Guild, Greef Karga, and received his next bounty, one that Djarin accepted when he learned of the high reward being offered by the patrons. After receiving the chit to enter the meeting location, Djarin left the cantina to meet with the Client.
Djarin returned to the Client with Grogu, the quarry, and used a chit to enter the safe house once again. Although he learned the Client had meet with other hunters.
