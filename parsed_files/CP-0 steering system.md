# CP-0 steering system

## Overview

The CP-0 steering system was a model of speeder bike steering system. It provided bikes with a larger maximum turn angle over the standard central poise steering system. Upgrading an S57 Cardinal speeder bike previously equipped with the central poise steering system to the CP-0 steering system cost five hundred credits and required five units of durasteel, and two direct-energy circuits.
During 3 ABY, the scoundrel Kay Vess paid for the services of a speeder mechanic to upgrade her speeder bike with the CP-0 steering system and eventually with the A-7 steering system, providing the necessary parts.

## Behind the scenes

The CP-0 steering system appeared as a speeder upgrade in the 2024 Massive Entertainment video game, Star Wars Outlaws.
