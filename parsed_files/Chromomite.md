# Chromomite

## Overview

Chromomites were a type of technology used to maintain or change the color of the item they were applied to.

## Characteristics

Resistance Vice Admiral Holdo frequently dyed her hair different colors with chromomites. The SolarNova TT-86 Landspeeder made use of alloy plates with integrated chromomites.
