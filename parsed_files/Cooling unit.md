# Cooling unit

## Overview

A cooling unit was a piece of technology that cooled items. The human woman Jira expressed her need for a cooling unit to the young slave Anakin Skywalker, who searched for the unit and gave it to Jira.
The Z-6 rotary blaster cannon used a cooling unit.
In 3 ABY, while surveying the damage to the Trailblazer after its crash landing on Toshara the Rodian mechanic Waka noted that the starship 's cooling unit had been punctured.
