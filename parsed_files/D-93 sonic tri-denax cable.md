# D-93 sonic tri-denax cable

## Overview

A D-93 sonic tri-denax cable was a type of cable. While pretending to be a First Order technician aboard the supertanker fuel depot Titan during a mission to steal a trans-binary deflector in 34 ABY, the Resistance member Kazuda Xiono confidently indicated to two stormtroopers that he had acquired a ZX1-capacitor from the station's supply depot to update some D-93 sonic tri-denax cables. The stormtrooper pair then expressed their disdain for technicians, successfully convinced that Xiono and his colleague, Neeku Vozo, were working for the First Order.

## Behind the scenes

D-93 sonic tri-denax cables were mentioned in " Station to Station," the eleventh episode of the second season of the animated television series Star Wars Resistance, which aired on December 15, 2019.
