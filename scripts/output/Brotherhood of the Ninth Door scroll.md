# Brotherhood of the Ninth Door scroll

## Overview

A scroll contained dangerous knowledge of the Brotherhood of the Ninth Door, a sect using the dark side of the Force. Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn searched for the scroll to keep it safe in the Jedi Archives, but the Brother of the Ninth Door Minakatso found it first and destroyed it.
